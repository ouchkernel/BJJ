# Volume 3 — Arumi Ashi Garami, Breaking to a Hip & The Four-Step Heel Hook — Quick Reference

## Build Arumi ashi garami

1. Outside leg governs distance — toes out, foot hooks on top of their hip.
2. **Back-heel** (pull heel toward your own hamstring). Never push into the ribcage — that pushes them out.
3. Shoelace placement, pick one:
   - **Straight buttock** (foot to their opposite buttock) → closed, reinforced wedge. Preferred for a solid hold.
   - **Cross buttock** (foot to their same-side buttock) → open wedge. Needed for breakdowns / reverse-X entries.
4. Close knee to ankle, weight over the shin.

## Test the connection (no hands)

- [ ] Dragging test — partner walks back on hands/butt, your ashi garami stays connected, knee inside knee line.
- [ ] Rolling test — partner rolls, you stay attached via back-heel/pull (never push).

## Opponent crosses ankles to block entry

1. Switch pulling grip → **pushing** grip.
2. Drive their hips directly over your head.
3. Take the 90° angle, enter with knee inside your knee line.

## Skill 3 — break a standing opponent down to a hip (bottom position only)

Standing opponent with good posture (head on centerline, back-stepping) won't fall from head pressure. Rule: push **across** their hips, never into their base of support.

**Method 1 — single leg:** straight buttock, knee-to-ankle touch, force up then out → foot trips the back-step leg → they fall.

**Method 2 — double leg (preferred):** if they split your legs (hand on knee, hand on ankle) → switch to cross buttock, keep the ankle grip, post your other foot, push their hands to the mat → grab the now-in-range second foot → re-throw the ashi garami gripping the leg.

**Method 3 — X-guard → reverse X-guard:** over-wrap/scoot grip → drop to X-guard (foot behind knee, foot in front of hip) → knee behind hamstring tilts their hands to the mat → switch to reverse X-guard (cross-buttock foot + foot halfway down quad) → lift knees to chest first, then curve **out and around** (the "power curve," not a straight lift) → walk the figure-four → push knee **across** their hips (not into the base) to sit them to a hip.

## The Four-Step Sequence

| Step | Goal | Position |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Entry & connection | Arumi ashi garami |
| 2 | Heel exposure | Inside ashi garami |
| 3 | Negation | Far-hip ashi garami (sankaku) |
| 4 | Breaking | Outside ashi garami |

**Step 2:** inside foot crosses centerline → heel exposed → wrist around heel, lock hands.

**Step 3:** lock a sankaku over the opponent's far hip. Closes all 3 escapes:
- [ ] Can't separate your legs — triangle is locked
- [ ] Can't out-rotate you — point your knee at the floor, you match their speed
- [ ] Can't create distance — reinforced wedge holds

**Step 4 — THE key principle:** your ashi garami force must **oppose** your break force. Your knees show the direction of ashi garami force. Right-leg heel hook breaks right-to-left → knees point the **opposite** way (outside).

Why outside ashi garami is the best breaking position: feet lock/reinforce each other, knees point outward (opposite the heel hook) → even a minor heel lift creates real pressure. Finish: both legs back-heel (pull, never push), top knee comes over the top.

## Don't

- [ ] Push into ribcage/quad instead of back-heeling
- [ ] Use cross buttock when you want a solid hold
- [ ] Try to break a standing opponent before getting them to a hip
- [ ] Push into the base of support during a breakdown
- [ ] Control only one leg during a breakdown (free leg saves their base)
- [ ] Lift straight out of reverse X-guard instead of the power curve
- [ ] **Cardinal error:** run the ashi garami and the heel hook in the same direction
- [ ] Break from an unreinforced (unlocked) wedge
- [ ] Push instead of pull/back-heel during the break

## Decision Points

- Opponent crosses ankles to deny entry → pushing grip, hips over your head, 90° angle.
- Need a rock-solid connection → straight buttock. Need a breakdown/reverse-X entry → cross buttock.
- Standing opponent won't go down → stop chasing the head, interfere with the back-step leg (single-leg or double-leg method).
- Opponent splits your ashi garami → X-guard → reverse X-guard, or the double-leg breakdown.
- Time to break → get to outside ashi garami, knees opposite the heel-hook direction, then a minor heel lift finishes.

## Terminology

- **Arumi ashi garami** — the "enter" ashi garami; primary entry/connection.
- **Straight buttock / cross buttock** — closed reinforced wedge vs. open wedge.
- **Back heel** — pull heel to your own hamstring to draw their quad in.
- **Inside ashi garami** — foot across centerline, exposes the heel (Step 2).
- **Far-hip ashi garami** — sankaku over the far hip, the negation position (Step 3).
- **Outside ashi garami** — both feet outside their leg, the reinforced breaking wedge (Step 4).
- **Single-leg X / X-guard / reverse X-guard** — breakdown positions.
- **Power curve** — lifting out and around, not straight, to bring hips over your head.
- **Opposing forces principle** — ashi garami force must oppose break force.
