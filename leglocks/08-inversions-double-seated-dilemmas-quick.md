# Volume 8 — Inversions, the Double-Seated Position, Dilemmas & Head Position — Quick Reference

## 1. Inversion entries (second entry family, alongside elevation)

From reverse De La Riva (opponent in split squat, your shoelaces on his quad):
1. Hand to his ankle, back of wrist through in front of the ankle/shoelaces.
2. Extend both legs — loads his weight onto the far leg, moves him off you.
3. Begin the inversion: feet close together, push his weight forward.
4. Switch legs around the corner so your knee is inside his hip, second leg through, drop him into control.
5. Far leg: switch around the corner. Near leg: post the far leg and switch under, lock a sankaku.
6. If he drops a knee to the floor: over-rotate all the way around into reverse X-guard, catch the knee, lift, drop.

## 2. Knee shield half guard entry

- No-gi: keep the knee **shallow, near-hip only**. Never bring it across to the far hip — you get squashed and passed.
- Pair with a cross frame (hand on shoulder, forearm/elbow on near shoulder).
- Use a scissor-sweep-style leg action to enter the legs.

## 3. Deep half guard — far-leg route

1. Transfer your arm from the near leg to the far leg.
2. Hook your foot under his ankle, elevate his leg out and over (not up).
3. Whip up behind him.
4. Build wedges both sides of your lower leg (knee behind his ankle, elbow/wrist in front of his calf) + a wedge over the hip so he can't yank the leg free.
5. Drop to an elbow, pass your foot through and around, roll into inside heel hook.

**Back-or-leg dilemma:** as your head comes out the back door, you threaten the back.
- He doesn't move his upper body → take the back.
- He does move it → take the leg.
He can't defend both.

## 4. Double-seated position

**Holding/pommeling:**
- [ ] Don't grip his foot with your thumb — too weak, he kicks free.
- [ ] Hug his ankle to your thigh with an open hand, hold with your extending leg's strength.
- [ ] Strip his grip, pommel your foot in — always to **cross buttock** (right foot → his right buttock). Straight buttock leaves a gap he drives a truck through.

**Distance ≠ inside position — you need both:**
1. Two-on-one grip.
2. Use your feet to pull your hips up to his knee line (you start outside it).
3. Enter ashi garami with his knee inside your knee line, expose the heel, roll through.

**Losing the pommel battle → change levels:**
1. Pull your inside foot back underneath your own leg (one forward, one back) — nothing left for him to pommel against.
2. Hip heist / pop to your knee as he hand-fights forward.
3. Catch the leg — direct grip (stronger kani basami control, no secondary leg) or under your own knee with a hand assist.
4. Only one foot inside is ever needed for the entry.

## 5. Finishing: head position is the #1 thing

1. Shift hips so your knees point at his belly button/chest, not out (pointing out helps *his* escape).
2. Extend your foot into the hamstring bar to slow his turn further.
3. Catching the secondary leg stops the turnout — but creates the release problem (must let go to finish).
4. **The actual key detail: drive your head inside his knee.** As he turns, your head becomes a floor post that stops his roll.
5. When rotation stops, catch up to the heel, lock it, roll through.

**Priority order:** head position (inside the knee → floor post) **>** secondary-leg control **>** everything else.

## Don't

- [ ] Rely only on elevation entries — ignore inversion (e.g. from reverse De La Riva).
- [ ] Bring the knee shield across to the far hip.
- [ ] Grip the foot with your thumb in double-seated — hug the ankle to the thigh instead.
- [ ] Pommel to straight buttock in double-seated.
- [ ] Assume inside position alone is enough — you still need to breach the knee line for distance.
- [ ] Stay stuck seated losing the pommel war — change levels instead.
- [ ] Let your knees point out in the finish.
- [ ] Chase secondary-leg control before head position — head position is the critical detail.

## Quick decision table

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| Opponent passing your reverse De La Riva | Inversion entry (load far leg, invert, switch or over-rotate to reverse X-guard) |
| Half guard, opponent seeking chest-to-chest | Near-hip knee shield + cross frame → scissor entry |
| Head out the back door from deep half | Back-or-leg dilemma |
| Double-seated, losing the pommel | Change levels — foot under own leg, hip heist |
| Heel hook on, opponent turning out | Head inside his knee → post it → stop the roll → take the heel |
