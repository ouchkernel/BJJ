# Volume 7 — Entering the Legs: Standing, Top, Bottom & Seated — Quick Reference

## Core idea

Bottom position is the best place to hunt legs. Confirm before drilling anything else:
- You: 2 legs + 2 arms working. Opponent: legs are weight-bearing, only 2 arms free. **4-on-2.**
- Your legs > their arms in strength.
- You're stable on your back/butt. They're only as stable as their base. **Kuzushi runs one way — toward you.**

After any entry, read the opponent's **head** to pick the subsystem:
- Head moves **away** → straight ashi garami
- Head comes **toward you** → cross ashi garami

## 1. Elevation game (bottom position, opponent kneeling) — the main entry

1. Check your own head — if it drops below your front deltoid, elevation is dead. Keep it up.
2. Cocked feet: actively hook shoelaces inside his knees. Never passive/dead feet.
3. Thumb posts inside his biceps, head to his clavicles.
4. Pull yourself forward with the **blades of your feet** (toes out), not the soles, no hands.
5. Pommel hands through, elevate, take the angle, thread second foot around.
6. Bring his hips/toes over your head, drop him into ashi garami.

**3 keys to elevation:** (1) his hips over your head, (2) an angle, (3) your knee inside his hip/head pocket.

## 2. Standing opponent (you seated)

1. Foot across his lead foot to claim inside space as he closes in.
2. Pull yourself to him with the blades of both feet.
3. Grip the lead leg **wrist-deep, palm down** (not finger-deep, palm out).
4. Pull your grip hand-to-hand to force him to step — catch the foot mid-step.
5. Pick your target:
   - **Near leg:** load it with his step, chop the inside, fire into ashi garami.
   - **Far leg:** outside grip, foot behind his far foot, shoot beyond the far leg (not through your centerline) — controls both legs.
6. Often flows through shin-to-shin guard → X-guard.

## 3. Stalled takedown → "ashi sit"

- **Failed double-leg:** double-tricep grip, level change, feet together drive in. On his sprawl, put your **inside** leg up (not outside), elbow-deep around the knee, head slides hip→knee (head outside), into ashi garami.
- **Single-leg + sprawl:** post a hand on the mat first. Foot inside the shoelaces, knee-slide toward his opposite leg (head comes out), draw him into ashi garami.
- **Knee-pull single:** inside control, pull the knee to bring him forward, knee around the corner into the legs.

## 4. Standing clinch entries

- **Front headlock:** underhook → head dominant inside → front headlock. As he posts up: knee down, head behind, reach across to pick the far ankle, second knee down, hand to his knee, foot up, into the legs.
- **Rear body lock:** don't stay bolt-upright/square. Control his hands, turn hip out (side-on), lower level, step a foot to inside position, free hand to his knee, post hand, head outside his leg, pull him around the corner into ashi garami.

## 5. Half guard / deep half guard

- **Half guard:** scoot hips **in** under him (opposite of normal half guard) — back-heel his calf + butterfly hook stops the kick-out. Plant foot, scoop your CoG under his, double-thumb to clear his head, knee inside, grip the off-mat knee, transition reverse X-guard, lift and drop.
- **Deep half guard:** arm around his waist + scoop grip → elbow-deep inside the leg → swing 180° to face his direction → pommel foot under his calf → lift/feed leg to near-X-guard → cross-arm frame → reverse X-guard → hips over your head (angle + knee in hip pocket) → lock triangle → push across the hip line to drop him.

## Don't

- [ ] Treat bottom position as inferior — it's a real structural advantage.
- [ ] Let your head drop below your front delt while elevating.
- [ ] Use passive/dead feet inside the knees.
- [ ] Post hands to move forward — pull with the blades of your feet instead.
- [ ] Grip the standing leg finger-deep / palm-out.
- [ ] Skip protecting inside space when a standing opponent closes.
- [ ] Shoot through your own centerline on the far-leg attack — go beyond the far leg.
- [ ] Put the outside leg up on a stalled takedown (that's a takedown habit, not an ashi-sit habit).
- [ ] End an entry with your head inside the leg or falling between the legs.

## Quick decision table

| Situation | Entry |
|---|---|
| Opponent kneeling | Elevation game |
| Opponent standing over you | Protect inside space → wrist-deep palm-down grip → near or far leg |
| Takedown stalls | Ashi sit, inside leg up |
| Front headlock / rear body lock | Convert directly into a leg entry |
| In half guard | Scoot hips in → reverse X-guard |
| In deep half | Swing 180° → reverse X-guard |
| After any entry | Read the head: away = straight, toward you = cross |
