# Opponent Inside Spin — Quick Reference

## What's happening

Opponent hand on his own leg → inverts onto shoulders → spins through. Goal isn't the spin itself — it's ending up with your hips facing UP and his facing DOWN (opposing directions). That's what breaks your grip, even a tight one.

## Why it beats you if you do nothing

Opposing hip direction = can't hold on. Confirmed: even a "death grip" gets spun/limped out under those conditions.

## The fix

1. See the inside turn starting.
2. Throw your **outside leg over** his leg — immediately, as it starts.
3. Follow his spin. Don't fight it.
4. His turning naturally realigns your hips with his (both ending up facing down).
5. More rotations = stronger lock, not a problem. Ride it out (1–3 spins is normal).
6. Finish: belly-down preferred. Flat-back/inside/outside also fine.
7. Can't get perfect half-foot after realigning? Belly-down is strong enough on its own — just finish from there.

## One-line rule

Opponent inverts and spins → throw outside leg over → follow, don't resist → hips realign → finish belly-down.
