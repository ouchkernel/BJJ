# Counter To Leg Lace (Part 1) — Quick Reference

## The situation

Opponent has your legs bound in a leg lace, controlling you, threatening heel hooks. You still have a counter.

## Step 1: check the bottom foot

- [ ] Foot exposed / lazy control → knee out, catch it, easy
- [ ] Foot hidden → dig for it (below)

## Digging it out

1. Foot to inside position, expose it.
2. Grab the knee.
3. Punch through.
4. Ankle bone visible = you've got half-foot.

Bonus: if he grabs at you defensively, his own leg lace ties up his other arm — usually not enough hands to stop you.

## Finish

1. Draw the foot out.
2. Two hands on it (reinforce with second hand on knee).
3. Full figure-four.
4. Finish.

## If he's smart and bails

He'll realize: he has the entanglement on YOU, not the other way around — so he can just let go and escape.

That's fine. His escape movement = opening. Follow him (reposition leg over/under as needed) and flow straight into your standard offensive ankle locks.

## Bottom line

Leg lace ≠ trapped. Naive opponent → finish directly. Smart opponent bails → his bail is your opening.
