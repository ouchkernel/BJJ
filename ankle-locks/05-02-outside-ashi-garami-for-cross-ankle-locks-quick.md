# Outside Ashi Garami for Cross Ankle Locks — Quick Reference

## Key difference from straight ankle locks

- Straight ankle lock limp = knee turns **outward**.
- Cross ankle lock limp = knee turns **inward**.
- Different escape direction → different leg entanglement needed.

## Build outside Ashi Garami — in order

1. Pass your ankle over their **hip** (not up near the armpit).
2. Knees stay pinched/engaged — don't split them open.
3. **Cross your own ankles**, pull feet back toward you. Don't leave a foot exposed — that's a leg lock risk for you.
4. Draw opponent in, pass the foot over the top, protect it immediately.
5. Inside leg goes under outside leg — inside leg pushes into their knee (dominant).
6. Use your back's surface area to pin them.
7. Drop your elbow — pressure starts even before you finish rotating.

## Sequencing — pick based on opponent

| Opponent skill | Do this order |
|---|---|
| Lower | Half-foot first (hand post) → pass foot into Ashi Garami → close and finish |
| Higher / resisting | Pass foot into Ashi Garami first → hip-expose the ankle → close and finish |

## Watch out

- Split knees = opponent can still turn the knee in.
- Uncrossed/exposed foot near their far hip = they can attack your leg back.
- Getting turned to your own inside hip here = hard to finish (fix is next lesson).

## Default

Outside Ashi Garami is your go-to ~90% of the time for binding legs in cross ankle locks.
