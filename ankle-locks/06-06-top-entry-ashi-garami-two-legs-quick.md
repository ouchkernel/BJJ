# Top Entry To Ashi Garami, Controlling 2 Legs — Quick Reference

## Golden rule

Entering from top position: **control both legs**, not just the one you attack. A hand on the secondary ankle/wrist is usually enough. Doesn't need to happen at the exact moment of entry — grab it as he starts to defend.

## Entry 1 — one-handed, ankle+knee control

1. Get between his knees.
2. Cup his knee.
3. Step your leg up to his hip line.
4. Now controlling ankle + knee, same side.
5. Pass the foot up, sit back.
6. You control both legs — grip on one ankle, both your legs wrapped around the other.
7. Finish: one-handed grip, all the way inside, hip down.

## Entry 2 — two-handed, triangle finish

1. Enter between his knees, hug with the knee.
2. Second foot touches just inside him (control point).
3. He tries to pull the other leg up → runs into your hand.
4. Pass second foot over the top.
5. He moves away → full triangle around his leg, locked at the ankle.
6. Finish: tap straight down. Strong finish.

## From a supine opponent (Entry 2 variant)

1. Move side to side into position.
2. Step up, secure grips.
3. Foot comes up, catches.
4. He moves away → foot inside, second triangle locks over.
5. One-handed grip, spread down, tap.

## Big picture

This closes the entry map: bottom vs. two knees / one knee / standing, plus top position with two-leg control, plus foot-passing to flip straight ↔ cross ankle lock.
