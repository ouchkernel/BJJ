# Ankle Lock From A Z-Lock — Quick Reference

## Setup

1. Basic Ashi Garami, feet passed over/inside.
2. Grab their second leg, bring your feet in.
3. **Hide your own foot behind** — never on top, or it becomes their target.

## Skip half-foot here — use this grip instead

1. **Palm to palm** grip (not figure-four).
2. Support-hand wrist bent 90°.
3. Elbow tight to your body — no space.
4. Roll **backward over their toes** (swallow them) instead of going forward.
5. Elbow down, pinch knees.

## Target

Anterior tibialis tendon — toes pulled back toward their own knee.

## Tighten it

Rotate your body toward the captured foot. Don't just fall straight back.

## Why they can't escape

Both legs tied up = can't yank free. Unlike other double-trouble grips, **they can't limp out of this one either.**

## Benchmark

Ashi Garami → stack your foot behind theirs → palm-to-palm, tight elbow, roll over the toes → rotate into the captured foot → finish.
