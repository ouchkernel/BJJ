# Elbow-to-Centerline Cue — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-05-07- A Simple Mechanical Cue That Will Add Greatly To All Your Ankle Locks.mp4`

## The one cue (works for straight AND cross ankle locks)

**Elbow toward your centerline = good. Elbow forward/away from centerline = bad.**

- Worst: elbow out and forward.
- Best: elbow pulled back, then driven toward centerline.

## Why it matters

- Elbow back alone tightens the lock but hand stays low.
- Elbow toward centerline → hand actually **rises**. That's the part that matters.
- Low hand vs. their ankle = easy limp-out.
- High hand vs. their ankle = hard limp-out.

## What it fixes, automatically

- [ ] Harder for opponent to kick through
- [ ] Harder for opponent to limp out
- Both improve just from elbow position — even before you've hit full half-foot.

## Do this every single time

1. Get elbow-deep around the ankle.
2. Drive elbow back and toward your centerline.
3. Check: is your hand now higher than their ankle? If not, elbow isn't far enough toward centerline yet.

## Why this one's worth memorizing

Simple enough to check for under full sparring/competition pressure. One adjustment, defends two escapes at once.
