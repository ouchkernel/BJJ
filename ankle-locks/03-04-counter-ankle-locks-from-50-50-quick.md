# Counter Ankle Locks From 50-50 — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-03-04- Counter Ankle Locks From 50-50.mp4`

## Why this works

Every Ashi Garami exposes some of the attacker's own leg. In 50-50, that's your opening.

## Defend first

- Don't point toes into opponent's armpit.
- Close and cross your legs — forces them to untangle you before they can attack.

## Counter sequence

1. Catch their **top heel** (the leg thrown over the top) — go under the 50-50 leg, over the top leg. No need to separate ankles like a real heel hook.
2. Hand on their knee → work to half-foot.
3. Close grip.
4. As they try to escape, cross your ankles over the top.

## You win both ways

| Opponent does this | You get |
|---|---|
| Fights the ankle lock, hand-fights | Their hands can't reach yours (your body blocks) → transition to heel hook, belly down |
| Stays and eats the pressure | Finish the ankle lock directly |

## Finishing mechanics

Elbow arches toward opponent, feet/knees drive away. **Knees drifting toward you = no power.**

## Note

Grip looks weak (not much to grab) — half-foot position makes it tight anyway, even one-handed.

## Benchmark

Get the top-heel catch → half-foot → force the dilemma → finish whichever way they choose.
