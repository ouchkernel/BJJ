# Achilles Grip → Ankle Lock Grip — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-01-03- Transition From Achilles Grip To Ankle Lock Grip.mp4`

## The problem

Achilles grip only pulls. Can't push. Can't finish a resisting leg from there.

## Do this — 3 things at once

1. **Push with your hand** — open hand or figure-four palm on their leg. Don't lock a full finishing grip yet, you need a free hand to push.
2. **Push with your leg** — foot/shoelace into their leg, butterfly-style.
3. **Hollow your body** — scoot hips back.

All three together = foot moves from behind you (useless) to half-foot (breaking position).

## Drill sequence

1. Start Achilles grip (figure-four or one-handed).
2. Check: opponent's foot is behind your elbow → bad position, control only.
3. Push hand + push leg + hollow body, all at once.
4. Foot lands in half-foot position.
5. Lock figure-four.
6. Squeeze knees, tap partner using hands only.

## Don't

Lock a finishing grip (elbow-to-elbow) before you've pushed to half-foot — you lose your pushing hand.

## Benchmark

Do this transition fast, against a resisting partner, and still get the hands-only tap.
