# Decision Tree Start: Half Foot → Outside Hip — Quick Reference

## Your default opening move

1. Opponent seated in front of you.
2. Get Achilles/holding grip.
3. Hollow body, convert to **half foot**.
4. Fall to the **outside** (not inside — easier, holds half foot better).
5. Pass outside foot over their leg.
6. Foot under their hamstring, inside leg passes over top.
7. Fall to outside hip + outside shoulder → finish.

## Why outside > inside

Easier to execute. Easier to keep half foot through the finish. Works on a wide range of opponents — this is your go-to, not a special case.
