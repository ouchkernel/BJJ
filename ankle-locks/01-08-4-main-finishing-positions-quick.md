# 4 Main Finishing Positions — Quick Reference

## 2 kinds of resistance you'll face

- **Linear:** boot on (pushed through) / limp leg (pulled out).
- **Rotational:** opponent spins/inverts. If he doesn't secure an inside foot position first, just follow the spin — lock holds.

## Master these 4 finishes — cover all 360°

1. **Outside hip** — knee points out, standard finish.
2. **Inside hip** — knee points in, pass leg over their legs, finish inside.
3. **Belly down** — any rotation direction, doesn't need perfect half-foot.
4. **Flat back** — opponent posts to a knee, foot on their calf, rotate onto your back, finish.

Goal: equally comfortable in all 4. Opponent can rotate into any of them at any time.

## The grip tradeoff (important, non-obvious)

Foot only partway through = super robust grip, weak breaking power.
Foot all the way through = strong breaking power, fragile grip (they can rip it out).

**You can't max both. Find the middle ground.**

## How to calibrate — do this with a partner

1. Take foot partway through.
2. Partner tries to violently push foot through → should fail.
3. Partner tries to violently limp leg out / twist knee → should fail.
4. Pinch knees around theirs for extra security while testing.
5. Adjust depth until both tests fail for them. That's your spot.

## Don't

Assume one grip depth works everywhere — recalibrate against live resistance, not a fixed formula.
