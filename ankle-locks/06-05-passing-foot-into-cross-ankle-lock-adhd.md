# Passing A Foot Into A Cross Ankle Lock — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-06-05- Passing A Foot Into A Cross Ankle Lock.mp4`

## What this is

Converting a straight Ashi Garami entry into a cross ankle lock by passing the foot across your body. (Skip this if your entry already gives you an inside heel hook — that already sets up cross ankle lock.)

## Get him down first

1. ID forward/back leg.
2. Hand on back-leg side, two-on-one wrist → elbow.
3. Pull forward, knees between his knees.
4. Grab ankles, kick up into Ashi Garami, take him to seated.

## Pass the foot

1. Cup his leg, pull your knee in — hip to hip.
2. Pinch knees around his leg, keep the foot covered (holds even if he scoots back).
3. Bridge and lift — pass the foot across the top.
4. Sit up and forward as it comes across.
5. You're now in cross ankle lock position, Achilles grip.

## Finish

1. Get your inside foot (key detail for cross ankle locks).
2. Expose the ankle bone.
3. Cross your hands — tight lock.
4. Hand high, one-handed or down to the floor, your choice.
5. Elbow to centerline = the finish.
