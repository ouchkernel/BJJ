# Counter Standard Ashi Garami — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-03-01- Countering A Standard Ashi Garami With Ankle Locks.mp4`

## Why this works

Any Ashi Garami your opponent puts on you exposes HIS legs to counterattack. That's just how the position works.

## Step 1: check his toes

- [ ] Toes outward + sloppy → go to Step 2A (toe hold)
- [ ] Toes outward + tight/correct → go to Step 2B (ankle lock)

## 2A: Sloppy toes → toe hold

1. Push the foot across the centerline, hold it.
2. Lock through underneath, palm-to-palm.
3. Head to hands, pull elbow back.
4. Push with forearm (toes point back) + rotate elbow. Finish.

## 2B: Tight toes → ankle lock

1. Find the gap — there's always one.
2. Hand in, lock your own hands, pull.
3. Half-foot lands automatically — barely any work needed.
4. Pass second leg over top (landing spot doesn't matter much).
5. Connect, finish. His own hip lock gives you the counter-force.

## Advanced entry (knee inversion)

1. Get inside foot position on your grip.
2. Invert your knee.
3. Second foot over his hip.
4. Slide knee through/underneath → pivot position.
5. Finish with standard ankle lock.

## Bottom line

Opponent shoots Ashi Garami on you → check toes → toe hold or ankle lock → flip him from attacker to tapped.
