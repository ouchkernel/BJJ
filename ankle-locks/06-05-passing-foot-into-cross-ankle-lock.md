# Passing A Foot Into A Cross Ankle Lock

## Overview

All the entries covered previously (kneeling, one-knee, standing opponent) led into a **straight** Ashi Garami / straight ankle lock. This lesson bridges the gap: how to take that same standard entry and **pass the foot from one side of the body to the other**, converting a straight ankle lock threat into a cross ankle lock threat. Note: any entry that gets you an inside heel hook also works for a cross ankle lock — this lesson focuses specifically on the foot-passing method.

---

## Setup: get the opponent down first

Using a previously-covered standing-opponent setup:
1. Identify forward leg and back leg.
2. Get the hand on the side of the back leg, two-on-one wrist → two-on-one elbow.
3. Pull the opponent forward, knees between his knees.
4. Grab the ankles, kick up into Ashi Garami, and take him down to a seated position.

## Passing the foot across

1. Cup the training partner's leg (either hand works) and pull your knee in close — hip to hip with him.
2. Pinch your knees around the training partner's leg, keeping the foot covered so he can't rip it free (this holds even if he scoots back on his butt — you stay stuck to him).
3. **Bridge and lift**, passing the foot across the top of your body.
4. As the foot comes across, sit up and forward.
5. You now have a **cross ankle lock position** with an Achilles grip.

## From Achilles grip to finish

1. From the cross ankle lock position, prioritize getting your **inside foot** — established earlier in the course as the key detail for cross ankle locks.
2. Use the preferred method of exposing the ankle bone (visual cue: you should be able to clearly see the bone of the opponent's ankle on camera).
3. Cross your hands into the finishing grip → produces a very tight lock.
4. Hand position: keep the hand up high; you can go one-handed or bring everything down to the floor.
5. Finish cue: as the **elbow comes in toward the centerline**, you get very strong locks (consistent with the finishing mechanic taught throughout the course).

---

## Key Takeaways

- This lesson is specifically about **converting a straight Ashi Garami entry into a cross ankle lock** by bridging and passing the foot across your body — it is not a new entry from scratch.
- Any entry that already gets you an inside heel hook also sets up a cross ankle lock without needing this pass.
- Mechanical core: cup the leg, pinch knees around it to trap the foot, bridge and lift to pass it across, sit up into the cross ankle lock position.
- Once across, the same principles apply as any cross ankle lock: get the inside foot, expose the ankle bone, cross hands, and finish by driving the elbow to centerline.
