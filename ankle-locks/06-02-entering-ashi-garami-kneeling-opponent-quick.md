# Entering Ashi Garami On A Kneeling Opponent — Quick Reference

## Golden rule

Can't enter Ashi Garami with both opponent knees on the mat. Get one knee up first.

## Method 1: He grabs your upper body

1. Pull grip, high on triceps/armpits (not elbows), ear to ear.
2. Force his hands down to the mat.
3. Switch: pull grip → push grip. Turn your body underneath, up to an ankle.
4. You're in Ashi Garami. Hand on stomach, Achilles grip on his ankle.
5. Light pressure → force hand further down → go to work.

## Method 1b: Lift entry

1. Bind him up, pull in, lift — both knees off the mat.
2. Post on his knee, thread foot through, lift.
3. He can't drive the knee back down — your leg blocks it.

## Method 2: He doesn't grab you

1. Grab his wrist or elbow.
2. Slide your body in toward him.
3. Lift, force hands to mat.
4. Hand on knee, lift → ankle lock territory.

## Method 3: He's playing defense (controls your arm)

1. Grab his hand, pull down into position.
2. Knee inside his knee. Foot points down (inverted).
3. **This is safe** — he can't leg lock you from either side (invert one, kick-out defense on the other).
4. Release the hand, scoot inside his leg.
5. Get his knee off the mat.
6. Foot behind his leg (quasi-X-guard) → into the ankle lock entry sequence.

## Bonus finish

Opponent's knee rotating outward as he defends → close over from outside, lock a triangle, finish belly down.
