# Winning On The Inside Hip — Quick Reference

## The problem

Outside Ashi Garami leaves a dead zone on the inside hip — cross ankle locks don't break there.

## The fix: switch entanglement, don't just roll away

### Setup (both options)

1. Feet back together — never leave one extended/exposed.
2. Grab their knee, throw your ankles across their **far hip**.
3. Back-heel — dig feet into the hip.
4. Expose ankle, lock in, fold elbow down.

### Option 1: ankles crossed over far hip

- Wedged against the floor = strong.
- Opponent tries to limp → locked in place.

### Option 2 (better): triangle over far hip

- Full triangle, not just crossed ankles.
- Lock hands together.
- Opponent tries to limp free → you just follow their body up and over. Their escape feeds your lock.

## Against a smart opponent

1. They'll try to steer you toward the inside hip dead zone.
2. Don't let them stall you there with a bare outside Ashi Garami — they'll hand-fight and sole-to-mat their way out.
3. Lock the triangle/back-heel early.
4. Finish: wrist-to-rib grip, or **roll them through to belly-down** (described as extraordinarily strong).

## One-liner

Inside hip isn't unwinnable — it just needs a triangle-over-the-hip entanglement, not an outside Ashi Garami.
