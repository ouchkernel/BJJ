# Opponent Outside Spin — Quick Reference

## Why this escape works

Your Achilles grip alone is holding the lock — no hip control (no triangle/entanglement over the hips). Opponent's hands go to the floor, he spins to the outside, hands free.

## Watch the toes

- [ ] Toes pointing up at your shoulder → grip is strong, he's stuck
- [ ] Toes turn to point down (mid-spin) → escape window is open, grip gets weak fast

## The fix

1. See the outside spin start.
2. Hook your **inside foot over his hip** (back-heel style).
3. His spin now carries YOU into belly-down.
4. Finish from belly-down.
5. Might take 1 turn, might take 2–4. Ride it, don't fight it.

## Prevent it entirely

Close the hip loop (triangle/entanglement over the hips) — outside spin becomes impossible if hips are pinned.

## vs. inside spin (02-08)

Inside spin → block with OUTSIDE leg.
Outside spin → block with INSIDE leg.
Mirror escapes, mirror answers.
