# One-Handed Cross Ankle Lock — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-05-05- The One Handed Cross Ankle Lock Variant.mp4`

## Golden rule

- Chest **down** toward the floor → go one-handed, it's stronger (toehold-like turning force).
- Chest **up or sideways** → stick with connected/locked hands.

## Do this, in order

1. Expose the ankle first — always.
2. Knee over the top, then set your hand.
3. **Check:** thumb lands just below your own pec. If it's sliding toward your hips, grip is weakening.
4. Roll over the top, pin their knee.
5. Drop the knee, head up, hips forward — done.

## Grip detail — don't make this mistake

- **Don't use a fist** — less surface area, slips down, bleeds pressure.
- **Do use the blade of your hand**, placed high, then flatten it onto their stomach for drag.

## Positioning

- Too close to opponent = they can grip-fight you.
- Move away (toward camera), pin their foot to the floor = they can't find anything to grab. Drives in tight fast.

## Bonus

Works turning either direction — inside or outside. Not locked to one side.
