# Inside Knee Edge — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-02-05- Opponent Tries To Stand Up To A Knee To Get Foot To Floor- Inside Knee Edge.mp4`

## Trigger

Opponent rises to a knee, knee turned **inward** — can't even get to half foot from here.

## Do this

1. Post on their knee.
2. Small shrimp.
3. Wedge your own knee **inside** theirs.
4. Foot-to-mat now blocked.
5. Use the wedge to seat their foot on your **rib cage** → half foot achieved.
6. Swap the wedge for **butterfly Ashi** (foot under hamstring + foot on calf).
7. Finish: flat-back — head rotates outside, back stays flat.

## Why use this one

Solves the problem *before* the foot lands on the mat, not after. Compare: 02-03/02-04 react once the opponent is already driving down.

## One-liner

Knee turns in before reaching half foot → wedge your knee inside theirs, secure to your ribs, swap to butterfly Ashi, flat-back finish.
