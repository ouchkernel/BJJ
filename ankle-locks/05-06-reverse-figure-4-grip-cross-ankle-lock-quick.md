# Reverse Figure-4 Grip — Quick Reference

## When to reach for this

Standard grip won't expose the ankle against a tough, resisting opponent. Not a default — a power upgrade for that specific situation.

## Build it — in order

1. Outside Ashi Garami already in place.
2. Feed hands through, elbow to elbow.
3. **Right-handed lock → left forearm goes BEHIND right forearm.** Get this backward and the whole setup breaks.
4. Left hand emerges between your right arm and your body → lock reverse figure-four.
5. Slip down toward the floor.
6. Drive elbow toward your own hip.

## Why it's stronger

Same mechanic as a figure-four toe hold — folds the foot inward onto the vulnerable ankle ligament. This grip gives more leverage to do that fold than the standard grip.

## Trade-off

Extra step = slower to set up than the conventional grip. Use selectively, not every time.

## Still check

Ankle bone visible once locked in — same prerequisite as every other ankle lock.
