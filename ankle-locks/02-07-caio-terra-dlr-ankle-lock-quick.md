# Caio Terra DLR Ankle Lock — Quick Reference

(Transcript ASR mangles "Caio Terra lock" as "coyote jerolog" throughout — corrected here.)

## When to use this

Opponent stands up and gets his foot flat/weighted on the mat — standard Achilles-to-half-foot transition gets hard. This is your answer.

## Setup

1. Enter **De La Riva guard** on the standing leg.
2. Grip the ankle, put your hand on your **own stomach** — reach it as far across as possible. Tighter reach = stronger connection.
3. Hook your **second foot behind opponent's other knee** — stops him backstepping/walking the connection loose.

## Finish sequence

1. Push with the DLR hook one way, trip the other leg behind the knee (tripod-sweep dynamic).
2. Bump him down **toward your stomach-hand side** — must land on the hip on the SAME side as the trapped leg.
3. Close knees, connect hands, drive hips forward from the shoulder.
4. Break.

## The one thing that makes or breaks this lock

Correct hip = lock works. Wrong hip = lock doesn't work. No exceptions.

## Their counter (know this)

- [ ] Opponent feels the knock-down coming
- [ ] Deliberately falls to the WRONG hip
- [ ] Then either stiff-arms + limps the leg out, or scoops/elevates the ankle free

If they do this correctly, you're done — don't force it.

## Live strategy

1. Threaten the lock to test opponent's ankle-lock IQ.
2. No defense from them → finish here.
3. Smart defense (wrong-hip fall) → bail, transition into your other ankle lock options.

Don't marry this lock. It's a probe.
