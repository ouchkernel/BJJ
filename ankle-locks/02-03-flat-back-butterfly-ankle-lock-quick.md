# Flat Back Butterfly Ankle Lock — Quick Reference

## Trigger

Opponent curls foot through, starts rising to a knee, trying to plant foot flat on the mat.

## Do this

1. Foot under their hamstring.
2. Second foot on their calf muscle. (This blocks the foot from reaching the mat.)
3. Hand on their knee.
4. Drive to half foot position.
5. Lock hands.
6. Back flat on the mat.
7. Rotate head to the **outside** → finish.

## The finish mechanic

Not an arch. **Flat back + head rotates outward.**

## Use this when

Opponent's knee orientation still allows an outside/flat-back finish. If the knee points inward instead, use the short ankle lock (next lesson) instead.
