# Opponent Tries To Stand Up To A Knee To Get Foot To Floor: Inside Knee Edge

## Overview

A third tool for the "opponent stands to a knee, drives foot to mat" problem — this time for the specific case where the opponent, from seated, comes up to a knee **turned inward**, making it hard to get to half foot at all (not just hard to finish). Introduces the **inside knee wedge**.

---

## Reading the situation

- Baseline: opponent seated, knee pointing outside → easy transition to breaking position, easy to start finishing people.
- Problem case: opponent comes up to a knee and turns that knee **inward** — this specifically makes it hard to reach half foot.

## The inside knee wedge sequence

1. Post on the training partner's knee.
2. Make a small shrimping movement.
3. Place your own knee as a **wedge inside** the opponent's knee.
4. With the wedge in place, the opponent driving their foot down to the mat becomes effectively impossible — you have a strong physical block in that path.
5. Use the wedge to take the opponent's foot and place it on your **rib cage** — this achieves the half foot position.
6. **Remove the inside wedge** and replace it with the **butterfly Ashi** (foot under hamstring + foot on calf, per 02-03) once the foot torque no longer down toward the mat is a threat.
7. From here, return to the **flat-back finish**: head rotates to the outside, back flat, strong finish.

## Why this matters

- This is presented as one of the best tools specifically for **preventing** the opponent from successfully getting the foot down to the mat in the first place — it solves the problem *before* the foot lands, rather than reacting after (contrast with 02-03/02-04, which assume the opponent is already driving toward the mat).
- Once the wedge has done its job (foot secured to your ribs, half foot achieved), it's swapped out for the butterfly Ashi to free your knee up and transition into the standard flat-back finish.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Trigger: opponent rises to a knee turned **inward**, making half foot hard to reach at all.
- Fix: shrimp + wedge your own knee **inside** the opponent's knee — blocks their foot from reaching the mat.
- Use the wedge to seat the foot on your rib cage (half foot achieved), then swap the wedge for butterfly Ashi.
- Finish from there with the standard flat-back: head outside, back flat.
