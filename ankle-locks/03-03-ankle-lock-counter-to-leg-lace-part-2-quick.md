# Ankle Lock Counter To Leg Lace (Part 2) — Quick Reference

## Defend first

- Toes point **away** from opponent's armpit.
- Heel stays **close to your own hip**.

This buys time — opponent takes longer to reach your heel.

## Counter sequence

1. Opponent digs for your heel (slow, because you defended right).
2. Grab your own ankle, pass it into the gap by their knee.
3. Get a hold of their knee, then pull up to grab their ankle.
4. Loop around their hip, hand on their knee.
5. Hollow body → half-foot position.
6. Close grips, finish.

## Safety rule

Cross your own ankles **outside leg on top**. Wrong way = opponent splits your feet and escapes.

## Tighten the grip

Daylight left after connecting hands? Roll your wrists over each other for a tighter elbow-to-leg connection.

## Why this is safe to commit to

Once both of opponent's legs are bound (your crossed ankles + your lock), they're in "double trouble" — even inverting won't get them out.

## Benchmark

Defend properly (toes away, heel to hip) → snatch the ankle in the window that buys you → cross ankles correctly → half-foot → finish.
