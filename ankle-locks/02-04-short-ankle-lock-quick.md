# Short Ankle Lock — Quick Reference

## Branch point

Opponent's knee turned outward → use flat-back/butterfly (02-03).
Opponent's knee turned **inward** → use this (short ankle lock).

## Do this

1. Post on their knee.
2. Lift elbow, bring your leg **short over their knee** (not long over the hip).
3. Lock a triangle, foot hooked inside their hip.
4. They try to pull out — it holds.
5. Finish: drive your elbow to your own hip, "around the corner."

## Keeping foot off the mat

- Knee turned **in**: keep elbow closed + knee in front — physically blocks the foot.
- Knee turned **out**: straighten your legs — foot touching the mat doesn't matter here, finish still comes on.

## One-liner

Knee in = go short over the knee, not long over the hip.
