# Can't Get Half Foot? Go Belly Down — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-02-02- If You Can'T Get The Half Foot Position - Go Belly Down.mp4`

## The rule

Can't get half foot → **go belly down.** Don't fight for it forever.

## Which direction? Check the knee.

| Opponent's knee is... | You go... |
|---|---|
| Outside / vertical | Outside |
| Inside | Inside (pass outside leg over) |

## Outside entry

1. Inside leg passes over opponent's head.
2. Feet in close together.
3. Post head + hand on floor, belly down.
4. Finish here, or adjust to half foot inverted for more power.

## Inside entry

1. Inside leg passes over opponent's head.
2. Second leg goes in short.
3. Rotate through to belly down.
4. Spread knees, sprawl, finish.

## If opponent also blocks belly-down (toes toward your spine)

1. **Cut the ankle** → pulls opponent's hips toward you.
2. Pass foot over top, lock triangle.
3. Rotate through, keep triangle.
4. Spread knees, belly down, finish — even without half foot.

## Bottom line

Half foot fails → knee orientation picks your direction → belly down still finishes, even in a non-ideal grip.
