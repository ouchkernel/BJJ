# Entering Ashi Garami — Opponent On One Knee — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-06-03- Entering Ashi Garami Ankle Lock On Opponent On One Knee.mp4`

## Why he's on one knee

Trades stability for mobility. Read his knee position:
- **Knee out wide** → he wants to move laterally.
- **Knee in the middle** → he wants to drive forward.

You need both entries — you don't get to pick which one he does.

## Entry: knee out wide

1. Two-on-one grip on his hand/arm.
2. Both feet underneath the upright leg.
3. Heels dig in, pull yourself toward him.
4. Feet under his knee, lift/elevate. Keep holding the arm.
5. Knee crosses over your stomach → kick through → Ashi Garami.
6. (Works with no hands too — just pull through and lift.)

## Entry: knee in the middle

1. Foot barrier directly in front of him.
2. One hand on his calf, other hand anywhere on his upper body.
3. Feet planted: one at his ankle, one at his hip.
4. Pull — his hand posts to the mat (or he faces-plants).
5. Kick through → Ashi Garami: hand on knee, hand around ankle, foot as butterfly hook.

## If he explodes to rip the foot out

Follow with your leg → belly-down finish. Don't fight the escape head-on.
