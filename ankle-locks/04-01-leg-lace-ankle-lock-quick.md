# Leg Lace Ankle Lock — Quick Reference

## The problem this solves

Double trouble (both legs laced) usually → release one leg → hunt heel hook. Skilled opponents spin out of that. Fix: attack the ankle instead, no release needed.

## Do this

1. Hand on their knee.
2. Hollow body out.
3. Elbow in.
4. Foot inside their ankle.
5. Close grips (now they can't pull out OR kick through).
6. Ear over your own shoulder, rotate out → finish.

## Check before finishing

- Whole sole of foot visible = holding only, no power.
- Bottom half of foot disappeared = you're in the breaking position. Go.

## If primary leg slips free

Foot through → hollow → half-foot → foot over their hip → same finish as before.

## If their knee points inward instead

Foot on their calf, second foot alongside, turn to your side, lock up, flat back finish.

## Pick your finish by knee direction

| Their knee points | Do this |
|---|---|
| Up / outside | Inside leg over the top, connect, finish |
| Inside | Foot inside, pivot knee to outside, finish |
| Can't move it | Go all the way around |

## The rule that decides everything

**Your knee has to beat their hip to the mat.** Hook your foot over their hip before going down — that lands your knee on the outside of theirs. Lose this race = they escape, every time.

## Benchmark

From double trouble, go straight to ankle lock without releasing a leg — hollow, elbow, foot inside, win the knee race, finish belly down.
