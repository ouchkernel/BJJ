# Proactive Inside Ashi Garami — Quick Reference

## The mistake

From inside foot position across Ashi Garami, chasing the heel for a heel hook.
- Skilled opponents turn the heel away.
- They can time a misdirectional escape against your heel hunt and take your back.

## The fix

Same position — grip the **Achilles** instead of hunting the heel.
- Pointing the toes (the standard heel hook escape) does nothing to an Achilles grip.
- Opponent tries the misdirection escape → he breaks his own leg instead.

## Do this

1. Get inside foot position across Ashi Garami.
2. Take an Achilles grip (not a heel hook grip).
3. Invert.
4. Connect and lock your feet.
5. Hollow your body, expose the heel/ankle.
6. Hip sync, pin his leg to the mat → inverted break.

## If he rolls through

1. Transfer feet to the outside.
2. Land in outside Achilles grip.
3. Finish cue: elbow down to your body, then elbow to the ceiling.

## Why this works

Same entry, different grip. Achilles grip is mechanically stronger than any heel hook grip — grip choice is what decides which submission you're actually threatening.
