# Entering Ashi Garami — Standing Opponent — Quick Reference

## First check

Square stance or bladed (staggered)? Most people are bladed. Have an answer for both.

## vs. Square stance

1. Two-on-one wrist grip → two-on-one elbow grip.
2. Feet together, pull, heels dug in.
3. Go supine, hook both feet behind his knees.
4. Small lift → forces hands to the mat.
5. Kick through → Ashi Garami.
6. Weight forward on the mat? Forward shift → half-foot position.
7. He throws over the top? Follow with a hook → belly-down.

## vs. Bladed stance — front leg (works, not preferred)

1. Foot in front, catch him.
2. Feet together, inside.
3. Pull his knee to your stomach.
4. Kick over → Ashi Garami → sit up → half-foot grip.

## vs. Bladed stance — back leg (**do this one**)

Why: front leg is weighted (hard to move), back leg is unweighted (easy to pull).

1. ID forward leg vs. back leg.
2. Hand-fight for the wrist on the **back-leg side**.
3. Two-on-one, pull — catches him behind the knees.
4. He pushes away? You're wrist-deep on the knee — grip holds.
5. Pull to seated, knees inside his knees.
6. Pick either side, go to work.
