# Best Upper Body Grips — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-01-04- The Best Upper Body Grips For Ankle Locks.mp4`

## The one rule that matters

Hand grip choice barely matters. **Elbow position is everything.**
Goal: elbow deep, driven across to your centerline, settling inside your own hip.

Benchmark: make a partner tap from upper body grip alone.

## 4 grip options

1. **Figure-four wrist** — grab own wrist, other hand on partner's shin.
   - Push AND pull. Good heel-slip resistance. Forearm blocks full centerline reach.
2. **Figure-four bicep** — grab own bicep + shoulder.
   - Pull only. Good centerline reach. Weak vs heel-slip.
3. **Grab own elbow** — best centerline reach of all.
   - Pull only. Worst heel-slip resistance.
4. **Bar/compromise grip** — bar sits on top of opponent's ankle.
   - Push AND pull. Good heel-slip resistance. Weakest centerline reach.

## Cheat table

| Grip | Push+Pull | Centerline | Heel-slip risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fig-4 wrist | Yes | OK | Low |
| Fig-4 bicep | No | Good | High |
| Own elbow | No | Best | Highest |
| Bar grip | Yes | Weak | Low |

## 2 failure modes (same regardless of grip)

- Daylight → fix: get elbow-deep.
- Foot too far through / too far in front → fix: half-foot position.

## Note

Once your foot position is good, heel-slip risk drops a lot no matter which grip you picked. Grip choice matters most when foot position isn't dialed in yet.
