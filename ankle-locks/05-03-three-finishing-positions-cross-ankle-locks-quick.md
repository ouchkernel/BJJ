# 3 Cross Ankle Lock Finishes — Quick Reference

Source: `7713668694114-05-03- 3 Finishing Positions For Cross Ankle Locks.mp4`

## The 3 that work (with outside Ashi Garami + half-foot)

1. **Outside hip** — shoulder down, close the elbow. That alone hurts. Works one-handed too.
2. **Flat back** — feet connected over their hip, rotate through. Compact body, elbow down, rotate to the other leg — not a straight-back fall.
3. **Over the top** — rotational variant of the same family, also strong.

## The 1 that doesn't: inside hip

- Feels weak even with a perfect half-foot grip, when paired with outside Ashi Garami.
- Opponent posts a hand, limps free easily.
- **Treat inside hip + outside Ashi Garami as a warning sign, not a finish.**

## If you land on inside hip anyway

Roll through:
1. Recognize it's not finishing.
2. Roll across your partner's body.
3. Come out the other side on outside hip (or another strong spot).
