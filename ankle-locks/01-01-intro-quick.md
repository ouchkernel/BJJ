# Ankle Lock Intro — Quick Reference

## Why bother with ankle locks

- Only leg lock legal at **every belt level, gi and no-gi**.
- High percentage submission, all levels.
- First leg lock most people learn.

## What changed (old vs new)

| | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Back of ankle (Achilles) | Front of ankle (anterior ligaments, tibialis tendon, joint capsule) |
| Effect | Discomfort only | Real structural damage |
| Grip | Wrist-deep | Elbow-deep — "half-foot grip" |
| Leg control | Basic Ashi Garami | Pin the hip AND knee |

## Why ankle locks > heel hooks (in some ways)

- Grip is more robust, harder to strip.
- Faster to get good at.
- Heel hook escapes often walk your opponent right into an ankle lock.

## This course = 3 skills

1. Upper body grip on the foot.
2. Ashi Garami leg control — pin hip/knee.
3. Decision trees for when opponent resists/counters.

Also covers: straight ankle locks AND cross ankle locks — learn both.

## Proof it works

Used in real competition by Mateus Diniz, Michael Musumeci, Adele Fornarino, Helena Crevar.
