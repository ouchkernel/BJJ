# Opponent Stands To 2 Feet — Quick Reference

## Get both ankles first

1. From bottom Ashi Garami, hook your foot behind opponent's near knee, pull leg closer.
2. Grab BOTH ankles, **no-thumb grip** (like a pull-up bar).
3. Can't reach the far ankle? Drop your left foot, plant right foot on opponent's near butt cheek, use it to bump the far foot into reach.

## Then feel (not look at) their weight

- [ ] Weight on **heels** → knock backward
- [ ] Weight on **toes** (leaning in) → switch grip, roll forward

This is a haptic cue — you feel it through the ankle connection, you don't see it.

## Path A: weight on heels

1. No-thumb grip, both ankles.
2. Knock straight backward onto their butt.
3. Adjust to half-foot as they land.
4. Hands connect, inside leg over, flat-back finish.

## Path B: weight on toes (resisting the backward knock)

1. Switch thumbs from behind (pull) to in front (push) the ankles.
2. Butterfly hook with near foot.
3. Plant other foot **in their stomach**, feet close together — this is what generates real lifting power.
4. Drive them forward, over your shoulder.
5. Valley-down finish.

## Key cue

Feet close together (butterfly + foot-in-stomach) beats a wide base for driving power — works even if you're the smaller athlete.

## Bottom line

Opponent standing to both feet ≠ escaped. Get both ankles, read weight, pick backward or forward. Always have an answer.
