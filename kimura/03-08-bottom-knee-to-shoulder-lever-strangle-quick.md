# Bottom Knee To Shoulder-Lever Strangle — Quick Reference

## When to use

- T-kimura bottom knee attack, opponent's knee is up and forward, blocking your normal hook-inside-the-hip entry.

## Version 1 — shoulder lever

1. Foot over his shoulder (instead of the hip).
2. He turns into you → open your knee → turn him over.
3. Release the kimura grip, connect knuckles to his chest.
4. Lock initial triangle, hand-assist.
5. Shoelaces to his back.
6. Lock the strangle.

## Version 2 — knee-behind-head entry

1. Get knee underneath, keep his elbow off the floor.
2. Knee up **behind his head** (not low elbow/low knee — won't clear).
3. Hook the leg over his pectoral muscle.
4. He spins into you → use the leg to turn him over.
5. Come up, connect knuckles to chest.
6. Fall, lock legs (initial lock), hand-assist, shoelaces behind back.
7. Knuckles to chest = immediate strangle.

## Key mechanic

- Shoulder = the lever that pins him down while you finish.
- Knuckles-to-chest connection stops him spitting out of the lock.

## Common mistake

- Don't try to throw the leg over from a low elbow/low knee position. Get the knee behind the head first.
