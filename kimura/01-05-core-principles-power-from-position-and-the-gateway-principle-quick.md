# Core Principles: Power From Position And The Gateway Principle — Quick Reference

## Power from position

- Top power line: opponent's shoulder → opposite hip.
- Bottom power line: opponent's shoulders → through his elbow → to your head.
- Mindset: kimura isn't a strength move — it's a positional move. Position gives you power, not muscle.

## Gateway principle

- Kimura's value = what it opens up, not just the lock itself.
- If figure four meets resistance, there are many other submissions/positions to flow into.

## Full system roadmap (recap)

1. Multiplicity: 7 kimura varieties.
2. Foundation: figure four kimura.
3. Central problem: hand behind the back?
   - Yes → direct submission + power line (top or bottom).
   - Not easily → legs (cross kimura, kimura plata).
   - Still no → switch submission (armbar, triangle) or switch position (usually the back, via T-kimura position or head-and-arm kimura).
