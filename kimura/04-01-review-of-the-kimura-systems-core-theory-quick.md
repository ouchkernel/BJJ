# Review Of The Kimura System's Core Theory — Quick Reference

## Core bifurcation

- Every kimura splits into two directions the instant it's locked:
  1. **Break** the arm.
  2. **Restrain** movement.
- Rule of thumb: hand behind back (weak posterior) → go for submission. Hand stuck anterior → switch to a different hold/position.

## Four main methods

1. Standard figure-four (two hands).
2. Cross kimura (legs incorporated).
3. Hybrid kimura (legs replace pushing hand — between kimura and omoplata, big breaking power).
4. Triangle/kimura hybrid (head locked in, strong breaking mechanism).

## T-kimura subsystem

- For uncontrolled scrambles.
- Bottom knee or top knee locks opponent down just long enough to hit a high-percentage finish.
