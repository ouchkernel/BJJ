# Russian Arm To Ashi Garami — Quick Reference

## Setup

- T-kimura → opponent's only real option is to turn up into you.
- Battle for elbow position, knee inside.
- Outside hook becomes a butterfly hook as he comes up to base.
- Even with his elbow inside, you keep forearm-to-forearm control.

## Critical rules (don't skip)

1. **Never stay passive on your back** — a violent pull rips the arm free unless you're actively hooking behind his leg.
2. **Never come up head-to-head** — training disaster. Head goes to the **opposite shoulder** as he pulls away.
3. **Grip at the shoulder**, not near the hand — pull his shoulder under your armpit. This is what actually stops the yank-out.

## Finish sequence

1. Lift him with two legs.
2. Hips clear yours → relinquish one leg.
3. Turn his body, catch him.
4. End in hold/block position.
5. Transition into your finish (ashi garami entry).

## Branch point

- Russian arm from T-kimura → either a pin (previous lesson) or this ashi garami entry.
