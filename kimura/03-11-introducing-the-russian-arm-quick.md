# Introducing The Russian Arm — Quick Reference

## What it is

- Russian arm = opponent's arm trapped across his own centerline, by any grip.
- Result: he can't base out on that side, no matter how you got the arm there.

## Why it matters

1. No base on that side → sweeps to that side become much easier.
2. Multiple grip variations can all count as a "Russian arm" — the shape (arm across centerline), not the specific hand placement, is what matters.
3. Even from bottom position, holding a Russian arm = significant tactical advantage.

## T-kimura as a system

- Two broad attack directions from T-kimura: bottom knee attacks, top knee attacks.
- Get good at T-kimura → can convert wild scrambles into a kimura, tumble into T-kimura, and dictate the pace of the match.
- Russian arm is the next major branch off bottom-knee control.
