# Trimura — The Triangle-Kimura Hybrid (Trap Triangle And Hantai Sankaku) — Quick Reference

## Concept

Trimura = triangle from bottom used to control head + arm together, making the Kimura finish much easier. Both versions start the same way.

## Shared starting point

1. Elbow two-on-one grip on opponent's arm.
2. Legs come over the top, trapping head and arm together — like closed guard around the head/arm.

## Option 1 — Trap triangle

1. Cross ankles simply.
2. Invert right knee: knee points to floor, foot points to ceiling.
3. Result: strong head + arm control, strong break.

## Option 2 — Hantai Sankaku (triangle locked backwards, no strangle intent)

1. Grip the head to pivot off the opponent's head/shoulders.
2. Reconfigure legs into Hantai Sankaku instead of trap triangle.
3. **Key detail:** lock feet in front of the opponent's near shoulder — maximizes head control.
4. Legs fire over → control head → pivot off shoulders → transfer hand to outside → lock Kimura.
5. Result: everything lines up on the power line — punishing, very hard to stop.

## Notes

- Frankie Rosenthal demos both: trap triangle (extreme arm bend, strong break) and full Hantai Sankaku (stronger control, more powerful break).
- Ethan Crelinson favors the Hantai Sankaku version in competition — relaxed leg lock, very tight Kimura result.
