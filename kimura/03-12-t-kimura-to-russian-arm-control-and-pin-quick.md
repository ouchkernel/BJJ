# T-Kimura To Russian Arm Control And Pin — Quick Reference

## Engagement rules

- Opponent turns away → his own kimura stops him.
- Opponent stays still → you pin him.
- So he must turn into you → battle for elbow position.
- Even if he powers all the way to his knees, his arm is still trapped across the centerline = you still have a Russian arm.

## Entry steps

1. Knee comes in; second foot in as a butterfly hook.
2. Lift his elbow, bring second knee across, catch his arm with your hand.
3. As he pulls away, sit up into the Russian arm — head one side, arm the other side.

## Control details (don't skip these)

- Grip: lat muscle + bicep, head in close.
- Hold at the forearm (not just the hand) — this is what stops the yank-free.
- Foot in his near thigh, fall back and lift so he goes up in the air.
- **Ear on his chest** — keeps him from spinning back into you (head upright = he escapes).

## Finish to pin

1. Post leg up, second leg in.
2. Settle into pin across his side.

## Alternate entry (opponent gets to knees anyway)

1. Butterfly hook in, second leg around the corner, sit up and hook.
2. Lift him in the air with two legs → one leg sustains once his hips clear yours.
3. Pull with your arm, he falls.
4. Sustaining leg = pendulum to get ear back to his chest.
5. Bring arm to the other side, lift, wedge underneath → settle into pin.
