# The Leg-Controlled Kimura Lock — Quick Reference

## What it is

A Kimura variant often mislabeled "reverse omoplata" — legs control the arm directly, no hand figure-four is ever locked.

## Setup

1. Bring partner's arm into standard Kimura position.
2. Legs (not hands) hold the arm in place.
3. Keep foot cocked back near the elbow — the closer to the elbow, the more control.
4. If partner extends the arm and your foot drifts away from the elbow, the arm can escape.

## Head control

- Your arm reaches across the training partner's torso.
- When he tries to sit forward, he runs into the back of your arm — blocked.

## Finishing mechanics

- **Wrong**: drive straight forward toward opponent's legs — kills pressure.
- **Right**: turn back toward opponent's head, knees toward mat, hips up off the mat, rotate for the finish.

## Entry

- Do not try to build this from a static position — unrealistic.
- Realistic entry: from turtle position (next lesson).

## Key reminders

- Foot-to-elbow proximity = control.
- Head control = arm across the torso.
- Rotate toward the head to finish, not forward toward the legs.
