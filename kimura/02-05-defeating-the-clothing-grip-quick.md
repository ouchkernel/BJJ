# Defeating The Clothing Grip — Quick Reference

## The problem

Opponent grabs his own pants/clothing to reinforce the hand. Legal even in no-gi (illegal only to grab *his* opponent's clothing). Straight-line pull loses to this grip — it's robust.

## The fix

Don't fight the grip head-on — move the hand in the **opposite/"wrong" direction** from the grip.

## Steps

1. Pull the hand away in the opposite direction from the clothing grip.
2. Take the elbow up above the shoulder line.
3. Knuckles in behind the scapula.
4. Shrug elbow over his hip.
5. Elbow down, sit on the power line → finish.

## Why the direction matters

Going "wrong way" denies him an easy re-grip. Bring it back the normal way and he sweeps right back into the same grip.

## Limits

Simple expedient — won't beat every deeply reinforced hand, but resolves a surprising number of stalled situations.
