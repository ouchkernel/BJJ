# Beating The Seat Belt — The Hand-Race Cheat — Quick Reference

## When to use

- Opponent behind you is about to lock a seat belt (body lock, head over your head, hands about to connect at your clavicles).
- Once fully locked, kimura is a bad matchup — this only works in the window *before* his hands connect.

## The cheat

1. Beat him to the spot: put your own hand on your own clavicle before his hand arrives there.
2. Protect your chin like a boxer — chin down, jaw connected to your fingers.
3. His hand runs into yours as he tries to connect his hands → instant wrist control.

## From wrist control to kimura

1. Climb for height immediately: head above his, elbow above his elbow.
2. Lock a one-handed kimura.
3. Hand to the floor, whip his elbow around in front.
4. Land across his side, grip his wrist as he tries to roll through — he comes up into simple leg control.
5. Bring everything to the shoulder line, turn base → roll-through kimura.

## If he tries to lock hands anyway

- Split his hands apart instead of letting him connect them.
- Climb for height, lock the one-handed kimura.
- **He lets go** → put him straight down, attack from there.
- **He locks anyway** → turn back in, force a severe position.
- Hand to floor, knees up, whip him over, leg over his leg, hand behind his back, follow through.
- End on the power line — bone-breaking finish.

## Key points

- Works only before the seat belt locks — start the race ahead of him.
- Height (head + elbow above his) is the trigger for locking the kimura.
- Two branches (naive release vs. locked hands) both end in a strong finish.
