# Introducing The T-Kimura Position — Quick Reference

## What it is

- A "system within the system" — the shape a kimura almost always takes the instant you first enter it out of a scramble.
- Not a submission position — it's a restraining position only.

## Why it matters

1. Nearly every uncontrolled scramble entry into kimura results in this T shape.
2. Understanding it turns "neutral" scrambles into immediate attacking opportunities.

## Restrain vs. finish

- Finishing kimura requires chest-to-chest alignment (chests facing each other), on top or bottom.
- T-kimura = bodies perpendicular, both chests point the same direction → no finish available here.

## Use it as a halfway house

1. Land in T-kimura mid-scramble.
2. Restrain from here.
3. Transition to full control.
4. Go to the finish, or switch to another attack.
