# Protecting Your Wrist And Drilling The Sit-Back — Quick Reference

## Wrist protection

1. Feel wrist/hand pain as opponent explodes up? Don't fight it.
2. Open the figure-of-four grip.
3. Turn hand palm up.
4. Curl wrist backward — no grip needed.
5. Counterintuitive but true: this does not weaken your control — control comes from elbow/lever-fulcrum, not grip strength.

## Sit-back drill

1. Partner starts in T-kimura, explodes up from bottom.
2. You practice sitting him back down.
3. Rep 1: lift his elbow immediately — hard for him to get up.
4. Rep 2: let him get up, then sit him back down via lever and fulcrum.
5. Repeat to build genuine confidence in the mechanism (not just theory).

## Key reminders

- Opponent structurally can't turn away, can't stay still — he must turn in. That's what makes the drill reliable.
- Open/grip-free kimura + consistent elbow lift = restraint even without a locked grip.
- If he does get his elbow to the mat and rises, you can still return him to the mat with the same lever-and-fulcrum extension.
