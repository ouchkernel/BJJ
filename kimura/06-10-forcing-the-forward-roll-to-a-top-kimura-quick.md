# Forcing The Forward Roll To A Top Kimura — Quick Reference

## Forcing the roll

1. From hand-behind-back, turn hips, bring opponent's elbow into your armpit, apply a pulling kimura — threatens a broken arm.
2. Opponent MUST roll over the same-side shoulder as the kimura (wrong-shoulder roll = they break their own arm, no escape).
3. As they roll, put both legs in the air — their roll carries you on top.

## Working the top position

1. Return to the one-arm kimura grip.
2. Watch for: opponent controlling your leg above the knee = they're in the driver's seat.
   - Fix: hand on bottom knee, bring knee in as a wedge, post top leg — extract your leg, pass the guard.
3. Come up, pressure toward the shoulder line.
4. Switch to two-handed kimura: step over the opponent's head, back on the diagonal power line, finish.

## Full sequence (numbered)

1. Bottom half guard, shallow knee shield, dominate shoulder line.
2. Control wrist, lock initial one-arm kimura.
3. Hips to the hand (not falling backward) — traps the hand.
4. Forced roll over kimura-side shoulder; legs up, ride over the top.
5. Lock one-hand kimura under your own wrist, up to shoulder line.
6. Push bottom knee in, push top knee, extract leg.
7. Come up, threaten shoulder line.
8. Classic figure-four, up to power line — finish.
