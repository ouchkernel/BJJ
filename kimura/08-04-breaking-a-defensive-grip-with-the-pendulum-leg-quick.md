# Breaking A Defensive Grip With The Pendulum Leg — Quick Reference

## The problem

- Once opponents have seen this roll, they defend by tucking their hand inside their own thigh before it can go behind their back.

## Governing principle

- Can't get the hand behind the back directly? Trust the legs, not the hands.
- You're using your legs + full rolling bodyweight against only his hands — favorable exchange.

## Breaking the grip — steps

1. Hook in as usual (wedge, hook the arm).
2. Opponent defensively tucks hand inside his own thigh.
3. Pendulum the outside leg — even a light wave stresses his grip.
4. He grips tighter under pressure → a strong pendulum swing pops the hand down/free.
5. Pass off, bring hand across, heist hips, post hand across his thighs — winning position.

## Finishing checklist

1. He tries to raise his head — blocked by your lat/tricep.
2. Heist to knees, hand catches across his two hips (stops him moving).
3. Turn back toward his head — strong, powerful lock.

## Key reminders

- Anticipate the defensive thigh-tuck grip — it's the normal counter.
- Pendulum leg = the grip-breaking tool, not brute hand strength.
- Head control (lat/tricep block) + hip control (hand across hips) lock him down before the final turn to finish.
