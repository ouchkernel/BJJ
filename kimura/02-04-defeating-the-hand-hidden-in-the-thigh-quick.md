# Defeating The Hand Hidden In The Thigh — Quick Reference

## Why it's dangerous

Hand-in-thigh does two things: reinforces the hand AND straightens the whole arm (unlike locked hands, which keep a bend that helps you). Straight arm = harder kimura setup.

## Legal grip

- ILLEGAL: grabbing 1-3 fingers and pulling.
- LEGAL: gripping all four knuckles as a line — strips the hand regardless of how tight he holds on.

## Steps

1. Dorsal position, chest to his elbow — hold one-handed with confidence.
2. Free hand palm up, grip all four knuckles.
3. Withdraw his arm out of his thigh.
4. Hand behind the back, other hand reinforces.
5. If he tries to straighten his arm back out — climb over the wrist, don't let go, lock it.
6. Elbow down, cover his head → finish position.

## Key rule

Grip all four knuckles, never individual fingers.
