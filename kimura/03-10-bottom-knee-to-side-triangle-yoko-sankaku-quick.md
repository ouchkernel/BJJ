# Bottom Knee To Side Triangle (Yoko Sankaku) — Quick Reference

## Version 1

1. Knee wedges underneath opponent's hip/lat as he explodes up — hold and control.
2. Go over his near-side head (not over the shoulder this time).
3. Hook — his arm ends up positioned so you can slip your arm underneath his elbow.
4. Push off bottom leg, push him back down.
5. Lock initial triangle with your feet.
6. Reinforce: hold elbow joint, unlock feet, straighten body around his head.
7. Relock → strangle position.

## Version 2 (mirror side, shows leg mechanics)

1. Knee inside as he explodes up.
2. Pass leg across, fit underneath his armpit.
3. Push off bottom leg, send legs through.
4. Initial lock is loose — don't expect a finish yet.
5. Adjust: lock elbow, bottom foot on floor, turn belly-down.
6. Relock for tight triangle coverage.
7. Return to kimura grip, strangle for the finish.

## Key point

- T-kimura → yoko sankaku (side triangle) by passing the leg under the armpit instead of over the shoulder.
- Always expect an adjustment step before the triangle is finish-tight.
