# Countering A Leg Grab With The Opposite-Shoulder Roll — Quick Reference

## The problem

- You take inside position → opponent grips your leg (good defense, sets up Ashi Garami-type entanglement).
- Same-side Kimura is now blocked — his head is tucked tight to your leg, protecting that arm.

## The counter — steps

1. Post two hands on him.
2. Use your **forearm** (not hand) to move his head to the side.
3. This exposes his other elbow (the one not blocked by the leg tie-up).
4. Catch that arm over the top.
5. Tuck your elbow through, roll to the opposite side (the "awkward" opposite-shoulder roll).
6. Land in T-Kimura on the other side.

## Follow-ups from T-Kimura

- Bottom-knee entry as he tries to come up.
- Butterfly hook as he reaches his base → sets up sweeps.

## Second version (same idea)

1. Step to inside space, he ties up the leg.
2. Move his head → expose second Kimura.
3. Reach across his body.
4. Roll across → T-Kimura → many attacking options.

## Key point

- Don't fight the leg grip — move the head with your forearm to open the far elbow, then roll opposite.
