# The One-Handed Kimura — Quick Reference

## When to use it

Opponent over-wraps your hip from side control kimura, denying you the power line and the step-over-the-head.

## Steps

1. Take your pulling hand and grip the **blade of his hand** (not the wrist).
2. Place your free hand down by his hip as a blocker.
3. Even as he wraps his arm around your waist, tripod up and walk all the way around until your ear touches his hip.
4. Drive forward, coming around the corner with the legs, then rotate all the way through.
5. Ear crosses his stomach — this is where the pressure spikes. Drive leg pushes off the mat.

## Key mechanics

- It looks like a one-hand break — it isn't. **Legs + rotation** do the breaking; the hand is just a connector.
- This is one of a family of kimuras that cover each other — when one is defended, switch to another.

## Watch for

- Opponent over-wrapping your hip specifically to stop you stepping over his head — that's your cue to switch to this variation.
