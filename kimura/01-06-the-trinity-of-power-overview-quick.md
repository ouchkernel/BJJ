# The Trinity Of Power Overview — Quick Reference

## Starting point

- Introduced from side kimura (applied from side control) — clearest illustration of the mechanics.

## The trinity of power

1. **Head control** — nearly all kimura escapes rely on moving the head. Lock it down.
2. **Shoulder line** — get the elbow up to the line joining both shoulders (elbow-shoulder-shoulder straight). Closer to the line = more tension, less effort to break.
3. **Leg power** — bring the legs into the kimura for pressure, not just the hands. Especially useful when the opponent's hands are locked together.

## Core reframe

- Hands = transmitters, not the power source.
- Real horsepower of every kimura variation = legs and hips.

## Why it matters

- These three elements (head, shoulder line, legs/hips) recur in every kimura variation and position in the course — master them first.
