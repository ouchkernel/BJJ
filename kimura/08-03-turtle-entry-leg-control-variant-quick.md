# Turtle Entry: Leg-Control Variant — Quick Reference

## Setup (same as wrist-control entry)

1. Wedge: knee behind the tricep.
2. Pass foot inside, hook the arm.
3. Pressure on the arm → opponent switches → come back in.

## The leg-control grip

1. Instead of grabbing the wrist, hook the opponent's **near-side leg**.
2. Keep foot cocked back tight — if he extends his arm, the hook can be lost otherwise.

## Roll and finish

1. Pendulum the other leg to throw him forward.
2. Controlling his leg limits his ability to sit up (main advantage).
3. Pass the leg, post across the face.
4. Come up into a strong finishing position.

## Checklist

1. Wedge behind tricep, leg around the corner, initial hook.
2. Sit on/catch near-side leg.
3. Outside leg starts the rolling action.
4. Pass leg, post, hand across thighs, turn up.
5. Strong finish.

## Trade-off vs. wrist-control version

- Pro: better control of opponent's lower body / harder for him to sit up.
- Con: hook on the leg can slip if he extends his arm — foot must stay tight.
