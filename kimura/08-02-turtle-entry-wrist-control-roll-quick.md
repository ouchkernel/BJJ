# Turtle Entry: Wrist-Control Roll — Quick Reference

## Getting to the crucifix-type hold

1. Move to the side of a turtled opponent.
2. Knee behind his tricep as a wedge (like a crucifix attack).
3. Sweep leg around the corner, hook in — back-crucifix-type position.

## Passing the arm leg-to-leg

1. Plant hand on floor, apply pressure to the trapped arm.
2. He turns his thumb to relieve pressure → arm catches in the second leg.
3. Sit on/align with the arm — very hard for him to withdraw it.

## Two roll options from here

- **Wrist control** (this lesson) — grab his wrist directly.
- **Leg control** (companion lesson) — hook his near-side leg instead.

## Wrist-control roll steps

1. Reach through, grab opponent's wrist (not his leg).
2. Come up inverted — wrist grip makes the arm impossible to pull free.
3. Pendulum the outside leg to force the roll, even against resistance.
4. Pendulum action drives his hand behind his back and takes him to the mat.

## Critical timing rule

- **Your head must stay in front of his head during the roll.** If his head gets ahead, you lose control before you can pin him.
- Once ahead: switch hands, bring your head across his body.
- His head rising to follow runs into your tricep — pinned.

## Full checklist

1. Wedge behind tricep.
2. Leg around corner, catch arm.
3. Pressure → arm feeds to second leg.
4. Hand past ear, grip wrist.
5. Invert, force roll via torque (arm hurts if he resists rolling).
6. Hand across body — head must stay ahead of his.
7. Plant hands, turn back toward his head for the finish.

## Watch for

- Letting his head beat yours across during the roll = lost control, restart or pivot to a different finish.
