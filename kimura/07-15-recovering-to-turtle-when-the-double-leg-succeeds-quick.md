# Recovering To Turtle When The Double Leg Succeeds — Quick Reference

## When to use

- Double leg too fast/strong — hands driven to the mat before the long-arm kimura grip locks.

## Recovery sequence

1. Drop down into turtle rather than fighting the takedown.
2. Recognize the inside-arm opportunity (same framework as earlier turtle-kimura work).
3. Lock the opponent's inside arm.
4. Post your knee up.
5. Land back across his body in the standard attacking position.
6. Step over into the winning position.

## If he gets deep and lifts before securing back control

1. Lock the forearm grip (same grip used throughout the system).
2. Put your leg up.
3. Roll underneath your own leg to take him through.
4. Land in a good attacking position.
5. Separate his hands, switch, step over his head.
6. Arrive on his power line, ready for the finish.

## Key point

- Losing the standing double-leg exchange isn't the end of the kimura attack — turtle is the built-in fallback, not a separate scramble.
