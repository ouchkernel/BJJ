# Three Methods Of Entry — Elbow Penetration And Wrist Grips — Quick Reference

## The three entry methods

1. **Elbow penetration** — go under the elbow, penetrate through, hand lands in kimura-ready position.
2. **Supinated wrist grip** — palm up.
3. **Pronated wrist grip** — palm down.

## Which wrist grip to use

| Opponent's hand position | Grip |
|---|---|
| Above the chest | Supinated (palm up) |
| Below the chest | Pronated (palm down) |

## Wrist control rule

1. First contact on the wrist: grip with the **thumb** (applies to both pronated and supinated).
2. Finishing grip (thumb vs. no thumb): personal preference — both have broken arms successfully.

## Reminder

- A complete kimura player needs all three entry methods, not just a favorite one.
