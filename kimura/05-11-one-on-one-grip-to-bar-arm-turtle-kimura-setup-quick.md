# One-On-One Grip To Bar Arm — Turtle Kimura Setup — Quick Reference

## What is a one-on-one

- Hands come through behind the opponent's armpit and secure his arm. Works either side; can switch sides freely.

## Grip progression

1. **Avoid:** gripping just the wrist — easy for opponent to pommel free.
2. **Better:** control the knuckle line (never fingers — illegal). Still pommel-able alone.
3. **Best:** connect his palm to his own chest — surprisingly hard for him to pull free. Still just 1 hand vs. 1 hand — don't hold forever, use it to generate motion.

## Getting to the bar arm

1. Goal of the motion: get past the opponent's head.
2. Get hold of the hand, move around the partner, apply **two hands on one**.
3. Step your knee over his head, lift/lever the arm out — forearm at his shoulder/tricep (near armpit) = one lever end, wrist = other end. Already resembles a kimura.
4. Team both hands to lift the hand higher than his back.
5. The instant the hand clears the back: bring your arm through and bar it across his arm.
   - **Never** put your hand on the same side as the gripped hand (he can drop his elbow and break it).
   - Drive your forearm to the **opposite shoulder** instead — locks the arm in place.
6. Result: strong kimura hold, full ability to move him around.

## Why it holds

- The bar running across his back means his hand trying to return underneath is blocked by your forearm bones — physically can't get through.
