# Bottom Knee To Back Control — Quick Reference

## Method 1 — knee, hook, head height

1. Punch bottom knee inside as he turns up.
2. Throw second leg over as a hook; extend it through as he bases.
3. Take top hand off, throw it across — two hooks + hands working together.
4. Avoid: opponent getting up inside your legs into your guard.
5. Win the head-height battle — head must end up higher than his.
6. Build a base: weight onto one arm, up to a hand (not just elbow) → height advantage.
7. Get elbow to floor, come across → back control.

## Method 2 — knee through, hook over top

1. Knee through inside.
2. Second foot hooks over the top.
3. Come up and hook as he rises → both hooks threaded in.

## Method 3 — lift and turn across the body

1. Knee inside, second foot passes over the top.
2. As he rises on top, elbow lifts him, leg turns him across the body.
3. Hug the arm, bring arm around → into usual back attacks.

## Method 4 — lever over the hip

1. Knee through, foot over his hip.
2. As he fights up, use the leg as a lever to lift him over.
3. Hug the arm, bring it around the corner.
4. Feet land in position to lock up the back.

## Watch for

- Losing the head-height battle = you end up in his guard, not on his back.
- Opponent getting inside your legs = failure state, avoid it while threading hooks.
