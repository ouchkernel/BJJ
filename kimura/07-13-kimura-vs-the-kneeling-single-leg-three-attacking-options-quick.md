# Kimura Vs. The Kneeling Single Leg — Three Attacking Options — Quick Reference

## Setup

- Scenario: you standing, opponent on two knees with a single leg (tackle/sweep single leg).
- Athletic, weighted stance — don't stand tall/passive or he switches to a double leg and drops you.
- Height advantage over him = easy access to control his head first.

## Option 1 — Stuff the head

1. Stuff opponent's head down.
2. Catch and lock the kimura.
3. Bring feet in close (safe — holding his hand blocks his double-leg switch).
4. Pitch down, bring him over into attacking position.

## Option 2 — Shuck the head outside

1. Hip bump to move his head away, pass head to outside.
2. Step foot around the corner as a hook.
3. Lift him over from the floor into attacking position.
4. Follow down: switch legs, elbow to floor, land in breaking position.

## Option 3 — Physically move the head over

1. Hip bump to bring head over.
2. Go in and lock the kimura.
3. Step foot all the way around the corner.
4. Take him over with sumi gaeshi; body over the top into the spine finish.

## Alternate flow — foot to foot

1. Head to the inside, stay standing, lock the kimura.
2. Go foot to foot with the opponent.
3. Drag him over using the kimura grip itself.

## If he holds on tight after a head stuff

1. Come down to a shoelace grip.
2. Switch across, sweep the leg through, put him down.
3. Separate his hands — when he tries to explode back up, use the kimura to sit him back down.
4. Come over the top for the finish.
