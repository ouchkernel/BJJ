# Basement Footage — Head-And-Arm Kimura Into Ushiro/Hantai Senkaku — Quick Reference

## The concept

**Seated head-and-arm kimura** — trap the head AND the arm (not just the arm) to open a route to the back.

## Why it works

1. Conventional kimura fails (can't get hand behind back) → trap head + arm together instead.
2. Take a **seated position** → both feet become unweighted.
3. Use leg strength to separate opponent's hands.
4. Lock a triangle with the legs — from the back (**Ushiro Senkaku**) or the "wrong way" (**Hantai Senkaku**).
5. From the triangle, go back to the kimura for the finish — a triangle makes any kimura easier.

## Two triangle options

| Triangle | Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ushiro Senkaku | From the back | Opens many triangle variations |
| Hantai Senkaku | "Wrong way" | One of Ethan Crowlinson's best moves — used for a legendary break in ADCC |

## Key point

Once a triangle is locked from this position, going back to the kimura for the finish is easy — a triangle greatly facilitates any kimura attack.
