# Losing The Head: Rolling Into The Back — Quick Reference

## The failure point

- If the opponent's head beats yours across during the leg-controlled Kimura roll → he sits up → Kimura is dead.

## The good news

- This failure sets up an easy entry into the back.

## Recovery steps

1. The moment his head is winning, bring both your hands back to your own body.
2. As he turns up toward you, drive your body across his.
3. Lock back a head-and-arm control (back-crucifix style).
4. Fallback guarantee: whatever the scramble outcome (even him ending up on top), you always have the back crucifix at minimum.

## Converting to full back

1. Apply pressure on his trapped arm in the back crucifix.
2. He raises his pelvis to relieve the pressure.
3. That reaction carries you back into full back position.

## Mindset

- Losing the head mid-roll ≠ disaster — don't panic.
- Treat it as a known branch that leads into the back, not a scramble to fear.
