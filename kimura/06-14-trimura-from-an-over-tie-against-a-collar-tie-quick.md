# Trimura From An Over-Tie Against A Collar Tie — Quick Reference

## Response to a collar tie

1. Opponent ties (e.g., right-hand collar tie).
2. Over-tie: go outside/over his tying arm with the opposite hand.
3. Other hand comes through underneath, seizes his elbow.
4. Result: his tying arm is controlled at both the tie and the elbow.

## Into the trap triangle

1. Easy kick-through into a triangle entry.
2. Basic triangle strangle is available here — but instead, as he defends it, shift to trimura.
3. Apply kimura pressure via trap triangle locked around his head + arm.

## With distance control (full sequence)

1. Over-tie vs. his collar tie.
2. Control his elbow.
3. Post on hip to control distance.
4. Kick legs into trap triangle.
5. Force his hand to the outside.
6. Bring everything up to the bottom power line (his elbow in line with shoulders).
7. His head goes down — strong finish.

## Key point

- The over-tie does double duty: kills his collar tie AND wins elbow control in one motion.
