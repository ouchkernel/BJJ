# Losing The Russian Arm — Quick Reference

## The rule

- Opponent can ONLY escape the Russian arm if he posts his second hand on the floor.
- No post = no escape, no matter how strong/sweaty/explosive he is.
- Post = he can push with one hand while pulling with the trapped arm and rip free.

## The trade

- Losing the Russian arm always means gaining the second arm — it's guaranteed, because posting that hand is the only way he could have escaped.
- Immediately shift your attack to whichever arm he just posted.

## Recovery steps

1. T-kimura lock-up, bottom knee penetrates as opponent explodes up.
2. Butterfly hook in and hold.
3. Opponent posts second hand, yanks Russian arm free.
4. Post your bottom foot on the mat.
5. Turn your whole body out to the other side.
6. Transition into udigatami on the newly posted (secondary) arm.
