# The Seven Varieties Of Kimura — Quick Reference

## The seven kimuras

1. **Figure four kimura** — the classic/standard version. The king of the family; most attacks build off this grip.
2. **Figure four bicep kimura** — shortened, mata-leao-style grip; different breaking dynamics.
3. **Cross kimura** — legs added for extra braking power when the hand won't go behind the back easily.
4. **Kimura plata (Tarikoplata)** — hybrid of kimura and omoplata; same use case as cross kimura.
5. **Trimura** — triangle + kimura hybrid; locks the head down. Works from bottom or side control.
6. **One-handed kimura** — specialized situational tool.
7. **Legs kimura** — no figure four at all; legs from the start. Go-to attack on turtle position.

## Structure

- Six of seven (all except legs kimura) start from a figure four grip.
- Legs kimura is the sole exception — straight to the legs, no figure four stage.
- Figure four kimura = foundation of the whole system; master this first.
