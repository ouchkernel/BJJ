# The History Of Kimura The Man And The Urigarami — Quick Reference

## Key facts

1. Kimura = a man first, a move second. One of the greatest judo players of all time (arguably THE greatest).
2. Japanese name: **Urigarami** — a longer specific term exists because "Urigarami" is also used for the American lock, so Japanese distinguishes the two with longer compound terms.
3. Brazilians renamed the hold "kimura" because of his dominance with it in Brazil in the 1950s — most famously against Elio Gracie.
4. He trained with many people in Brazil; all quickly recognized him as a formidable floor grappler, especially skilled with Urigarami/kimura.

## His favorite setup

- Versatile — used the kimura lock in many scenarios.
- But historically, his personal kimuras were **predominantly attacked from turtle position**.
