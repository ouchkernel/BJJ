# Russian Arm To Inside Heel Hook

## Overview

A leg-locking entry out of T-kimura that most opponents won't see coming, since it doesn't come from a traditional heel-walking position. The mechanics mirror the pin and ashi garami versions of the Russian arm entry — knee inside, lift the opponent up with two legs — but the finish requires deliberately turning the opponent around a corner rather than dropping him straight to the side, so that the leg needed for the heel hook is preserved.

---

## Sequence

1. From T-kimura, as your opponent comes up, get your knee inside — not many opponents expect an inside heel hook to come from here.
2. As he explosively comes up to his base, put your foot inside even as you feel him pull away.
3. Come in and lock.
4. Bring him down, then get him up in the air using two legs.
5. Once he's up in the air, relinquish one leg and focus on turning him around the corner.
6. **Don't drop him straight to the side** — you need that leg, so he has to be turned at an angle, almost out to the side rather than straight down.
7. End with the legs locked in place, catching and setting up the finishing lock.

## Why the angle matters

- Dropping him straight to the side sacrifices the leg you need for the heel hook.
- Turning him at an angle preserves your leg position while still taking him down, letting the legs lock in place for the finish.

---

## Key Takeaways

- T-kimura can convert directly into an inside heel hook entry — an attack opponents rarely anticipate from this position, since it isn't a traditional heel-walking setup.
- The lift mechanic matches the pin/ashi garami versions: knee inside, foot inside as he tries to pull away, lock, then bring him up in the air with two legs.
- The key adjustment unique to this finish: relinquish one leg but turn him around a corner at an angle — never drop him straight to the side, or you lose the leg you need.
- End state: legs locked in place, ready to catch and set up the finishing leg lock.
- This is a third distinct branch off the T-kimura Russian arm entry, alongside the pin and the ashi garami entry.
