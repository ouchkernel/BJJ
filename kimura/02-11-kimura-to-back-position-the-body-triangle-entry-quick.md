# Kimura To Back Position: The Body Triangle Entry — Quick Reference

## When to use

Dorsal kimura, hand won't go behind the back — third strategy: abandon the kimura submission, use it as a restraint, take the back instead.

## Entry steps

1. Chin BELOW opponent's elbow (never above it).
2. Turn hand palm up.
3. Walk forearm/elbow behind his head, past the shoulder line, drive across.
4. Wedge behind his back, shoulder down below his head → get EAR TO EAR (this is what makes his far hand reachable).
5. Lock figure-of-four (kimura with the arm) — drag him in; he must lock hands or eat a simple arm lock.
6. Foot in close, pass across his body; second leg out → two unweighted feet, you're on his back.

## Into body triangle + strangle

1. Hook foot into his thigh, lift, fall across him → initial body triangle.
2. Double straight grips (back-system standard).
3. Pass-off to control wrist → he peels it off, gives you double cross grip.
4. Leg over top, hook; secure knuckle line, palm pinned to HIS chest.
5. Walk through → strangle finish.

## Mirror-angle version (same idea)

1. Chin down, post, knee open, walk around.
2. Pressure forward, guide behind head, ear to ear.
3. Kimura locks head + arm together.
4. Drive forward, knee/elbow in close.
5. Foot across opposite side, second leg out → 2 unweighted legs.
6. Hook foot into hip, lift → body triangle.
7. Both hands at knuckle line; pass-off baits primary defensive hand.
8. Foot behind spine, secure knuckle line → strangle.

## Key checkpoints

- Chin below elbow = non-negotiable for the walk-around.
- Ear-to-ear = the checkpoint that makes the far hand reachable.
- Two unweighted feet on his back = trigger to enter the body triangle.
