# Russian Arm To Inside Heel Hook — Quick Reference

## Steps

1. T-kimura, opponent comes up — knee inside.
2. He explodes up to base, pulls away — foot inside anyway, come in and lock.
3. Bring him down, then lift him up in the air with two legs.
4. Once up, relinquish one leg.
5. Turn him **around the corner at an angle** — do NOT drop him straight to the side (you need that leg for the finish).
6. Legs lock in place → catch and set up the finish.

## Why it's effective

- Opponents rarely expect an inside heel hook out of T-kimura — it's not a traditional heel-walking entry.

## Watch for

- Dropping him straight to the side costs you the leg you need. Always turn him at an angle.
