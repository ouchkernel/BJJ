# Defining The T-Kimura: Shape And Restraint Only — Quick Reference

## The shape

1. Opponent lying down = stem of the T.
2. Your body crosses his, perpendicular = hat of the T.
3. Kimura locked, both chests face the ceiling.
4. No finish possible from here — kimura only finishes chest-to-chest.

## What it restrains

- Opponent turns away → blocked by the kimura, impossible.
- Opponent stays still → you pendulum your leg up and pin him.
- Opponent turns into you → his only realistic option, but risks him getting all the way up and escaping.

## Value

- Zero value as a submission hold.
- High value as a restraining hold — buys time for other attacks to land.
- His options are boxed in structurally: can't turn away, can't sit still, must turn in.
