# Kimura From Outside Position — Securing The Wrist First — Quick Reference

## The problem

1. Every prior butterfly-guard kimura entry needed some inside foot position.
2. Some opponents' footwork denies that entirely — legs stay under threat, forcing you outside.

## The danger

- Putting a leg up carelessly while your feet/knees are still outside position = free entrance into single leg X / Ashi Garami for your opponent.
- Once he gets that entanglement, he's immediately attacking back.

## Rule

- Be very careful about *when* and *how* you commit a leg from outside position.
- Solution (next chapter): control the wrist early, then move decisively once committed rather than delaying.
