# Incorporating The Legs: Three New Methods — Quick Reference

## System split

Kimura system = anterior (hand in front) vs. posterior (hand behind back). Always try for posterior first.

## The realistic problem

- Skilled/aware opponents anticipate the kimura and actively fight to keep the hand reinforced in front.
- Question: what do you do when you can't win the hand directly?

## The answer (part 1 of 3): use the legs

1. Kimura isn't just two hands vs. one arm — bring your legs in too.
2. Rule: legs are significantly stronger than arms — use that advantage any time you're working for a kimura.
3. Especially useful when he's locked his hands or reinforced with a leg of his own.

## Three new leg-based methods (intro)

- First and most important: **cross kimura**.

## Cross kimura setup

1. Works from dorsal position (and many others).
2. Scenario: opponent's hands are locked together.
3. Post a leg on him and throw your foot from one side of his body across to the other.
