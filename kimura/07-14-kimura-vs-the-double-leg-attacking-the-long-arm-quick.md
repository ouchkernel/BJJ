# Kimura Vs. The Double Leg — Attacking The Long Arm — Quick Reference

## Identify the arms

- Short arm = same side as opponent's head.
- Long arm = crosses to the opposite side.
- Always attack the **long arm** — attacking the short-arm side means fighting through his head.

## Why turning matters

- Fast drive → your hands get driven to the floor, or he pulls your knee to a hip (worse).
- Fix: open your body so your knee comes across instead of collapsing forward.

## Sequence

1. Turn body open toward the long arm as he drives.
2. Access long arm, pull his hand to your hip.
3. This kills his finish — he needs low position pulling knees inward, which you've now denied.
4. Step: knee goes just outside his hip.
5. Big step with outside foot to line up with his feet.
6. Sit down, send him over with sumi gaeshi.
7. Scissor legs through his body.
8. Land on his power line for the finish.

## Deep double-leg variant

1. Turn everything to the outside to access long arm.
2. Hand up to your hip.
3. Step foot in line with his feet (three feet in a line).
4. Sit down, sumi gaeshi over.
5. Scissor legs into finishing position.

## Rule of thumb

- Long arm = correct target, every time, regardless of shoot height (below/at/above the knees).
