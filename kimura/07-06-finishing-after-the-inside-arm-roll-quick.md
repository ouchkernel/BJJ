# Finishing After The Inside Arm Roll — Quick Reference

## Sequence

1. Opponent to your side, body lock situation.
2. Small motion until his elbow is exposed.
3. One-handed grip, leg up, roll him across your body — head over his hips.
4. Secure his wrist, small push to get the full kimura grip.
5. If he stays static → finish right there.
6. If he explodes up on top (most common):
   - Keep head over his hips.
   - Throw your legs on top of him, maneuver your body over.
   - Work the hand behind his back.
7. Hand behind the back → forces him into a forward roll over his own shoulder.
8. Follow the roll: elbow to mat → land on power line → finish.

## Second demo (base variation)

1. Expose elbow, work through.
2. He bases a leg out — ignore it, roll **away** from his base, never into it.
3. End up on top, hips directly over his.
4. Even as he tries to come up: leg over the top of his, work elbow behind his back.
5. Follow the forced forward roll → power line → finish.

## Rules

- His explosive scramble energy is the fuel for the finish — redirect it, don't fight it.
- Trigger moment: get the hand behind the back exactly as he's exploding upward.
- Never roll toward his base — always away from it.
