# Why Kimura Vs Single Leg Is Trained Constantly (Drill Rationale) — Quick Reference

## Why this situation matters

- Kimura vs. single leg on the mat = one of the most common Kimura applications used on the floor.
- Opponents shooting single legs are almost always hunting an angle → either complete the double leg, or circle to the waist/back.
- Kimura is a simple, highly effective counter that converts the angle attempt into T-Kimura — a good attacking position.

## Why drill it constantly

1. It's one of the most common Kimura situations his own students actually use.
2. Single-leg shots happen fast and dynamically — nobody shoots in slow motion.
3. Defender can't move slowly either; the entry has to be smooth, efficient, and available on short notice.
4. Not about being the fastest — about not hanging around while someone actively tries to put you down.

## Takeaway

Frequency + simplicity + effectiveness = drill this constantly until the entry is automatic. Sets up the dedicated entry drill (next lesson).
