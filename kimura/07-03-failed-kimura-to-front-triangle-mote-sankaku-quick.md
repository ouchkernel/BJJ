# Failed Kimura To Front Triangle (Mote Sankaku) — Quick Reference

## The branch rule

- Kimura contact → hand posterior (behind back)? **Finish now.**
- Hand anterior (in front)? → next system step:
  1. Use legs to back up the kimura.
  2. Can't use legs? Switch position, OR switch submission (kimura becomes a restraining hold — armbar or triangle).

## This lesson: stalled kimura → front triangle (mote sankaku)

Core idea: invert from bottom to flip the top-man/bottom-man relationship → triangle becomes highly likely.

## Primary entry

1. Half guard bottom, shields up, lock initial kimura.
2. He hides the hand on his own thigh — stalemate.
3. Wait for him to raise his head (relieves his own kimura pressure) — this creates your window.
4. Shimmy knee back, knee in front of his body as a shield, hook foot under his armpit.
5. Move bottom leg out, invert/roll under him.
6. Aim foot for his collarbone; bring other leg around behind his head.
7. As he turns to face you, drag him in close — knee behind head as wedge.
8. Release the kimura, catch back of his head.
9. Turn corner, switch to shin grip, lock figure-of-four.
10. Legs turn out — strangle finish.

## Variation — far-side knee shield

1. Lock kimura, knee shield over his shoulder, move body out/away.
2. Swing bottom foot around/underneath, come up to attack.
3. One foot behind him, other leg steps over his head as he squares up.
4. Catch head, catch shin, turn corner, figure-of-four, finish.

## Variation — opponent stays chest-down

1. Lock kimura, drop the leg (give up half guard on purpose).
2. Knee in front, invert under him as he straightens up.
3. Push off outside foot, hook foot behind his armpit.
4. Scoop his second hand — both arms controlled, hard for him to escape.
5. Spin through, knee behind his head, figure-of-four, finish.

## Variation — drop the bottom hook

1. Catch arm, lock kimura, drop bottom hook.
2. Push off bottom leg, pull him in elbow-to-elbow.
3. Leg as wedge behind his head, pull around corner.
4. Figure-of-four, strangle finish.

## Checklist

- [ ] Confirm hand is anterior before committing to this whole sequence.
- [ ] Let his own escape attempt (head rising) create your inversion window.
- [ ] Knee behind his head as a wedge before locking the figure-of-four.
- [ ] Legs turn out (standard front-triangle mechanic) for the strangle.
