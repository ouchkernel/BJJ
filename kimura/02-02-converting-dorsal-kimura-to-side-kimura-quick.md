# Converting Dorsal Kimura To Side Kimura — Quick Reference

## Why

Finishing straight from dorsal position = push-dominant, weak, and lets opponent post on your knee, spin out, and threaten arm locks. Convert to side kimura instead — puts you on the power line.

## The conversion steps

1. Connect his elbow to your armpit.
2. Drive his hand up to the **scapula** — never the lower back.
3. Windshield-wiper your feet; turn with your chin in front of his shoulder.
4. Take your elbow **over** his body and post on it — never lead down with the elbow in front (face-plant risk).
5. Take a big step across his body → lands you on the power line.
6. Shrug elbow over his hip, push pelvis forward, step legs ~90 degrees apart, elbow to elbow.
7. Finish — on the power line, even millimeters of pressure breaks the arm.

## Watch for

- If he's not flat enough, the position can slide back into a push — reset with the pelvis push + big step.

## Bottom line

This dorsal → side conversion is the single most desirable transition from a dorsal kimura into a strong finish.
