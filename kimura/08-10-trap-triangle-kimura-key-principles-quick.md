# Trap Triangle Kimura: Key Principles — Quick Reference

## Entry: elbow post and wrist

1. Control his elbow (post) and his wrist.
2. Feet on hips, kick legs up, drag him forward.
3. Pass wrist to the outside (hand-fight passes right hand → left hand).
4. Shimmy, turn, lock — up to the power line.

## Why this position works (the two pillars)

1. **On the power line** — the preferred bracing position from bottom.
2. **Real head control** — unlike closed guard, where the opponent can posture up and escape easily.

## Matches the general Kimura system rules

- Control the head.
- Brace/finish from the power line.
- Get a good bend in the opponent's arm.

## Bottom line

Following these three rules consistently = higher finishing percentage from bottom, not just on this move.
