# Freeing The Trapped Knee To Land T-Kimura From Half Guard — Quick Reference

## Why it matters

- T-Kimura = excellent control from any scramble, with bottom-knee AND top-knee finishes available.

## Core problem

- Opponent controls leg **above the knee** → he controls the whole leg.
- Free the knee → he loses most control (lock beneath the knee = minimal control).

## Steps — freeing the knee (Kimura already locked)

1. Post on head.
2. Come up to tripod.
3. Second foot in as a wedge, inside his knee.
4. Knee frees.
5. Bring freed knee across his hip, wedge on hip.
6. Come out onto a free leg.
7. Cross his body → land in T-Kimura.

Note: the transition is unstable — that's fine, the Kimura grip is already controlling him.

## T-Kimura response tree

| Opponent does | Result |
|---|---|
| Turns away | Fails — Kimura holds him regardless |
| Stands still | Fails — sit up, pin across his side, arm trapped |
| Turns up into you | Only real option — respond below |

## If he turns up into you

- **Strategy 1**: come in with the bottom knee.
- **Strategy 2**: leg over as a cross-face → both shoulders to the floor → up to your knees → knee around his hands → cross-Kimura → close and finish, legs amplify the power.

## Checklist

- [ ] Kimura locked before attempting the transition
- [ ] Knee freed via tripod + foot wedge
- [ ] Knee across his hip, onto free leg
- [ ] Landed T-Kimura
- [ ] Read his reaction and pick the matching response
