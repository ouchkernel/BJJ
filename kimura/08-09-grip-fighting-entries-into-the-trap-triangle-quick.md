# Grip Fighting Entries Into the Trap Triangle — Quick Reference

## All entries end the same way

Force hand to outside → invert knee → lock ankles → align on power line.

## Entry 1 — Pronated two-on-one (favorite)

1. Hand-fight, pass opponent's hand across.
2. Pronated grip (palm away), connect to your hip.

## Entry 2 — Supinated elbow two-on-one

1. Pull his elbow to your center line.
2. Hand up, five fingers, hug hand-to-wrist, knuckles to your chest (supinated, palm toward you).
3. Lock his hand onto your own sternum (not your hip).
4. Force hand outside, invert knee, power line.

## Entry 3 — Vs. collar tie → over tie

1. He grips collar tie → drop your same-side knee to the floor immediately (don't leave it up — he can clear it and pass).
2. Take an over tie: hand through, hold under his elbow.
3. Foot on his hip, hold tight to the knee.
4. Foot all the way through → lock trap triangle.
5. Clear out to the side, invert knee, power line.

## Entry 4 — Vs. inside bicep tie → tricep/elbow-pit control

1. He controls inside of your bicep → hold at his elbow with one hand.
2. Second hand chops into his elbow pit (now controlling tricep + elbow).
3. Kick leg in over the top, lock.
4. Pass wrist outside, hand around the corner.
5. Invert knee, lock ankles, knees over the head → power line.

## Core principle

Whatever grip you end up with, it must be strong enough that the opponent cannot yank the arm free before your legs fire into the trap triangle.
