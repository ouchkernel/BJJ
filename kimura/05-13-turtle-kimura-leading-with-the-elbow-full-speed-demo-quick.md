# Turtle Kimura — Leading With The Elbow (Full-Speed Demo) — Quick Reference

## Entry sequence

1. Head forward to access wrists; optionally feed a wrist across and bring it in tight.
2. Come around the corner, secure two-on-one grip, hold.
3. Knee controls his head.
4. Go straight to the bar arm — trapped even if he bases his other hand and pushes to come back.
5. Lock the original kimura grip, work hand over.
6. If he keeps his base hand down and won't let the arm go over: kimura pressure is forward and breaking — he has to roll to relieve it.

## The critical correction

- **Mistake:** dive head-first around the corner — if he turns left, you face-plant.
- **Fix:** lead with the elbow, not the head.
  1. Elbow comes over first.
  2. If he turns left, elbow + foot hold you up (not your face).
  3. Big bold step across his body.
  4. Elbow to elbow.
  5. Turn back onto the power line = breaking position.

## Why it works

- Leading with the elbow lets your body keep traveling past the break point → tremendous follow-through pressure.
- This follow-through is the mechanism behind Kimura's historic success finishing (breaking) opponents' arms with this move.
