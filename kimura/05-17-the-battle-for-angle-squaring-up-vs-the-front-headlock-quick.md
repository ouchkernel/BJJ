# The Battle For Angle — Squaring Up Vs The Front Headlock — Quick Reference

## The situation

- Both on knees, opponent in on a single leg. First fight is over angle.

## Attacker's plan (the guy on the single leg)

1. Work around behind you, forming an angle.
2. This weakens your whizzer and lets him reach your second leg with his free hand.
3. Pull → drop you to the hip → takedown complete.
4. Always hunting angle toward your back.

## Defender's plan (you)

1. Punch a hand through as he tries to come around.
2. Stuff his head with the other hand.
3. Sprawl your hips down directly over him.
4. Result: takedown becomes very difficult → go directly into the front headlock system.

## Outcomes

| Who wins the angle battle | Result |
|---|---|
| You (defender) | Front headlock → front headlock system |
| Him (attacker) | Takedown completed, or he takes your back |
