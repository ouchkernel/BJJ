# Pronated Wrist Grip Entry From Bottom Side Control — Quick Reference

## Grip selection rule

- Hand **above** chest line → supinated (palm-up) grip.
- Hand **below** chest line → pronated (palm-down) grip. (This lesson.)

## Situation

- You're on top, opponent defending from bottom side control with an underhook.

## Step 1 — kill the underhook first

1. Control at the hip and at the elbow joint.
2. Bring your elbow to his elbow (elbow-to-elbow) — the farther apart your elbows are, the more he can use the underhook to move you around.
3. Elbow-to-elbow makes the underhook considerably harder for him to use.

## Step 2 — pronated grip entry

1. Punch back, take a palm-down grip on his wrist (hand positioned close to his elbow).
2. Quick lift directly over the wrist — works even against a strong resisting arm ("lift, flick, hand's in front").
3. Feed your hand inside.

## Step 3 — finish based on his reaction

- Hand left exposed → go straight behind the back into finishing sequences.
- Arms locked / hand tucked under thigh → control partner, work hands through, lock arm over back, elbow on floor, then finish.
- Can't separate his hands at all → throw a foot over into cross-Kimura and attack from there.

## Freebies (don't rely on these)

- Opponent posts a hand on the knee (e.g., in knee-on-belly) = easy grab-and-go Kimura entry.
- Assume a good opponent won't hand you this — use the elbow-to-elbow route instead.
