# The Elbow Battle: Lever And Fulcrum Control — Quick Reference

## The rule

- Elbow inside wins. His elbow inside yours = he gets up every time.
- Your target: elbow underneath and inside his, his elbow kept elevated off the mat.

## What each elbow position gives him

| His elbow position | Result |
|---|---|
| Held up, can't move | Can't get up at all |
| Pulled in, gets to the mat | Gets up quickly like an athlete |
| Gets fully inside your elbow | Gets all the way up, you have nothing |

## Lever and fulcrum

1. Dominate his elbow as the lever.
2. Even if he gets all the way up to his knees, lift the elbow → sit him back down.
3. Build a strong base with your hand as he rises to face you.
4. Trust the mechanism — don't bail to guard just because he got up.

## The one failure condition

- His elbow getting fully inside yours kills the lever/fulcrum. If that happens, switch to a different attack.
