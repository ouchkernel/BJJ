# Elbow Penetration Method From North-South — Quick Reference

## Requirement

- Must be in **north-south**. Doesn't work from perpendicular side control.

## Step-by-step

1. Thumb under opponent's tricep — hand on one side, arm on the other.
2. Push thumb under as far as it goes.
3. Use floor hand to shift into quasi-north-south.
4. Work arm under the elbow; opponent keeps arms tight — bring hand up as far as it'll go.
5. Bring hand across, drive **elbow forward** (not back toward opponent's shoulder).
6. Drive whole body toward the opposite hip; legs get opponent moving.
7. Goal: elevate opponent's elbow as high as possible.
8. Elbow elevated → flip wrist to **palm up**.
9. Take knees off the mat.
10. Push opponent's wrist over your hand.
11. If stuck: use heel of hand to push opponent's hand forward (always works, no matter how strong the arm) → creates space to thread hand through.
12. Hand through → lock the figure-four.
13. Middle finger controls wrist bend (yours and his).
14. Come around the corner, cover the head, go to finishing sequences.

## Key mechanics

- Elbow forward = whole-body drive. Elbow back toward opponent's shoulder = just your bicep, and it loses.
- Opponent's elbow height is everything: high = penetrable, low/floor = very hard.

## Full sequence from side control

1. Side control → near side → far side → move around.
2. North-south: wrist in, elbow forward, flip wrist.
3. Knees off opponent for compression.
4. Heel-of-hand push → hand goes inside.
5. Lock figure-four.
6. Block the head.
7. Finish.
