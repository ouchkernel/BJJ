# The Outside Arm Roll (Makikomi) — Quick Reference

## Which roll to use

| Opponent's hands | Roll to use |
|---|---|
| Locked | Inside arm roll |
| Not locked (tight waist grip, hand on bicep) | Outside arm roll (makikomi) |

## Makikomi steps

1. Control his arm wrist to wrist — never reach above the elbow.
2. Lock wrist to wrist so his body ties into yours.
3. Roll in a **forwards diagonal** — never into his base.
4. Extend inside leg, turn body open, roll him through.
5. End up across his side in good attacking position.

## Follow-up kimura (don't miss this)

- As he swims back to re-face you, his arm slides underneath yours → catch it, lock a quick kimura.
- Frame hand comes through for the grip.
- Keep his trapped hand behind his back — don't let him tuck it under his own thigh.
- Switch base, elbow to floor, walk around his shoulder line, finish on the diagonal power line.

## If he never locks his hands (variation)

1. Catch wrist to wrist, bring leg out, turn underneath him → good pin.
2. If he pushes his hand through to escape, catch it there.
3. Lock hands figure-of-four.
4. Hips up, walk past shoulder line, finish on the diagonal power line.

## Key points

- Wrist-to-wrist control, not above the elbow.
- Forwards diagonal, not into his base.
- Best kimura chance appears *after* the roll, when he tries to swim back to face you.
