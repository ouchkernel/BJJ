# Power Prop Counter — Snake Through To T-Kimura And Strangle Finish — Quick Reference

## Target

Attack the **propping arm** (the elbow post), not the underhook arm.

## Steps

1. **Free the trail knee**: post hand on his hip, turn knee toward his opposite hip, come up into a knee-on-belly-type posture.
2. **Seize the wrist.**
3. **Head control (mandatory)**: use your head to press his head down, then bring your armpit over the top of his head.
4. **Snake the hand through** and roll your body across his in one motion → kimura grip locked (head trapped too).
5. **Point toes, scoot hips**, put your foot on his leg and push out → lands you in **T-Kimura**.
6. As he tries to turn into you, control his body direction.
7. **Bottom-knee finish**: he comes up → bring bottom knee in underneath → hook foot over his shoulder → turn him over.
8. Fall to your side, pull his knuckles into your shoulder/chest.
9. Lock an **initial figure-of-four** (hand-assisted if needed).
10. **Finish with the strangle** (not the arm break).

## Key details

- Nothing works on the propping arm until the trail knee is free — do this first.
- If his head is up, the armpit-over-head step fails — press the head down first.
- If he fights to stand up mid-sequence, keep manipulating the elbow throughout.
