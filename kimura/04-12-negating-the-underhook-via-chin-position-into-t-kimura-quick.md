# Negating The Underhook Via Chin Position Into T-Kimura — Quick Reference

## The key rule

- Best way to control an underhook = head position, specifically **chin over his elbow**.
- Chin drifting up toward his shoulder = underhook gets stronger. Chin over his elbow = underhook gets shut down.

## Sequence

1. Chin over his elbow (before he hikes it).
2. Sweep across to elbow-to-elbow.
3. Secure the initial Kimura lock.
4. Post on head, come up to high tripod.
5. Foot inside his knee → frees the trapped knee.
6. Roll across his body.

## If he turns up into you

- Keep his elbow elevated the whole time.
- Lever him back down to the mat — works even if he gets all the way up to his knees.

## Finish

1. Shoot leg over the top.
2. Tripod again.
3. Knee over his hand.
4. Come out the back door → tight cross-Kimura.
5. Elbow up to the shoulder line.
6. Finish.

## Checklist

- [ ] Chin over elbow BEFORE he hikes the underhook
- [ ] Elbow-to-elbow secured
- [ ] Kimura lock in place before freeing the knee
- [ ] Elbow kept elevated throughout his turn-up attempt
- [ ] Cross-Kimura locked tight via the back door before finishing
