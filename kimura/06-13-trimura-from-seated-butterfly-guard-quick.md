# Trimura From Seated Butterfly Guard — Quick Reference

## What is a trimura

Hybrid of triangle (leg configuration) + kimura (submission mechanic).

## Why seated butterfly guard

- Default starting point for all triangle-family attacks.
- More mobile than closed guard → much easier to get into trap triangles for kimuras or strangles.

## Entry steps

1. Opponent grips inside tie (e.g., inside your bicep).
2. Control his elbow with one hand.
3. Opposite hand comes across to control top of forearm / pit of elbow.
4. = Two-on-one control on his arm — very hard for him to pull free.
5. Kick legs into place, lock an initial trap triangle.
6. Adjust out to the side, invert far leg → converts to kimura attack.
7. Cross ankles — strong head/shoulder control.
8. Everything lined up on power line (his elbow in line with shoulders) — finish.

## Note

- A straight triangle strangle is available from the same trap-triangle position — this lesson diverts it into the kimura finish instead.
