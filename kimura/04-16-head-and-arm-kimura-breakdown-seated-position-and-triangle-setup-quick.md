# Head-And-Arm Kimura Breakdown — Seated Position And Triangle Setup — Quick Reference

## Trigger

Conventional kimura stymied — can't separate opponent's hands.

## Steps

1. Move around to the back; lock up **head + arm together** (head-and-arm kimura).
2. Take a **seated position**.
3. Pass the first leg across his hips through to the far side → this frees access to the second leg.
4. Result: **both feet unweighted**.
5. Use leg strength to separate his hands; place a foot inside his armpit.
6. Transition into **Ushiro Senkaku** (triangle from the back).
7. Attack the kimura directly from Ushiro Senkaku, hand on the outside of the thigh — strong breaking position.

## If he turns into you

- Switch legs → **Hantai Senkaku** (triangle locked the "wrong way", in front of his far shoulder).
- Intent here is kimura, not strangle.
- Lock feet in front of his far shoulder, bend the arm as usual.

## If he turns inside Ushiro Senkaku

- No need to reset — it converts directly into a **front triangle**.

## Why it works

- Head trapped in the triangle → he can't move it to help defend the kimura.
- Breaking pressure hits shoulder and elbow hard, and the setup is unconventional enough that opponents rarely see it coming.
