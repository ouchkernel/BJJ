# Single-Leg Kimura Entries — Additional Reps And Finishes — Quick Reference

## Rep One — clean entry to T-Kimura

1. Opponent shoots single leg, starts forming an angle — you lose the ability to keep him squared up.
2. Punch hand inside; use it as a brief base while frame arm sets; lock the Kimura.
3. Outstretched leg out first, second leg sweeps through inside the first.
4. Kimura from seated position — one strong pull puts him over even if he bases his free hand out.
5. Clamp in tight; if he moves, keep the elbow elevated (holds even if he comes back to his feet).
6. Place feet in one at a time, stretch his body out.
7. Result: T-Kimura position — ready to attack the back with any established finish.

## Rep Two — opponent locks his grip on your legs

1. He shoots, gets an angle, locks the Kimura despite you, and grips your legs hard.
2. Sweep the first leg through; tilt him over anyway.
3. If his hands stay locked through the tilt: lock a triangle to separate his hands, turn his face → classic T-Kimura.
4. If he comes up on top, priority = keep the arm trapped by any means.
5. From trapped arm, lift into whichever finishing attack fits.

## Key points

- T-Kimura = universal launch point regardless of route taken to get there.
- Frame beats scrambling: locked grip, basing out, and standing back up don't break it.
