# Getting To Kimura — Neutral Vs. Dominant Position And The Tombstone Defense — Quick Reference

## Neutral vs. dominant

| Position | Opponent's mindset | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral (e.g., open guard) | Doesn't feel threatened | Reaches freely → easy kimura entries |
| Dominant (side control, mount) | Recognizes real danger | Pulls arms in tight → tombstone defense |

## Tombstone defense

- Arms drawn in tight against the body, like a tombstone.
- Very hard to get in on the arms and secure a kimura grip from here.
- Standard response of a skilled opponent once they feel the threat of your dominant position — not a beginner "arms flopping" situation.

## The problem to solve

- How do you get a kimura hold on someone in tombstone defense?
- Answer: three main entry methods (next lesson) — need all three to be a complete kimura player.
