# Connecting The Figure-Four Grip Under Resistance — Quick Reference

## Grip rule

- Hands unlocked? Use your **thumb**. No-thumb lets him pommel free or recover his arm position.

## The problem

- Guard passed, wrist trapped, arms in — but his arm is too straight for you to reach your own wrist and lock the figure-four.
- Don't claw for your own wrist. Don't just grab-and-hope (short-lever disaster if done badly). Change body position instead.

## Method 1 — step over

1. Step over opponent's body.
2. Elbow down to his elbow.
3. Hands separated, lift — his elbow comes off the mat.
4. Throttle hands together → figure-four connects.
5. Wrist to bent-arm finishing position, step over head.

## Method 2 — shorten the lever

1. Grab your own forearm halfway down (short lever, but a real figure-four — hard for him to twist free).
2. Bring everything down, lift.
3. Off the lift, walk your grip down the wrist in two steps.
4. Sweep hand in behind, drive forward, foot over — elbow to elbow, ready to break.

## End state (both methods)

- Secure standard figure-four, wrist in bent-arm position, leg stepped over the head, ready to attack.
