# Recap: The Two Directions Of The Kimura System — Quick Reference

## The split

The instant the figure-of-four locks, choose a direction:

1. **Breaking** — hand goes behind the back.
2. **Restraining** — hand stays in front.

## Breaking mechanics

- Can't get the hand behind the back with hands alone? Add legs and hips — use their strength to drive the break, don't just muscle it with arms.

## If breaking stalls — fallback options

1. Switch to a different submission hold.
2. Switch to a completely different position/attacking system (e.g., the back).
