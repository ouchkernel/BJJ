# Udigatami Finish From T-Kimura — Quick Reference

## Full chained sequence

1. T-kimura, opponent explodes into you.
2. Bring everything inside.
3. Opponent yanks Russian arm free (posts his other hand to do it).
4. Switch immediately to that secondary/posted arm.
5. Control it, move body out to the side.
6. Set favored leg position (cross-face knee wedge + foot hooked behind armpit) — he can't yank out or push in.
7. Elbow goes over.
8. Extend body away.
9. Finish: udigatami, straight out of T-kimura.

## Key point

- No reset between losing the Russian arm and entering udigatami — one continuous flow.
