# Bottom Knee To Three-Quarter Juji Gatame — Quick Reference

## Steps

1. T-kimura, opponent works his way up — slow him at the elbow.
2. Knee comes underneath; bring leg over his shoulder, correct his body to the side.
3. Instead of finishing to ushiro sankaku (rear triangle) — slip foot over his head.
4. Come up, head over his hands.
5. Pass foot through → lock three-quarter juji gatame (not traditional juji).
6. Straighten out the arm.

## Alternate entry (opponent explodes up)

1. Knee underneath, foot over the shoulder.
2. Return him to the mat as he defends.
3. Pass foot through, second leg over.
4. Foot always goes inside/underneath his arm.
5. Lock three-quarter juji, straighten the arm.

## Why three-quarter over traditional juji

- Controls head + shoulders so tightly he can't spin out.
- Traditional juji gives up that control — easier to escape by spinning.

## Key checkpoint

- Foot must pass inside and underneath the opponent's arm, not around it.
