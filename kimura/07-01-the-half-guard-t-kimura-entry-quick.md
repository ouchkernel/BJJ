# The Half Guard T-Kimura Entry — Quick Reference

## Setup

1. Bottom half guard, knee/forearm shield — one controls hip line, one controls shoulder line.
2. Deny chest-to-chest and cross face; dominate inside position.
3. Work to the wrist, lock the kimura grip (hand still in front of body = restraining hold, not yet a submission).

## When he hides/reinforces the hand

1. Willingly give up half guard: put your leg inside his feet, let him pass.
2. Shimmy hips out with both feet.
3. Whip legs in one motion around his body — the kimura-trapped arm can't base out.
4. Use leg momentum to throw him forward/down → lands you in T-kimura position.
5. Scissor legs, step over his body, come up to elbow → power line, ready to finish.

## Guard-pass variation

1. Take the wrist, he hides the hand.
2. Put your leg inside his, give him a simple guard pass.
3. Shoot hips out, swing around to the side using gathering momentum.
4. Follow the momentum — leg across, up to elbow, breaking position.

## Rules to remember

- Follow the body: throw him right → come up on your right.
- Head must end up inside/underneath his chest, or the throw fails.
- If he tries to come back up into you, he comes up into a cross face — roll through it.
- Kimura restrains even without the hand behind the back — that restraint alone is the lever that lets you dump him from bottom position.
