# Udigatami Finish From T-Kimura

## Overview

A short, direct tie-together of the previous two chapters: losing the Russian arm from T-kimura, and the modified udigatami mechanics, chained into one continuous sequence starting and ending in live positions.

---

## The full sequence

1. Start in T-kimura position; opponent explodes into you.
2. Bring everything inside.
3. From that inside position, opponent violently yanks the Russian arm out and back — you lose it.
4. Immediately transition attention to the secondary arm (the one he was forced to post to escape).
5. Control the secondary arm and move your body out to the side.
6. Apply the favored leg positioning (cross-face knee wedge + armpit-hooking foot) — the opponent finds it difficult both to yank out and to push in from here.
7. Bring the elbow over.
8. Extend the body away.
9. Result: a clean udigatami finish, arrived at directly out of the T-kimura position.

---

## Key Takeaways

- This chapter is the applied version of the previous two: T-kimura → lose the Russian arm → recover onto the secondary arm → udigatami mechanics → finish.
- The whole sequence flows without resetting position — the loss of the Russian arm is absorbed directly into the entry for udigatami on the other arm.
- Extending the body away (not just compressing) is what completes the finish, consistent with the mechanics described in the udigatami detail chapter.
