# Ushiro Senkaku: Triangle Strangle From The Back & Closing Thoughts — Quick Reference

## When to use

Alternative finish off the same kimura-to-back transition (see previous lesson) — a triangle-reinforced strangle instead of the straight strangle.

## Entry

1. Same dorsal start — can't get hand behind back.
2. Chin drops, turn, drive through, bicep behind head, ear to ear → grab wrist.
3. Sit down, one leg across, one leg out → two unweighted legs (opponent's arm strength stops mattering).

## Build the strangle

1. Two feet around his wrist, separate hands.
2. Foot into armpit.
3. His elbow to your armpit, his knuckles to YOUR chest, sit back.
4. Fall with legs kinked (spin-out already very hard here, pre-triangle).
5. Lock initial triangle (hand-assisted), shoelaces behind his lat muscle.
6. Keep knuckles connected to chest.
7. Squeeze legs → strangle.

## Mirror-angle version

1. Kimura start, can't separate hands → switch to back system.
2. Chin down, turn, lock back of head, up to knees, ear to ear.
3. Figure-of-four, sit down, foot across belt line, second foot out.
4. Separate hands, foot to armpit.
5. PRIORITY: knuckles to chest first.
6. Fall to side, kink legs.
7. Initial figure-of-four → hand-assist → foot behind back, both arms connected, knuckles at chest.
8. Squeeze legs → finish.

## Closing thoughts (big picture)

- Kimura = one of the best gateways to other systems: kimura → legs, kimura → front headlock, kimura → back (favorite).
- Don't tunnel-vision on the kimura submission itself — a locked kimura is always a few steps from a stronger alternative system.
