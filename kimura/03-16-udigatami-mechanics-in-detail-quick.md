# Udigatami Mechanics In Detail — Quick Reference

## Why the conventional version fails

- Standard teaching: two knees pinched around head/shoulders, various arm-control methods.
- Opponent's escape: bring head toward you → rotate elbow free. The pinch doesn't stop this.

## The fix — leg configuration

1. Near knee = **cross-face wedge in front of the shoulder** (not pinched around).
2. This alone opens a gap for him to slide out sideways — so add:
3. Bottom **foot hooks behind his armpit** — actively hooks and controls.
4. Second leg often comes across too.
5. Check: with legs alone, both yanking out and pushing in should feel difficult for him.

## Elbow position

- Point elbow at his ear (NOT straight-locked or a shallow half-fix — both let his elbow rotate free).
- Go elbow-to-elbow, then reinforce with your own elbow = strongest breaking mechanics.

## Finish

- Don't just compress down with the arms.
- Move hips away from opponent + head toward opponent = actual breaking point.
- There's still more range past that point for a deeper finish.

## Checklist

- [ ] Knee wedged as cross-face, not pinched
- [ ] Foot hooked behind armpit
- [ ] Second leg across (if available)
- [ ] Elbow pointed at ear, elbow-to-elbow reinforced
- [ ] Hips away / head toward opponent to finish
