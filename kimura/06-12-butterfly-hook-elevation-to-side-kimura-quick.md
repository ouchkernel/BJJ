# Butterfly Hook Elevation To Side Kimura — Quick Reference

## The battle

- Attacker wants: hand behind the back.
- Defender wants: hand in front of body, knee closed down for base (leg-out-wide = easy extraction, so they avoid it).

## Butterfly hook elevation steps

1. Knee-in half guard, opponent crowds forward, kimura grip stalls (hand hidden/braced).
2. Shimmy hips out, hook second leg in as a butterfly hook.
3. Start lifting action — opponent's base becomes very hard to hold.
4. Drive off the hooking foot, look down at floor, tilt opponent down to the mat.
5. Land in position for a side kimura.

## Side kimura finish

1. Chase and beat the line of the opponent's shoulder.
2. Control the head.
3. Push everything down onto the power line — finish.

## Full recap

1. Knee-in, control upper body, opponent drives.
2. Come up, lock kimura grip — opponent hides hand.
3. Shimmy hips, second foot in as butterfly hook (first foot may already be over his heel).
4. Drop foot, lift, free second foot.
5. Simple lift drives opponent over.
6. Come across, quick pull, hand to floor, correct — elbow on mat.
7. Walk around to shoulder line, step over his head, reposition on power line.
8. Finish.
