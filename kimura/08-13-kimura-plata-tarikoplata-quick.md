# Kimura Plata (Tarikoplata) — Quick Reference

## What it is

- Hybrid of omoplata + Kimura = "Kimura plata."
- Named Tarikoplata after the Norwegian athlete famous for it.
- Classic version: figure-four grip → throw leg over.

## Coached version (why it's different)

- Use a **partial** figure-four: hand flat on training partner's back instead of full wrist grip.
- Benefits:
  - More hip exposed to step your leg over.
  - Easier for less flexible athletes (incl. post-hip-replacement).
  - More space for the leg to travel through.
  - More secure grip — prevents hand slippage at the last second.

## Steps

1. Partial figure-four grip, hand flat on his back.
2. Throw leg across, tuck into crook of knee.
3. Head over your own hand, turn hips out to the side.
4. Right leg advances forward, left leg comes around — hips travel across his hips.
5. Drive your hip through onto your own buttock — aim for the "falling off your opponent" feeling.
6. Strong pulling pressure → finish.

## Cue

If you feel like you're falling off your opponent to the side, you're doing the hip mechanic correctly.
