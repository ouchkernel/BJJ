# Recap And Introducing Top Knee Attacks — Quick Reference

## Why T-kimura is the cornerstone

- Opponent's only options from T-kimura:
  1. Turn away — impossible.
  2. Stay put — gets pinned.
  3. Turn into you — creates an elbow-lever battle you're favored to win.
- Even losing that battle: he only gets to his knees, still attackable.

## Two attack branches from T-kimura

| Branch | Method |
|---|---|
| Bottom-knee (prior chapters) | Bottom knee scoots underneath as space opens |
| Top-leg (this chapter) | Top knee thrown over as a cross-face to pin shoulders |

## Top-leg cross-face steps

1. T-kimura; battle for opponent's elbow lever as he turns into you.
2. Instead of bringing bottom knee in, throw the **top knee over as a cross-face**.
3. When he tries to stand, he's fighting your leg with his head.
4. Result: shoulders stay pinned to the mat — easy to hold.

## Demo repeat

- Elbow lever keeps him down as he explodes up → top knee thrown over as cross-face → he's now fighting leg strength directly.
