# Trap Triangle Kimura: Full Repetition — Quick Reference

## Setup

- Opponent in seated butterfly guard.

## Entry sequence

1. Take the hand to the center line.
2. Take a pronated grip on it; connect everything to your hip (very hard for opponent to yank free).
3. Pull training partner into the trap triangle.
4. Two thumbs underneath his hand → push wrist to the outside (works even if his hand stays glued to your chest).
5. Turn the corner around him; catch the head.
6. Shimmy hips so knee points down to the floor — **inverted knee**.
7. Lock your feet (cross ankles) around his head + trapped arm.

## Finish alignment — the power line

- Line up: his far shoulder → his near shoulder → his elbow → your head, all in a straight line.
- This is the **bottom power line** = strongest finishing angle from bottom.
- Locked ankles make posturing up very difficult.

## Note

- This is just the finish/full rep. Multiple grip-fighting entries into this same position follow in later material.
