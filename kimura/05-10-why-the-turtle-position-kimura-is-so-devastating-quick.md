# Why The Turtle-Position Kimura Is So Devastating — Quick Reference

## The claim

- Kimura vs. turtle position = very strong, very effective — and underused.

## Why it's underused

- Back attacks are so well developed in modern jiu-jitsu that most people default to conventional strangleholds when they get behind a turtled opponent, instead of the kimura.

## Why it deserves priority

1. Opens a defensive turtle player up to further attack.
2. Leads to a devastating submission hold on its own.
3. Historical evidence suggests this was the man Kimura's own favorite, most-used personal setup.
