# The Right-Right-Right Rule — Standing Kimura To T-Kimura — Quick Reference

## The rule

- Right lead → roll over right shoulder → attack opponent's right arm = **right-right-right rule** (default/conventional pattern).

## Entry steps (version 1)

1. Move in from head-to-head position.
2. Lift/pull to get foot inside.
3. Score the wrist.
4. Right knee on right hip.
5. Go over the hip (easy from standing) → straight into T-Kimura.
6. If he scrambles to knees: push his base away with your feet → attack the back.

## Entry steps (version 2)

1. Tie up standing, push-pull to get inside position.
2. Right leg across right hip as he comes in.
3. Score the wrist, leg goes high.
4. Cartwheel through his guard.
5. If he gets to knees: feet on his knees, stretch him around → back attack.

## Note

- Opposite-shoulder rolls are also viable (covered next) — useful addition/expedient beyond the default right-right-right pattern.
