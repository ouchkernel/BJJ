# The Trimura — Two-On-One Grip Fighting Into Trap Triangle — Quick Reference

## The grip

- Supinated elbow two-on-one: palm faces back toward you (vs. pronated = palm faces away).
- Weakness: opponent can break it by driving his hand toward you.
- Fix: hug his knuckles into your chest to cover that weakness.
- Use a full five-finger grip — thumb not needed, doesn't help.

## Entry sequence

1. Two-on-one grip, knuckles hugged to chest — arm now hard to pull free.
2. Foot on opponent's hip.
3. Kick over into the trap triangle.
4. Push wrist to the outside.
5. Hips up.
6. Pass the hand off, take control of the elbow.
7. Elbow to sternum, lock the kimura.

## Why "trimura"

- Trap triangle leg config + kimura arm lock, run together = half triangle, half kimura.

## Foot roles

- Controlling/near foot: manages distance.
- Kicking/far foot: goes over into the triangle shape.

## Finishing note

- As opponent's head is driven down during the sequence, the kimura gets progressively easier to lock.
