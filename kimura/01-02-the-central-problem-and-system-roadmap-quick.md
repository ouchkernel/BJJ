# The Central Problem And System Roadmap — Quick Reference

## The central question

After locking figure four: can you get the opponent's hand behind his back?

## Decision tree

1. **Hand goes behind the back** → attack directly.
   - Establish a power line first:
     - Top: side pin power line.
     - Bottom: closed guard or triangle variation (trimura).
2. **Hand won't go behind easily** → bring in the legs.
   - Legs >> arms in strength — avoid pure hands-vs-hands (the classic "strong man's move" criticism).
   - Tools: cross kimura, kimura plata / Tarikoplata.
3. **Still can't get it behind, even with legs** → switch tactics.
   - Switch submission: armbar, juji gatame, or triangle.
   - Switch position: usually to the back, via:
     - T-kimura position (pin the hand in front of the chest, maneuver around the body).
     - Head and arm kimura (route into back control).

## Remember

- Kimura's "strong man's move" reputation comes from fighting hands against hands — the system's answer is legs, then alternate submissions/positions, not more grip strength.
