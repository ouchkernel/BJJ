# Kimura As The Response To Losing The Angle Battle — Quick Reference

## Trigger

- Losing the angle battle: attacker forms a perpendicular (right) angle on you and squaring up isn't working.

## Step 1 — golden rule

- Never leave both knees on the mat once he's square to you at a right angle → easy double leg for him.
- The instant he forms the right angle: post your second leg up.

## Step 2 — build the kimura grip

1. Try to square up; if he's giving you a hard time, seize the wrist instead.
2. Bring your head OVER his head (not down toward the hips).
3. Punch the frame arm through.
4. Control him as he tries to spin around behind.
5. Sweep your leg through on him with the kimura grip locked.
6. Pull him over → land in the T-kimura.

## Step 3 — work the T-kimura

- As he fights up: penetrate with the bottom knee, or cross-face with the top knee.
- Strong finish available from here directly.

## Backup finish

- If his head rises: come around the corner, lock the triangle → triangle-kimura ("trimura") finish.
