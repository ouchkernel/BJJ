# Standing Kimura — Flanking Tactics To Get The Foot Inside — Quick Reference

## The problem

- Well-structured standing butterfly guard (feet close together) blocks a direct step-in — you'll get grabbed or foot-blocked.

## Flanking sequence

1. Stand in front, post/move to take a flank angle — force him to turn and face you.
2. Move laterally — his foot has to follow, which opens space.
3. Compound with hands: control one hand + the opposite shoulder to move him.
4. Step into the space as he resets to face you.
5. Optional finisher: fake a wrist grip, pull a leg as he sits back up → foot inside.

## Toolkit (mix and match)

- Flanking (lateral) movements
- Lifting movements
- Push-pull movements

## Goal

- Get one foot to the inside position → then start the Kimura game.
