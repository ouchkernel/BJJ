# Kimura Entry Vs Butterfly Guard — Height Advantage And Inside Position — Quick Reference

## Two prerequisites (check both before attacking)

1. Your head higher than his head.
2. Your attacking elbow higher than his elbow.

## Quick height test

- Is your armpit higher than the crown of his head?
  - No → rise up first, don't attack yet.
  - Yes → proceed.

## Entry steps

1. Forearm as buffer, come up off your ankles (don't sit on heels).
2. Move to head-to-head position.
3. Thumb post inside both his arms — dominate inside space.
4. Pommel knee inside, switch thumb post to forearm grip, get one inside foot.
5. Pass height check (armpit > crown of head).
6. Bring elbow across centerline.
7. Roll through — Kimura snaps into place as he comes up on top.

## After the roll

1. Keep his elbow elevated as he tries to stand up.
2. Bring knee in, thread foot through as a hip hook.
3. Switch and take the back as he keeps fighting to get on top.

## Watch for

- Opponent with both feet already inside (square butterfly guard) — don't jump straight for Kimura, you can get elevated/swept.
- Butterfly guard is fast and dynamic — treat this as one entry among several, stay flexible.
