# The Yoko Sankaku (Side Triangle) Setup For A Basing Opponent — Quick Reference

## When to use it

- Opponent bases his free hand out long against your turtle-kimura lever attempts → hard to turn him over → switch to yoko sankaku instead of fighting the lever.

## Entry steps

1. One-on-one grip behind opponent.
2. Convert to two-on-one.
3. Knee over his head, lever the arm up.
4. He bases his hand out long → don't fight it.
5. Lock a kimura grip right there.
6. Step foot over and behind his armpit — back-heel and hook, don't just lay it over.
7. Fall straight over.
8. Hook top leg around his arm.
9. Frame arm in, catch with elbow, turn body through, lock figure of four (= yoko sankaku).

## Finishing menu from the yoko sankaku

- Strangle (directly available).
- Pushing kimura on far arm.
- Mixed pull-and-push kimura on far arm.
- Release far arm → focus near arm → power line → breaking finish.

## Alt-angle version (when he resists the turn-in)

1. One-on-one → punch in → two-on-one.
2. Try to turn him in; he resists.
3. Lock initial kimura.
4. Foot over the top, back-heel in.
5. Sit straight back.
6. Catch his arm, secure elbow.
7. Come up, relock the triangle.
8. Options: strong strangulation, one-handed push kimura, two-handed push/pull kimura, near-arm to power line for breaking finish.
