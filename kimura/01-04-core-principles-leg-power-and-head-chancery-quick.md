# Core Principles: Leg Power And Head Chancery — Quick Reference

## Arms vs. legs asymmetry

- Legs are always stronger than arms — few opponents have arms stronger than your legs.
- Rule: can't get the hand behind the back with your arms? Bring your legs into the kimura.
- Bonus: using legs also breaks the "my arms vs. his arms" trap kimura is often criticized for.

## Head chancery principle

- Almost all kimura escapes come from the opponent moving his head — not from arm strength.
- Lock the head down (usually with the legs, most often via the trimura/triangle) →
  1. Removes his main escape route.
  2. Locks the shoulder girdle → elbow/shoulder become much more vulnerable to breaking.
- Priority: control the head with your legs whenever possible before finishing.
