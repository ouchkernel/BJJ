# Side Control Kimura: Lead With The Elbow — Quick Reference

## Setup

Opponent in side control bottom, elevated onto his side in the dorsal kimura position. Goal: convert to attacking side kimura.

## Mistake to avoid

- Leading with the **hands** when hand is already behind his back.
- If he turns vigorously into you while your hands lead, you face plant — bad control and bad leverage.

## Correct method

1. Get the hand behind his back.
2. Rotate and turn, leading with the **elbow** — elbow goes to the floor, not the hands.
3. Head stays up off the mat.
4. When he turns vigorously, you don't move — elbow is wedged on his hip.
5. Step over his head, split your legs into breaking position.

## Why it works

- Elbow wedge = anchor against his hip → kills his turn.
- He can't shrimp, turn, or yank the arm free — hip pressure + elbow position lock him down.
- Walk across his body for breaking pressure without exposure to his counter-turns.

## Rule

Dorsal → side kimura conversion: **elbow leads, not hands.**
