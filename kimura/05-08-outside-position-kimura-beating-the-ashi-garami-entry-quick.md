# Outside-Position Kimura — Beating The Ashi Garami Entry — Quick Reference

## Core sequence

1. Secure the wrist first (easy — opponent is focused on his foot position, not his hand).
2. Put the springboard leg up — this is a calculated risk, giving him an Ashi Garami entrance.
3. Do NOT delay: the instant your knee leaves the mat, go — push the hand to the mat.
4. Roll: your rollover beats his Ashi Garami entry if you commit immediately.
5. If he still gets to his knees: throw him back down, head to tackle position, pin with trapped arm.

## Three starting variations, same finish

| Start | Key detail | Finish |
|---|---|---|
| Standing outside position | Leg up, opposite hand to mat | Throw down, pin w/ trapped arm |
| Down on two knees, tight | Rollover beats his leg as he rises | Knockdown, hand to opposite hip, high-step to attacking position |
| Riskier — moving in from outside | Rollover takes you up and over the top | Put him to his back, pop up, pin w/ trapped arm |

## Watch for

- Hesitation after the leg goes up is the mistake — commit fully and immediately.
- Works even when completely shut out from inside foot position.
