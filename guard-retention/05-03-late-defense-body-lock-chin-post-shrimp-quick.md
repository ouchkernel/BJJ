# Late Defense: Chin Post And Shrimp — Quick Reference

## Do this, in order

1. Opponent has deep body lock (long arm/short arm, knees pinched in) — don't panic, don't stall.
2. Both hands to his head. **Heels of hands only** — fingers together, nowhere near the eyes.
3. Slight hip lift, work hands up along the head.
4. Grab chin. Can't get chin → jaw. Can't get jaw → ear.
5. Push head to one side **while shrimping the opposite way, at the same time**. This is what breaks the lock — not sequential.
6. Bring a knee inside (top knee preferred, bottom knee fine too).
7. Keep pulsing/shrimping out to the side until fully clear.

## Then pick one

- [ ] Closed guard
- [ ] Knee in front of shoulder
- [ ] Omoplata

Any of the three kills the body lock.

## Safety rule (always)

Heel of hand, fingers together, never fingers/thumbs near the eyes. Non-negotiable — this is how you lose training partners.

## Also do this the whole time

Keep active "sticky" hooks with your feet. Passive/relaxed legs = opponent steps right over them.
