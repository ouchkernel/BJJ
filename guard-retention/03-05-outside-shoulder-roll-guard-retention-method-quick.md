# Outside Shoulder Roll Guard Retention Method — Quick Reference

## When to use this

Opponent has contact, distance, angle, AND is now level-changing toward chest-to-chest. Frames alone (thumb post, high leg) aren't enough here.

## Do this, in order

1. Get knee-elbow connection — near side if available, **far/outside side** if he cleared your near knee.
2. Pass your hand under his armpit.
3. Push foot into the mat, come up into a **knee prop**.
4. You now have a knee shield + cross frame — enough to hold his weight.
5. Push off the floor, **roll all the way through** — you're back in guard.
6. Reset: knees and elbows together, elbows outside knees.

## Why this one

- Uses leg drive, not arm strength — very hard for a bigger/stronger opponent to stop your momentum.
- Low back exposure compared to turning away.
- Same footwork as the solo knee-prop drill — drill it daily as one movement, then apply live.

## Note

Don't fight for near-side connection if it's already lost — switch to the far side immediately and keep moving.
