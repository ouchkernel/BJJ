# Double Scoops vs Locked Hands — Quick Reference

## Situation

Opponent worked underneath both your legs (soup line / double scoop) and locked his hands together.

## Do this, in order

1. **Straighten your body out.** Staying curled/bent = wrists out of reach. Straightening exposes his wrists to your thumbs.
2. Shimmy — same motion as taking off tight pants.
3. Work **one knee through** the gap. That's the tipping point — position is broken the moment it happens.
4. Bring the whole foot through too.
5. Post that foot on his body, angle off, work back to guard.

## Remember

Straighten first. You can't reach his wrists curled up — that's the whole unlock.
