# Inside Leg Retention Method — Quick Reference

## When to use this

Outside leg control is too strong to high-leg over → use inside leg instead.

## Do this, in order

1. Thumb post on him (even if he has a hand on your knee).
2. Bring **inside leg** in, hook your shoelaces onto his lead thigh.
3. Pull your body **across the centerline**, presenting the opposite side to him.
4. He has to re-figure his whole pass from scratch — use that window to connect to his legs and go offense.

## Why it works

He's calculating everything based on the side he thinks he's passing toward. Switch him to the other side = every calculation he made is now wrong. Like a boxer switching orthodox↔southpaw mid-fight.

## Drill both directions

Works the same regardless of which side he initially commits to.
