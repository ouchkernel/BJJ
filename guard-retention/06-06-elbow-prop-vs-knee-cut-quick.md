# Elbow Prop Vs Knee Cut — Quick Reference

## Do this, in order

1. Get a shallow **knee shield** into the hip pocket as he comes in.
2. Control his reaching hand/wrist (or hook inside his elbow) — denies the underhook, easy to win since there's no gi to grip.
3. As he presses forward, let the shield **redirect him out to the side**.
4. Come up to an **elbow prop** off that redirection.
5. Retract feet toward your own butt, scoot away, create space.
6. Face him, guard reset, ready to go again.

## If he's already fighting for the underhook

- Constantly re-attach your hand to his wrist/elbow — don't let one grip loss end the fight.
- Double up: second hand inside his elbow means he has to beat two hands, not one, to wrist-pass you.

## Key point

Knee shield alone redirects; **elbow prop is the actual recovery mechanism** that turns "redirected" into "guard reset."
