# Countering Head Control — Elbow Post — Quick Reference

## The situation

Opponent already beat conditions 1-4 (contact, distance/angle, cleared your legs, level change). This is the last battle — head/shoulder control. Win it, everything resets.

## Do this, in order

1. Cross-frame: thumb post inside opponent's elbow (opposite-side arm).
2. As their hand works toward your head, switch — other hand takes over the elbow control.
3. Withdraw the first hand, reach past where they wanted to go, come up to your own elbow on the far side.
4. Your back is exposed but so is theirs — neutral trade, not a loss.
5. Bring both feet back to yourself, scoot your hips.
6. Reset into guard, ready to attack.

## The key mechanic

Never leave the opponent's reaching arm unmonitored. Switch hands actively as they maneuver — one hand always has to be tracking that arm.

## Remember

This is the 5th and final condition of the guard-pass sequence. Losing conditions 1-4 doesn't end the round. Losing this one does. Prioritize training this specific defense.
