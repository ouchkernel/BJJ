# Inside Foot Or Knee Guard Retention Method — Quick Reference

## The #1 rule

Get your limbs **inside** his limbs. Whoever's inside wins the position fight. This is the method the instructor prefers above high leg and inside leg.

## Do this, in order

1. Opponent approaches, works to an angle — maintain stance: elbows outside knees, knees to chest, one side presented.
2. He starts level-changing down → **elbow inside his elbow** on the near side (switch from thumb post to full elbow control).
3. At the same time → **knee inside his shoulder** on the far/outside side.
4. Hold both connections. He can't pass without breaking them first.
5. If he does send a leg through, you still have a knee shield — reset, don't panic.

## Foot vs. knee

- He's standing / far away → use **foot** (inside his shoulder).
- He's level-changed / close → use **knee** (default — most real passing ends up close range anyway).

## Why this beats high leg / inside leg

Those methods commit one leg to one direction — a good passer times a side-switch and beats you. Inside elbow + inside knee occupies **both** sides at once, so switching direction doesn't work against it.

## Checklist

- [ ] Elbow inside his elbow (near side)
- [ ] Knee inside his shoulder (far side)
- [ ] Stance intact — elbows outside knees, knees to chest
- [ ] Never fully extended

## Don't

Extend out of stance. The moment you extend, side-to-side switching works on you again — inside position alone doesn't save you if your base breaks down.

## Result when done right

He can't put weight on you, can't reach your head, can't body-lock, can't tight-waist. Only way he passes: physically disengage and restart from zero.
