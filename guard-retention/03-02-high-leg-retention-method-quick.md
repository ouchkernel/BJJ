# High Leg Retention Method — Quick Reference

## Do this, in order

1. Opponent clears your legs, starts working to an angle → **thumb post inside his lead elbow** immediately.
2. Bring your **outside leg high** over his body, sole of foot to his shoulder.
3. Use that foot contact to **drag your body around**, squaring back up to him.
4. Bring legs back in and **make contact with his legs** — don't stop at just "neutral."
5. He's now reacting to you — go on offense.

## Why the thumb post works

Thumb inside his lead elbow = he can't reach your head or your leg without going through it first. Same idea as standing collar-tie control.

## Don't

Stop once you're re-squared with no leg contact. If you sit in "neutral," he can just throw your base to the other side and restart his pass. Always finish by connecting to his legs.

## Note

Thumb post is temporary — it buys you the time to do the leg movement, not a permanent hold. Drill both sides; you can't predict which way he'll pass.
