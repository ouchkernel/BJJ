# Sit-Up Recovery — Quick Reference

## The problem

Smart opponents stop touching your legs (so you can't frame them) and circle to north-south at a distance instead.

## Do this

1. As they circle at a distance, sit up **into the space they just vacated** — not toward where they currently are.
2. Quickly put your back down, push off the floor.
3. You're back in front of them, in guard.

## Why not just turn to face them

Turning toward a flanking opponent = front headlock + they spin behind you + take the back. Classic mistake. Go to where they came from, never where they are.

## Two-mode defense (combine with leg-framing lesson)

| Opponent behavior | Response |
|---|---|
| Makes contact with your legs | Frame the lead leg, stick to them |
| Stays at a distance, no contact | Sit-up into vacated space |

Goal either way: re-connect to their legs and get back to guard retention work.
