# The 3 Guard Passing Methodologies — Quick Reference

Every pass targets one of exactly 3 areas. Identify which one, apply that category's toolkit.

1. **Lower legs (knee-to-ankle)** — e.g. Toreando-style grip switches to knee/ankle. Biggest focus of this course.
2. **Hips** — tight waist, body lock. **Knee cuts count here too** — the actual stopping mechanism is inside-hip control, not the knee itself.
3. **Head and shoulders** — opponent over one leg, controls head/shoulders. Standard half guard passing.

## Why it matters

3 categories, not dozens of named passes to memorize individually. Master all 3 = master guard retention as a whole.
