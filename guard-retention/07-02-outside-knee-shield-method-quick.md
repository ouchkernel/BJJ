# Outside Knee Shield Method — Quick Reference

## When to use this

Half guard, opponent chest-to-chest with top head/arm control, and you **can't** get the inside knee through (too tight). Use the outside knee instead.

## Do this, in order

1. Try to invert the inside knee first (previous lesson). Can't get it through? Switch to outside knee.
2. Outside knee, angled **slightly downward**, shallow into his hip — kneecap only, not foot-deep.
3. Feet stacked one over the other (not spread apart) — you should feel real tension in his hip.
4. Once shield is engaged, his cross-face pressure stops working.
5. Single hand inside his shoulder.
6. Come up to an **elbow prop**.
7. Use the shield to extract your bottom foot → place it on his hip.
8. Shrimp out. Guard restored.

## Two things that determine if the shield works

- [ ] Angled slightly **down**, not up (up = slips out)
- [ ] Feet stacked, not spread apart (spread = no tension = weak shield)

## Can't get the knee in?

Use a small kipping motion (quick hip taps) to tip the knee into place instead of forcing it.
