# Knee Cut: Knee Shield Method — Quick Reference

## The danger zone

No-gi knee cut = opponent wants underhook (one side) + knee in **the pocket of your hip** (other side). That diagonal combo pins you. If he lands it, he wins ~9/10.

## Gi vs no-gi (know the difference)

- **Gi:** opponent goes long (knee far out), uses collar/sleeve or tricep grip to pin your shoulder so you can't turn and elbow-escape. Long + jacket grip = dangerous.
- **No-gi:** opponent can't pin your sleeve, so going long just lets you elbow-escape for free. He has to stay tight — underhook + knee in your hip pocket.

## Do this (no-gi knee cut defense)

1. See the cue: his knee punching between your knees toward your armpit.
2. Immediately shield: put your knee across, shallow, right in your own hip pocket — before his knee lands there.
3. Frame a hand/arm in close to slow his head control.
4. As he drives forward, **misdirect him out to the side** with the shield — don't try to stop him dead.
5. Space opens — plant your bottom foot on his hip (opposite side).
6. Turn off that foot, rotate all the way out, back to guard.

## Don't

- Let his knee land in the hip pocket with an opposing underhook already in place — that's a near-automatic pass.
- Assume the gi and no-gi versions play out the same way — the danger zone and the passer's tools are different.
