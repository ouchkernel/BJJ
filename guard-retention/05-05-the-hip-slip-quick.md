# The Hip Slip — Quick Reference

## The exploit

Most body lockers grip long-arm/short-arm, not evenly. Short-arm wrist is exposed right in front of you. Long-arm wrist is out of reach — don't bother with it.

## Do this, in order

1. Identify the short arm.
2. Grab that wrist.
3. Thumb-post the **opposite shoulder** (where shoulder meets bicep) with your other hand.
4. Shimmy your hips sideways — like taking off tight pants.
5. His lock slides from your **waist** (bad, controls hips) down to your **legs** (fine, doesn't control hips).
6. Collect his arm/elbow, shift your body to a good angle.
7. Throw a leg over → omoplata setup. He has to deal with that before passing.

## If he holds on to your legs

Push his head to a side → throw the leg → omoplata.

## If he unlocks his hands to jump over your legs

Good news: the second his hands unlock, **every normal guard retention tool works again** — get forearms inside his elbow/neck, bring legs back, rebuild guard. Locked hands were the only special thing about the body lock.

## Remember

Wrist + shoulder control, then shimmy. That's the whole move.
