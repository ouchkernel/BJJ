# Knee Lever Guard Retention — Quick Reference

## Prerequisite

Opponent's knee must be **on the mat** for the standard version. Not on the mat? Use the double knee lever variation below first.

## Standard knee lever — do this, in order

1. Foot up to his knee, feet crossed at the **toes** (not ankles).
2. Pinch both knees around his hip.
3. Lift your butt slightly off the floor.
4. **Crimp his elbow to his side** as your hips lift — kills his arm base. (His other hand is usually pinned under your head already.)
5. Point knees in the lever direction, hit it **sharp, like a punch** — not a slow push.
6. He bases/steps or tips over — either way, he's destabilized.
7. Thumb to his armpit, knee up to your chest → knee in front of his hip.
8. Hips outside his knee, lock closed guard.

## Double knee lever (his knee NOT on the mat)

1. Pinch legs around his **thigh**.
2. Lever to force his knee down to the mat.
3. Stand your feet on top of his calf — locks the knee down.
4. Hit the same lever as he tries to climb back up.

## The detail that makes it work

Crimping the elbow to his side = no arm base = the lever actually off-balances him instead of him just posting through it.
