# The 3 Danger Zones — Quick Reference

When any of these 3 triggers happen, switch to **100% defense, zero offense**, until retention is re-established.

## The 3 triggers (increasing severity)

1. **Hip line passed** ("J-point") — no leg contact left. → Response: throw up upper body frames first, then work on realignment.
2. **Shoulder line passed** — heading toward North-South. → Response: frame + ride the roll back to guard.
3. **Chest-to-chest + underhook, past your knee** (worst case) — near-side, far-side, or double underhooks. → Response: pure escape mode. No offense at all.

## Why it matters

- Removes the guesswork of "attack or defend right now?" — the #1 source of beginner mistakes in guard.
- Only think offense again *after* retention is solid — not before, not during.
