# Near Side Underhook Retention — Quick Reference

## The threat

Opponent half guard top, near-side underhook, head jams your head into his shoulder/his underhook — head and shoulders fully pinned, hand walks your knees out.

## Golden rule

**Control his hip. Never his knee.** Pinching the knee = he pops it and spreads over the top. Pinching the hip = he's stuck.

## Do this, in order

1. Both hands on his lats, elbow jammed low to your side.
2. The instant his knee lifts off the floor: pinch your two knees around **his hip**.
3. If he puts a hand on your knee to push it down: peel it off (safe — your overhook shuts down his kimura).
4. Foot on his calf muscle, then pinch knees — this is your butterfly hook base, don't skip the foot-on-calf step.
5. Pommel your foot inside.
6. Check: knee should now be in **front** of his hip (not behind his butt cheek).
7. Hands on lats, lift, knee comes in front — guard restored.

## Don't

- Pinch around his knee. Ever.
- Stay hip-passive while his knee climbs.
- Let a hand sit on your knee uncontested.
- Try a lazy hook without the foot-on-calf step first.

## Cue to remember

Hip control, not knee control. That's the whole fight.
