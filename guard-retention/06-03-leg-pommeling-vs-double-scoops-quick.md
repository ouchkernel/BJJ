# Leg Pommeling Vs Double Scoops — Quick Reference

## Watch out for

- Hips on his centerline → he stacks you easily.
- Your leg riding up onto his shoulder → his hand crosses your centerline → he starts passing.

## Do this, in order

1. Get an **angle** — don't stay square/centerline to him.
2. Move legs off his shoulders/trapezius, **down to his biceps/elbows**. Keep spine straight (rounded spine = easy stack).
3. Turn hips **offline** as he tries to lift/move you.
4. **Pommel a foot inside** his bicep/shoulder — either leg.
5. Foot in = he can't lift that leg anymore.
6. Get both feet back to his hips → back in your normal guard game.

## The two things that beat the stack

- [ ] Angle off centerline
- [ ] Legs down to elbows/biceps + straight spine (= "heavy" legs)

## Priority #1

Don't let your leg get all the way up onto his shoulder — that's what lets his hand cross your centerline and start the pass.
