# Reverse Underhook Sit-Out — Quick Reference

## What it is

Same-side arm under same-side arm (not the standard opposite underhook). Strong shoulder control, weaker head control than crossface/reverse-crossface. Risk: back exposure if your leg comes free while he turns in.

## Do this, in order

1. Hand to his lower back (thumb in belt if gi; lower back only if no-gi — drawstring grip is illegal).
2. Trapped elbow + head: move away from his body.
3. Knee into his hip. Feet close together — not crossed, not triangled. Buys time, not a permanent hold.
4. Open trapped elbow, post up on it. **Shoulder off the mat = not passable, period** (pass needs hip + shoulder down 3 seconds). Legs are now safe to move freely.
5. Bring your knee up underneath you.
6. Grab the handle on his oblique, scoot around the corner, pull.
7. He pulls his own head back to protect his back → you're back in position, reset guard.

## Don't

Force your knee through the gap between your bodies — there's no space, you'll get stuck. Come up to a knee first, then reset.

## Cue to remember

Shoulder off the mat = pass clock reset to zero. Move your legs freely once you're up on that elbow.
