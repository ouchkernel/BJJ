# Knee Shield → Outside Shoulder Roll — Quick Reference

## The combo (3 tools you already know)

1. Knee shield — deny the hip pocket.
2. Knee prop — stable base to push from.
3. Outside shoulder roll — the actual recovery.

## Do this, in order

1. See his knee driving toward your hip pocket — that's the knee cut signal.
2. Knee shield goes into the pocket first, blocks the straight line in.
3. Use the shield to misdirect him off to an angle (scissor-sweep-like).
4. Hand underneath his armpit, cross-shoulder control.
5. Prop up onto your knee. **Critical: knee stays outside your ankle.**
6. Push off your foot, work feet back around, re-establish guard.

## The one detail that matters most

- Knee **outside** ankle when propping = stable, you can push back.
- Knee **inside** ankle = you get pushed over easily.

## Why this combo

Small toolkit, reused everywhere > memorizing a new counter for every pass. This is knee shield + knee prop + outside shoulder roll — three moves you already drilled, chained together for the knee cut specifically.
