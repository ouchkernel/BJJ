# Stance And Posture — Quick Reference

## 2 non-negotiable rules

1. Elbows close to knees.
2. Knees close to chest.

## Checklist

- [ ] Feet asymmetrical (different heights/distances) — never present two matched, grabbable feet
- [ ] Soles pointed at the opponent, not straight down
- [ ] Elbows **outside** your knees, not inside
- [ ] Body angled ~45° to the opponent (not squared-on) so you always know which side he must commit to passing
- [ ] Spine rounded (rocking-chair/banana shape), never straight

## Why

- Elbow/knee gaps = the exact space he penetrates with a knee or his chest.
- Symmetrical feet = easy double-leg grab. Asymmetrical = he only ever gets one.
- Squared-on stance = he can pass either side, you don't know which. Angled stance = you know exactly which side to load defenses onto.
- Straight spine = more mat friction, less mobility. Rounded spine = less friction, faster movement.
