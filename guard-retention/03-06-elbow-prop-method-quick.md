# Elbow Prop Method — Quick Reference

## The rule this uses

A pass legally needs hip **and** shoulder pinned for 3 seconds. Get either off the mat, no pass. This lesson = the shoulder side.

## Do this, in order

1. He levels down → switch to a **cross frame**: forearm across his collarbones, thumb inside his elbow (denies head control).
2. Free hand props you up onto your **elbow** — shoulder off the mat.
3. No pass possible now regardless of what else happens.
4. Finish with either:
   - **Scoot** — feet back to your buttocks, scoot away, guard re-established. (Simpler.)
   - **Shoulder roll** — hand underneath, prop, roll through as he posts in. (Use vs. strong/pressuring opponents where scooting is hard.)

## Pick your finish

| Opponent is... | Use |
|---|---|
| Normal pressure | Scoot |
| Strong, pushing hard into you | Shoulder roll (leg strength beats his weight) |
