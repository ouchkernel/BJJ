# Double Scoops vs Unlocked Hands — Quick Reference

## Situation

Opponent is underneath your legs but NOT locked hands — one arm wrapped around a leg, other hand gripping the calf. (Skilled opponents prefer this over locking hands.)

## Do this, in order

1. Target the **elbow**, not the wrist (different from the locked-hands version).
2. Lift hips off the floor.
3. Heel of your hand goes **behind his elbow joint**.
4. As he pushes in, swing that leg like a **pendulum** — rolls his elbow over, brings you up to a hand.
5. His back is now exposed.
6. Scoot (feet to buttocks, standard scoot motion) back to open guard.

## Second leg version

Straighten + lift hips to expose elbow → pendulum the second leg to push him away → create distance as he turns to face you → you're in a strong holding position.

## Rule of thumb

Locked hands → attack wrist. Unlocked hands (calf grip) → attack elbow.
