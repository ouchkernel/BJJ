# Inside Position Leg Scissor — Quick Reference

## Two-hand frame (last line of defense)

1. Hand on your own forehead (block-a-punch position).
2. Other hand on your own chest (salute-the-flag position).

Why: forehead hand means they only ever grab your forearm, never your head. Chest hand becomes your underhook the moment they pin.

## Then escape via leg scissor

1. Bring hands/elbows back to yourself.
2. Grab the opponent's leg.
3. Turn onto your side, scissor your legs — go from supine (chest up) to prone (chest down).
4. From prone: guard is not passed.

## From prone, pick one

- [ ] Sit back to guard
- [ ] Grab the opponent's legs directly
- [ ] Stand up and back away

## Don't

- Don't put your hands anywhere except forehead + chest — the temple must be protected.
- Don't try to hold the two-hand frame indefinitely — opponent can step over into mount. Transition out via the scissor.

## Timing note

Opponent needs 3 seconds of pin to score the pass. 3 seconds is long in combat sports — this frame only needs to survive that window before you scissor out.
