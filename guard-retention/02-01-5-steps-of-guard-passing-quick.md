# The 5 Steps Of Guard Passing — Quick Reference

## The rule

Every guard pass needs all 5 of these. Deny **any one** and the pass cannot complete.

1. **Contact** — opponent must physically touch you to pass; no contact = you just follow him.
2. **Distance + angle (together)** — neither alone is dangerous. Only closed distance + an angle at the same time threatens you.
3. **Clear your legs** — any leg contact you still have on him means no pass yet.
4. **Level change** — knee (conservative) or chest-to-chest (more committed, more dangerous).
5. **Final pin** — hip **and** shoulder held simultaneously for 3 seconds = official pass.
   - Side passes usually finish pinning **head/shoulders**.
   - North-South passes usually finish pinning **hips**.

## How to use it live

- Ask: "which of the 5 does he NOT have yet?" — fight to deny exactly that one.
- More of the 5 he's accumulated = more danger. 1-2 = not close. 4 = very close.
- Order varies by pass type (e.g. half guard often pins *before* clearing your legs) — but all 5 are always required, in some order.
