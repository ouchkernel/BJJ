# 3 Safety Positions Against A Body Lock — Quick Reference

## The 3 positions (any one = zero body-lock risk)

1. **Knee shield** — knee in front of opponent's shoulder. Either knee, either guard (full or half). Top knee preferred, bottom knee works too. Most repeatable option.
2. **Closed guard** — fully stops any body lock, forces opponent to open guard and restart their whole pass from zero.
3. **Omoplata** — only needs a small angle to set up. Zero body-lock risk the instant it's on; they must escape the submission threat first.

## When to use these

- You're fatigued and need a reset without relying on fast hand-fighting.
- Facing a body-lock specialist — fall back here between active negation attempts.

## Combine with active negation

Cycle: hand control / thumb posts / feet-on-hips / cross-shoulder / side-neck (active tools) ↔ knee shield / closed guard / omoplata (static safe positions). Denies all 3 body-lock prerequisites (head below shoulders, connected hands, elbows around hips) and gets you the inside-knee position that kills it outright.
