# Inside Knee/Elbow Escape (Half Guard, Top Head & Arm) — Quick Reference

## When to use this

Opponent has half guard, underhook on far side, cross-face on near side, hands locked ("top head and arm"). You can only pull with your legs right now — that's the problem.

## Do this, in order

1. Elbow inside his hip (hand on hip also works — elbow inside his knee is the key part).
2. Other hand on his oblique muscles.
3. Turn onto your **side**. Don't stay flat on your back.
4. **Don't lock a leg triangle** — kills your mobility.
5. Hook one foot behind his knee, other foot on floor, knees pinch around his hip.
6. Shimmy onto your side fully.
7. **Toes point DOWN to the floor** (inverts your knee). Toes up = you will never get your knee through.
8. Knee lands in the pocket of his hip.
9. Push, push, push — works even if he's still gripping.
10. Shrimp, shrimp, shrimp until your hips clear outside his knee.
11. Re-lock closed guard.

## The one detail that makes or breaks this

Toes down = knee inverts = knee gets through to his hip.
Toes up = dead end, can't get there.

## Don't

Lock a triangle with your legs — feels safe, actually traps you and prevents the shrimping/inverting this escape needs.
