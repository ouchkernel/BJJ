# Half Butterfly Retention Method — Quick Reference

## Trigger

Opponent chest-to-chest in half guard, top head-and-arm, **lifts his knee off the mat**.

## Do this, in order

1. Top leg goes on top of his quad / outside his thigh immediately — don't let his knee get higher than your hips.
2. Hug your knee to his hip when he tries to rise, so your upper leg stays on top of his.
3. Move your hips out to the side, pommel your foot in — **never break leg contact while doing this** (pinch + back-heel the whole time).
4. Check knee direction: pointing the same way as his intended pass? → swivel hips underneath him so your knee (and hook) point **against** his pass direction.
5. Use the resulting sticky hook to move his hips away from his pass side, bring your other knee in.
6. Back in guard.

## The 2 traps

| Trap | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Passive leg when his knee lifts | He gets knee-height advantage, drives it either way to pass |
| Pommeling the hook without keeping contact | He backsteps and kicks free — the "retention" move creates the pass |

## Don't

- Let go of leg contact mid-transition to half butterfly.
- Leave your knee pointing the same direction as his pass — you won't be able to follow when he kicks out.

## Cue to remember

Knee points **against** his pass direction = sticky hook that follows him. Knee points the same way = he escapes clean.
