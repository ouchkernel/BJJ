# Wrist Control Vs Double Scoops — Quick Reference

## The danger

Opponent wants a hand across your centerline to control your hip or shoulder — this significantly boosts his passing potential. Deny it.

## Do this, in order

1. Legs down toward his **elbows** first (same idea as leg pommeling).
2. Immediately follow with **hand/wrist control** — grab whichever grip feels natural.
3. Even if his leg is already up on his shoulder, wrist control still stops the hand from crossing your centerline.
4. Work hips out to the side.
5. **Palm-laying action**: use your grip on his hands to drag your legs/body back to a winning position, return to guard.
6. Keep the wrist control locked in — it also blocks him from switching grips.

## Two-part rule

Legs to elbows + hand control = the combo. Neither alone is the full answer.
