# Double Underhooks Retention — Quick Reference

## Reality check

Upper body battle is 100% lost with double underhooks. Not coming back. Win with legs/hips instead.

## Do this, in order

1. Foot on top of his calf muscle (controls lower leg).
2. Pinch knees at his **hip** — not his knee, not a tight triangle, not crossed ankles, not a scorpion.
3. Firm grip only — don't lock max-tight, you'll gas out.
4. Work your foot inside. Target distance is tiny: knee moves from behind his butt cheek to inside his knee (about one knee-width).
5. Both hands on his lats (still reachable even with double underhooks locked).
6. Wrestle that small distance, slip knee back inside.
7. Once one knee's inside → move him freely, double underhooks stop mattering.
8. Butterfly hook the other foot in, get both knees in front of his hips, push/break his grips.

## Don't

- Fight his knee. Fight his hip.
- Lock a tight figure-four/triangle/scorpion — doesn't help, and a freed scorpion foot actively helps his pass.
- Pinch knees without also controlling his lower leg with your foot — he just moves the lower leg free.
- Try to move him far. It's the width of your knee, not a big shift.

## Cue to remember

Foot on calf + knee pinch at hip = the whole system. Small movements, not a big escape.
