# Turning Away From The Pass — Quick Reference

## Default rule

Turn **toward** your opponent. Never turn your back — that's the standard for a reason.

## Exception: when to turn away instead

He's pressuring forward hard enough that turning in gets you flattened. Go with his force, not against it.

## Risk decision first

- Short points match, can't afford to concede pass points → worth the back-exposure risk.
- Submission-only / no-points → **not** worth it. Just get passed, escape the pin, return to guard.

## If you do turn away — do this, in order

1. Cross frame — forearm across his collarbones.
2. Elbow prop away from him.
3. Put your **back on the ground** — fastest way to kill back exposure and create distance.
4. Roll into the gap you just created, back to guard.

## Pattern to remember

Frame → gain height → create space (back down or walk away) → push into that space → guard.

## Preference order

1. Shoulder roll into him (preferred, from earlier lessons).
2. Turn away (only when #1 isn't realistic).
