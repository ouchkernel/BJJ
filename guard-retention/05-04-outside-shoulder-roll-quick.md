# Outside Shoulder Roll — Quick Reference

## Situation

Opponent has a body lock AND has already passed your legs. No leg connection left. Can't move hips (body lock blocks the usual pin-escape option).

## The key idea

Body lock controls hips. To actually pin you, opponent must eventually release the hips and shift to head/shoulder control ("cranial shift"). If he never shifts, he can't finish the pass. Exploit that.

## Do this, in order

1. **Thumb-post inside his elbow** immediately — this lets you gate when/if he can release the body lock.
2. Come up to your base, get your shoulder off the floor.
3. **Knee prop**: knee inside, propped on his hip. Hip off floor = pass can't finish (needs shoulder AND hip pinned together).
4. When he reaches for your head to force your hip back down → slide your free hand under his armpit.
5. **Shoulder roll**: push off, turn the corner, land back in guard, knee in front of his shoulder.

## Why it works

Pass finish needs shoulder + hip pinned at the same time. Elbow control blocks his cranial shift; knee prop blocks hip pin. He can never get both.

## End position

Knee in front of shoulder — guard re-established, holding him off.
