# Guard Retention Against Distance Passing (Toreando) — Quick Reference

## Do this on approach

1. Point sole of foot at his sternum/centerline (not down at the mat).
2. Feet **asymmetrical** — one up, one down. Toes pointing different directions.
3. On contact: pommel your foot **inside and over his wrist** — top foot position, not top hand.
4. Present **one side** of your body to him — don't stay square.
5. Elbows and knees tight together the whole time.

## Why

- Top hand position (his hand on your foot) = he can push your foot down and throw your legs anywhere. Top foot position (your foot on his wrist) = he can't.
- If you're square, he can fake and pass either side. If you present one side, the far side is too far for him to reach in time — you always know which side he's committing to.

## Checklist before he gets close

- [ ] Sole of foot toward his sternum, not the floor
- [ ] Feet asymmetrical, toes split in different directions
- [ ] Foot inside/over his wrist on contact (not his hand on your foot)
- [ ] One side of your body presented, not square
- [ ] Elbows + knees tight together

## If he switches sides

Re-present the other side of your body — it takes him long enough to run around that you can beat him there.
