# No-Gi Guard Retention — Introduction — Quick Reference

## Why this matters

- Pin escapes + guard retention = the two most foundational skills in jiu-jitsu. Everything else needs you to be able to move; passing/pinning is what takes that away.
- Bottom position needs 2 resources: **space** and **time**. Retention is what gets you both.

## Don't do this

Don't let bottom position become a race between "submit them" vs "they pass me" — that's a coin flip, not a skill. Fix: prioritize retention over rushing offense.
