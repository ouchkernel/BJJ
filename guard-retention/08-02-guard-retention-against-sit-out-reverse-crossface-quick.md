# Sit-Out Reverse Crossface — Quick Reference

## What it is

Arm goes over your head, opponent sits out, pulls with full lat strength — locks your head, can't move it back. Stronger no-gi grip than a plain crossface.

## Do this, in order

1. Backhand frame: back of your free hand on his chest near his armpit, fingers poking through the gap.
2. Keep elbow **high** — don't let him sit/lean on it (kills the frame).
3. Knee into his hip, kip both feet, and tuck chin to chest at the same time.
4. Grip slips off.
5. Immediately forearm in front of his elbow — inside position, so he can't re-grab.
6. He comes back up to his knees → foot to hip, knee shimmies in front, guard restored.

## Cue to remember

Chin to chest + kip = taking off a tight sweater. That's the motion.

## Don't

- Let your elbow drop — he'll pin it and the frame is gone.
- Muscle through it — the kip + chin tuck does the work, not strength.
- Skip re-establishing inside position after the slip — he'll just crossface you again.
