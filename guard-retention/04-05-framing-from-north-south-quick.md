# Framing From North-South — Quick Reference

## Golden rule

**Never frame the body from north-south.** Reaching for the torso pulls your elbow off your knee — that's how they pass. Frame the **elbows** instead.

## Why

Opponent completes the pass by getting their elbows inside yours + head below your hip line. Attack the thing they need (elbows), not the torso.

## 3 methods

1. **Post both elbows** — stalling grip. Blocks their level change, doesn't reset guard by itself. Keep your own elbows/knees tight while doing it. Their frustration → their movement → your window to reconnect legs.
2. **Scoop outside + inside knee post** — one hand scoops outside their arm, other posts inside their knee. Opens counters.
3. **Elbows inside knees** (vs. a level-changing opponent) — note this flips your normal posture (elbows outside knees when they're in front of you → elbows inside knees when they're north-south). They run into your connection when they drop weight; spin them back to guard from here.

## Pattern to remember

Control elbows → they can't get the head down → delay forces them to move → their movement = your chance to reconnect to the legs and flip back to offense.
