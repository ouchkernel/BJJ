# Straitjacket System — Introduction — Quick Reference

## What it is

Legs pin the opponent's arms → opponent becomes relatively helpless → neck opens up for the rear naked strangle.

## Why it's the centerpiece

1. Most well-known, most publicized part of the whole back attack system.
2. By far the #1 method of finishing from the back in competition — not statistically close versus the auxiliary systems.
3. Overwhelming majority of finishes have come from SJS specifically.

## Track record

- EBI-style overtime: choice between armbar-position start or back-position start — SJS-trained athletes exclusively chose back position.
- Result: extraordinarily high success rate vs. the field, which is what put the SJS on the map.
