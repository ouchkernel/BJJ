# Cross Body Ride — Transitions — Quick Reference

## Twister (torso/spine attack)

1. Punch wrist-deep hand through, transfer behind opponent's neck (arm trapped underneath yours).
2. Elbow to floor, go forward, get an arm under the head around the neck.
3. Pick a version:

| Bottom-arm dominant | Top-arm dominant (preferred) |
|---|---|
| Bottom arm grips head, draws in | Come forward onto chest first |
| Near shoulder stays free — opponent can wiggle/push out | Control top arm → score bottom arm → figure-4 → head underneath |
| Still submits, but leaves an escape route | Near shoulder pinned, slippage eliminated, much stronger finish |

4. Alternate entry to top-arm version: punch through, sit over opponent's body, use bottom hand to *feed* the top arm around instead of locking bottom-arm first.
5. Crowd the near-side shoulder before locking — any fade here lets the head move and the arm slip.

## Strangle transition — when opponent locks hands defensively

1. Withdraw hand from crook of elbow → pass elbow-deep under the neck.
2. Elevate with your knees, staying **behind** their elbow (elbow-to-floor between you = problem).
3. Bring their elbow onto your sternum as your arm goes elbow-deep around the neck.
4. Secure a 1-on-1 grip on the near arm (no need to lock hands) — restores diagonal control.
5. Swap outside scorpion → crab hook to get the angle.
6. Lift, shift hips out, post foot on opponent's hip, second leg up (lock figure-4, or post + high foot if opponent's waist is too big).
7. Go directly into the rear naked strangle.

## Alternate full sequence

1. Standard Cross Body Ride entry (no strangle threat yet — both your arms are under theirs).
2. Opponent locks hands defensively → control elbow, tilt elbow onto your sternum.
3. Arm goes under the head, bends 90° — chest blocks the elbow from coming back between you.
4. Shoot other hand to a 1-on-1 wrist grip → over the shoulder.
5. Hook the leg, shimmy hips around the corner, foot over the hip.
6. Figure-4 if leg length allows, else post rear mount.
7. Rear naked strangle.

## The big picture

Legs denied → hammer lock. Hammer lock denied → twister or strangle. Every defensive reaction opens the next attack — that's the whole point of the auxiliary system.
