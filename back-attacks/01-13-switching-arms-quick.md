# Switching Arms — Quick Reference

## When to use this

You sense the opponent is about to beat your head — the single most dangerous step in his escape sequence (game over if it happens).

## Steps

1. Punch the strangle arm out and underneath the opponent's arm; grip your own wrist. Now both arms are control arms (underneath both of his).
2. As he commits to his escape, release one control hand, lift the arm through, and pull — use his own momentum to help you.
3. Swing around to the opposite side of his body.
4. Reset into a standard, well-set attacking position on the new side.

## Variant

Cross-grip his far wrist instead of punching purely underneath — same goal, same result: preserve the head trap and reposition.

## Trade-off to accept

- No strangle threat while both arms are underneath — that's fine. Staying on the back beats losing the position while defending a doomed strangle.

## Core principle

Proactively reassign the strangle arm before the opponent drags it out of position for you.
