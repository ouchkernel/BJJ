# Introduction — Quick Reference

## The point

This volume shifts the course from finishing mechanics (main + auxiliary systems, already covered) to entries — how to actually establish hooks and rear mount against a resisting opponent.

## Why it exists

1. Lots of athletes are good at getting to the back and holding it.
2. Very few can finish reliably once they're there — that's the problem the whole series was built to fix.
3. An instructional on the back would be incomplete without also covering how to get there, so this volume closes that gap.

## What's ahead in Volume 7

1. Stances/postures for working against turtle + the 3 control focus points (hips, head/shoulders, wrist).
2. Foot-entry vs. knee-entry theory for inserting hooks.
3. Concrete near-side entry techniques (basic, resistance responses, diagonal control, tight-waist breakdown variations).
