# Back Crucifix — Strangles — Quick Reference

## What it is

- Spine perpendicular to opponent's (not aligned, like conventional back control).
- Constant that carries over: chest-to-back contact + uncontested strangle hand (both arms pinned — one by legs, one by hand).
- Opponent breaks spinal alignment to escape conventional back control? Accept it and switch into Back Crucifix instead of fighting to reset.

## Head-trap rule (same as main system)

Strangle arm and head must be on **opposite** sides. Same side = no strangle, easy escape.

## Entry 1 — turtle breakdown

1. Off-balance: hand wrist/elbow-deep, pull until opponent bases on an elbow (collapse to shoulder = free pin instead).
2. Break down with tight waist grip; post a fist on the floor to slow their recovery.
3. Catch/post the arm, knees off the floor, penetrate with a knee.
4. Sweep foot around the corner, hook the arm.
5. Hunt and catch the second arm as they try to recover.

## Entry 2 — sit-through / seatbelt (open turtle / wide base)

1. Seatbelt grip.
2. Knee inside opponent's tricep.
3. Level-change, drive knee in, sit on their forearm.
4. Pull over, capture the arm.

**Pick by elbow position:** elbows tucked in → Entry 1 (off-balance). Elbows/base opened wide → Entry 2 (penetrate with knee).

## One-handed rotational strangle

1. **Flattened fist through the jaw**: thumb knuckle leads (thinnest part first, like a knife edge) → finger-walk across chest → wrist enters → elbow follows → hand around the corner, chin over your wrist.
2. **Close the circle**: hand all the way behind, chin forward over the wrist. Open circle (gap) = weak strangle + built-in escape.
3. **Finish**: rotate elbow directly over the opponent's shoulder.

### The #1 fix

- Lead with the **elbow** into the opponent's chest/breastbone — NOT the wrist across the front. Elbow touches first, hand follows to full extension.
- Head must move forward (twice: penetration, then seating the finish). Static head = arm won't penetrate.

## Supine vs. prone

| Supine | Prone |
|---|---|
| Sit to butt, pull opponent back, catch far arm | Control knuckle line at neck height |
| Knee wedges opponent's far armpit | Free hand posts on mat for stability |
| Control near arm at **knuckle line**, not wrist | Snake hand through/over when opportunity appears |
| Rotate elbow over shoulder | Head forward, raise elbow to strangle |

## Drill progression

1. Turtle only (no crucifix) — single hand through, elbow to chest, not wrist-across.
2. Add trapped arm + live resistance, keep head moving.
3. Self-test: can you get your wrist all the way over the trapezius? If not, elbow isn't touching correctly.
