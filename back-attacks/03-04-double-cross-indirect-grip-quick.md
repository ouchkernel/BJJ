# Double Cross / Indirect Grip — Quick Reference

## Setup

1. You already have single cross grip.
2. He anticipates your strangle hand and pre-blocks it with his remaining defensive hand — smart move on his part.
3. Exploit it: feed him your strangle hand, fake the strangle.
   - No reaction → finish the strangle right there.
   - He reacts → he takes a direct grip on your strangle hand.

## Why this is still good for you

- He has direct grip on your strangle hand (indirect for you) — doesn't matter.
- You have **direct cross grip on his secondary hand** — the one that actually matters, the one your leg is hunting.

## Finish

1. Foot over top (prior perception), secondary hand behind his back.
2. He's down to one functioning defensive limb; you have two.
3. Fake strangle again to get him committing/pushing.
4. Seize knuckle line, strip his remaining grip.
5. Result: uncontested strangle hand.

## Freeing your hand from his grip

Don't pull against it (needs strength). Immobilize the hand, turn your wrist through the weak point of his thumb — it comes free easily.

## Remember

Direct > indirect is a preference, not a rule. What matters is which hand gets the direct grip — make it the one your leg needs to trap, not your strangle hand.
