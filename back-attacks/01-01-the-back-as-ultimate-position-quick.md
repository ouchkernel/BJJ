# The Back As Ultimate Position — Quick Reference

## The core claim

Back position > mount, knee-on-belly, side control, north-south — in submission grappling specifically.

## Why

1. **Body asymmetry** — humans defend the front well, the back poorly. Back position = the ultimate flanking angle: maximum attack, minimum counterattack.
2. **Primacy of strangles** — joint attacks can be fought through by a tough/courageous opponent surrendering the joint; a fully locked strangle cannot be resisted, period.
3. Combine best angle + most certain finish → back is the #1 position for submission grappling (MMA context is more debatable).

## The problem this course fixes

- Most athletes: great at getting to and holding the back, weak at finishing from it.
- Points-based competition removes the incentive to finish once back is secured.
- Submission-only formats (EBI overtime, etc.) exposed this gap — holding back position stopped being enough.

## The system's shape

- Centerpiece finish: rear naked strangle (RNC) — highest-percentage back finish, full stop.
- **Main system**: hand-fighting + leg-trapping technique that sets up the RNC.
- **Auxiliary subsystems**: backup attacks spread across the opponent's whole body for when the main system alone can't break through.
