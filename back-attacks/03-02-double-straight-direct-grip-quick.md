# Double Straight Direct Grip — Quick Reference

## Priority order

1. Control opponent's **primary defensive hand** at the knuckle line first (never grip fingers — illegal).
2. Drive his **secondary hand's wrist below the pectoral/chest line** — this is the critical demarcation line.

## Double straight indirect (bottom of hierarchy)

1. You have top-hand dominance on his primary hand; he has top-hand dominance on your strangle hand (indirect).
2. Lift control hand, use the indirect grip to push his wrist below his chest line.
3. Use prior perception: slap your foot to your own hand.
4. Thread foot through — shoelaces behind his back, touching his spine.
5. Result: uncontested strangle hand, only his chin left.

## Double straight direct (upgrade)

1. Rule: never fight into the strength of a grip — fight out of it. His grip is weak on the shoulder opposite where he's gripping.
2. Take a straight grip on the knuckle line, top-hand dominant, on that weak side = double straight direct.
3. Push secondary wrist below chest line.
4. Prior perception: foot slaps hand, threads through, shoelaces to spine.
5. Result: both arms pinned, ready to fight for the strangle.

## Watch for

- If his head starts to rise above yours: stop, wraparound-grip his head, pull it back into position.
- Direct beats indirect because you now decide where his hand goes, not him.
