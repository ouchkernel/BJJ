# General Reflections On The Back — Quick Reference

## The core relationship

1. Back position + rear naked strangle (mata leão) are nearly inseparable in no-gi/MMA — it's the overwhelming go-to finish once you're on the back.
2. Rare to see a rear naked strangle finished from anywhere other than rear mount.
3. SJS builds on this by using the legs to strip the opponent's defensive arms, making the back → strangle pipeline even stronger.
