# Near Side Entries 02 — Quick Reference

## Leg-assist entry vs. resistance

1. Drive weight over opponent's head to sag him down.
2. Watch for his **buttocks rising** — that's the tell he's fighting the breakdown.
3. Step your foot over and inside his ankle.
4. Pull his leg as you break him down — physically drags the hook into place, even if he scrambles back to base afterward (you follow and keep it).

## Diagonal control (stabilizing 1 hook)

- Rule: both sides of the body must always be controlled.
- 1 hook = only controls that side. The instant you have it, snatch a **one-on-one on the far side** with the free hand.
- Result: near-side hook + far-side arm = both sides covered, opponent can't escape to guard on the open side, you stay attached whichever way he moves.

## Formula for "both sides controlled"

| What you have | What you need |
|---|---|
| Only legs | 2 hooks |
| Only arms | Over-under (or equivalent 2-sided grip) |
| 1 hook | Opposite-side arm grip (diagonal control) |
