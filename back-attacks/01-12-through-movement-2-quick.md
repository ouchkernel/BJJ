# Through Movement 2 — Quick Reference

## When to use this

Opponent beat your bottom hook **and** the bottom knee (more advanced escape than the basic through-movement case).

## Steps

1. Track his head — move your own head in the same direction he's moving his (via the bottom leg pushing off the mat). Get your head to the mat/in front before his does.
2. Push up into a **tripod** (brief weight on head + feet, body lifted).
3. While elevated, lift and reinsert your knee on the far side of his arm.
4. Hook drags him back — knee re-established on both sides.
5. Re-lock: post rear mount or body triangle on the recovered side.

## Good news / bad news at this stage

- Good: head trap is often still intact (seat-belt grip keeps head + strangle arm split) — still a thread of strangle.
- Bad: can't "shovel" him back with the leg anymore — that option died with the knee.

## Key rule

The tripod is not a technique in itself — it's just the mechanism to recover knee position, which is the real thing you're always fighting to control.
