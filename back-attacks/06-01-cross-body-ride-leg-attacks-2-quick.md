# Cross Body Ride — Leg Attacks 2 — Quick Reference

## The setup, every time

1. Elevate opponent's hips — knees to chest, foot toward ceiling.
2. Leg attacks only start once hips are up and squared.
3. Diagonal control principle: 2 limbs restrain (e.g. right hook + left wrist/elbow grip), 2 limbs stay free to attack.

## Hip Breaker (2 ways in, same effect)

**Neck brace method:**
1. Catch foot at shoelaces, elbow-wedge for control.
2. Draw foot in, pinch against side of neck.
3. Both hands to knee.
4. Extend leg away + pull knee down (same direction opponent is already moving).
5. Target: hip flexor **tendon** (not muscle) — flexibility doesn't matter.

**Achilles lock method:**
1. Pass into achilles lock, lock figure-4.
2. Same extend-leg-away action → same hip flexor breaking pressure.

## Escalation: Inside/Inverted Heel Hook (no Ashi Garami needed)

1. Free arm wraps opponent's ankle.
2. Foot hand grips opponent's foot.
3. Hand-over-hand as opponent tries to extend.
4. Apply heel hook from the outside, rotate inward.
5. Works because there's too much hand in the way for opponent to straighten the leg.

## Alternate route to the heel hook

1. Catch foot at end of lever, elbow wedge outside opponent's leg.
2. Pass foot into neck / armpit / hand-to-hand pass-off.
3. Heel stays pinned on your chest — reinforce with second hand.
4. Turn in for the lock.

## Don't forget

- Near leg denied → go back to far leg. Far leg denied → go back to near leg.
- Opponent springs the foot free? Reset to the original control and restart. Position isn't lost.
