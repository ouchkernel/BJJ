# Neck Penetration System — Quick Reference

## The problem

Your wrist is wider than the gap a tucked chin leaves against the chest. Can't force it straight in.

## The metaphor

Knife: thin edge enters first, thick spine follows through the opening it made. Never lead with the thick part.

## Penetration order (thin to thick)

1. Thumb knuckle (thinnest part of your hand — the "blade edge").
2. Flattened fist (knuckles straightened, not a punching fist).
3. Extended flattened hand, walking through.
4. Full wrist.
5. Elbow follows immediately once wrist is through.

## Step by step

1. Thumb knuckle behind the ear, find the curve of the jaw.
2. Wedge thumb knuckle under the jaw, strong.
3. Flatten the fist.
4. Finger-walk across the chest (same mechanic as finger-walking an arm up in mount) until the wrist penetrates.
5. Elbow follows around the corner immediately.
6. Hide your thumb line behind his neck.

## Don't

1. Don't extend the hand/expose fingers before the thumb-knuckle wedge is set — they can be grabbed.
2. Don't lead with the wrist — too thick, won't fit.
3. Don't leave the thumb line exposed after penetrating, or you may have to restart the whole entry.

## Rule of thumb

No strangle without some jawline exposure — same logic as no heel hook without heel exposure (exceptions covered later in the course).
