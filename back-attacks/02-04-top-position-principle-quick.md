# Top Position (Principle) — Quick Reference

## The point

Top hand position = clear path to the strangle. Bottom hand position = you have to fight through his hands first. Win the top-hand battle and everything else gets easier.

## Why

1. Hands on top → nothing between your strangle arm and his throat.
2. His hands on top of yours → you must fight through them before attacking.
3. Direct analog: top hand position here = inside foot position in ashi garami. Whoever wins it wins the exchange.

## Action steps

1. Control hand: fight hard to keep this on top — near mandatory.
2. Strangle hand: top position is ideal but not guaranteed.
3. Can't win strangle-side top hand? Bring a leg over the opponent's arm instead — a leg on top works just as well as a hand.
4. Best case: two hands on top, both with cross grips.
5. Don't grind in an endless top-hand stalemate — once a leg-over opportunity appears, take it and free the strangle hand.

## Rule

Something must always be on top of his defensive hands — a hand or a leg. Whoever's limbs dominate top position dominates the strangle game.
