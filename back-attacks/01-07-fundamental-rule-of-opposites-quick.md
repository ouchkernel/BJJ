# Fundamental Rule Of Opposites — Quick Reference

## The rule

Head and strangle arm must always be on **opposite** sides of the opponent's head. This is the head trap.

## Why it's fundamental

1. Head trap intact → live strangle threat, no matter what else is happening.
2. Head + strangle arm end up on the **same side** → threat is dead, opponent turns to face you.
3. Doesn't matter how it happens — your positioning error or his grip-fighting (e.g., 2-on-1 on your strangle arm, dragging it across) both kill the threat the same way.

## Role split this defines

- **Offense's job:** preserve the opposite-sides relationship at all times.
- **Defense's job:** break it — get your strangle arm onto the same side as your head, by any means.

## Use this to read every escape

Any escape attempt you see from the back is, underneath the details, an attempt to do exactly one thing: get your strangle hand and your head onto the same side of the opponent's head. Recognize that pattern and you'll see the escape coming before it lands.
