# Primacy Principle — Quick Reference

## The point

Given a choice between a strangle and a joint lock from the back, always take the strangle.

## Why

1. Strangles have finality — no toughness, courage, or willpower gets you through a well-applied one. You simply pass out.
2. Joint locks don't have that guarantee — a tough opponent (especially with the clock running out) may accept a broken limb rather than tap.
3. This gap in certainty is why strangles get default priority, especially at the highest levels of competition.

## Default weapons from the back

1. **Rear naked strangle** (mata leao) — arm-based, precision.
2. **Rear triangle / ushiro sankaku** — leg-based, power.

Both are strangles, both extremely high percentage. Start here whenever the back is available.

## Don't

Don't treat joint locks as equally good options from the back — when both are on the table, the strangle wins by default.
