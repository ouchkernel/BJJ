# Default Principle — Quick Reference

## The point

During a hard hand fight, the opponent's chin will inevitably rise, if only slightly. The instant it does, your hand goes straight in for the neck — automatically, no thinking required.

## Why it works

1. Holding chin-to-chest is exhausting to sustain through a real hand fight — it will rise.
2. You only need it to rise the width of your own wrist — a small angle change is enough.
3. If his chin is tucked, it stops your wrist. The moment it rises past that threshold, your wrist slides in underneath.

## Action steps

1. Constantly monitor chin position while hand fighting.
2. Chin rises ~1 wrist-width off the chest → hand shoots in immediately, in one motion.
3. Once in, apply the hidden hand principle — bring it around and behind toward the finish.
4. Make this a hardwired reflex — never a "hm, I could've gone" afterthought.
5. If the neck attack stalls, fall back to the hand fight for arm control, then return to the default the next time the chin rises.

## Don't

Don't treat this as a conscious decision you deliberate over. Unless his chin is physically blocking your wrist, you should already be moving.
