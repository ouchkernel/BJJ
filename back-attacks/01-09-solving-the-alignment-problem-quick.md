# Solving The Alignment Problem — Understanding — Quick Reference

## The point

Before fixing anything, know exactly what you're fighting: the opponent's escape from the back is a fixed 4-step sequence, not a random scramble.

## The 4-step escape sequence (always in this order)

1. **Beat the bottom hook** — falling to a side creates a low bottom hook that's easy to slide over.
2. **Beat the bottom knee** — the real control point; opponent slides past it after clearing the hook.
3. **Beat the head** — opponent swings his head across, putting your strangle arm and head on the *same* side of his head (kills the head trap).
4. **Beat the inside shoulder** — inside shoulder touches the mat = escape complete, abandon and reset.

## Core definitions

- **Alignment** = your chest glued to his back + head trap intact.
- **Head trap** = your strangle arm on one side of his head, your head on the other. Break this = no strangle possible.
- **Open side** = the gap next to your control hand — this is always where he tries to fall/escape.

## Why this matters

- Each step requires the previous one — he can't skip ahead.
- Stopping step 1 is cheap. Stopping step 3 is nearly impossible.
- Priority: shut it down as early in the sequence as you can.
