# Double Cross / Direct Grips — Quick Reference

## The goal

Double cross, both grips direct = the top of the hand-fighting hierarchy. Most associated with competition success. Not always reachable, but always the target when you have a choice.

## Path to it

1. Double straight grips → pass off → single cross grip.
2. Opponent reacts one of two ways:
   - Blocks at the neck → losing move, fake strangle and go through anyway.
   - Tries to **peel your cross grip off** with his free hand → this is the one that helps you.

## Exploit the peel (principle of top hands)

1. He reaches to peel your cross grip → his hand becomes top-hand dominant on yours.
2. You still have a spare hand — use it to pull his peeling hand off.
3. Result: double cross, both grips direct.

## Finish

1. Foot over the top (prior perception).
2. Shoelaces behind the back.
3. Pass primary hand to the knuckle line.
4. Result: uncontested strangle hand.

## Why not just stick with double straight

Fine at lower levels, but doesn't control elbows — strong opponents eventually yank free at higher levels. Cross grips control the elbow and hold up better.
