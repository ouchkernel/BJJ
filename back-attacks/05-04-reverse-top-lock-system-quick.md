# Reverse Top Lock System — Quick Reference

## The idea

Top lock = same trick that makes top-lock Juji Gatame from closed guard work (feet over the shoulders first, short turn) — applied from behind the back instead of just throwing your leg over the opponent's head.

## Why not just throw the leg over

- Opponent sees the strangle threat disappear (arm switched to Kimura), reads the armbar coming, and catches your leg → you lose your cross-face leg → he turns into you and bases up.
- Or he turns his head sharply into you before your leg lands → the "turn-in problem," no room to get over.

## Build steps

1. Forsake the strangle hand, bring it across, lock a Kimura grip.
2. Leg over the far **shoulder** (short distance) — not over the head.
3. Lock feet over that shoulder = top lock.
4. Drive his shoulders down to the mat as he tries to turn in.

## Branches (pick based on his reaction)

| He does this | You get this |
|---|---|
| Hands separable, foot into armpit | Rear Triangle (Ushiro Sankaku) — strangle |
| Turns hard into you to escape | Turns himself into Front Triangle (Omote Sankaku) — strangle |
| Stays flat, hands locked | Three-quarter Juji Gatame (head + shoulders controlled — harder to turn out of than a normal Juji Gatame) |
| Arm won't straighten | Kimura or Americana from the same control |

## The one fight to win

His only goal: turn inside your legs, get to his base. Counter every time: open the knee, drive his shoulder to the mat. If arms are locked too tight, feed the second foot/shoelace inside instead of forcing it — he either turns up into the front triangle or stays down for the three-quarter Juji Gatame. Both outcomes are finishes.
