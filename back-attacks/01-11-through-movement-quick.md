# Through Movement — Quick Reference

## When to use this

Opponent beat your bottom hook, but NOT the knee yet. Recoverable.

## Steps

1. The instant the hook is lost, **extend that leg out** — straighten it to push the knee further away (bigger distance for him to beat the knee).
2. Foot touches down ball-first, then rolls to heel.
3. Plant the foot, push off the mat, turn your body (and him) back the other direction.
4. Bring the foot back up and re-lock — body triangle or post rear mount, per opponent's build.

## Key rule

- Bring the **foot** in, not the **knee** — the knee's position is the real thing you're protecting.
- A hook can always be replaced. Don't panic when it's lost.

## Don't

Don't just repeat the same loose setup on the new side — that lets him beat the new bottom hook the same way and creates an endless left-right chase. Recover, THEN reinforce so the escape can't repeat.
