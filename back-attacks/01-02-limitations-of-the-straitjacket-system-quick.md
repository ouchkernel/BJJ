# Limitations Of The Straitjacket System — Quick Reference

## The limitation

SJS = one part of your body (arms) attacking one part of theirs (neck). Small target → opponent can pool all defenses there.

## Why it matters

1. Legs trapping arms doesn't change the fact that the neck is still a small piece of real estate.
2. Concentrated opponent defense on a small target = harder breakthrough.
3. Fix: distribute attacks across the opponent's *whole* body, not just the neck.

## The system relationship to remember

- SJS = main/central system.
- Auxiliary systems (later in the series) = spread attacks wider, make opponent's defense less concentrated, make the breakthrough easier.
- Main system + auxiliary systems working together is the core structural idea of the whole course.
