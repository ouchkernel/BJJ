# Rolling Entries 01 — Quick Reference

## The Category

1. Rolling entries = a broader alternative to knee/foot entries, using roll momentum instead of a discrete leg movement.
2. Higher amplitude than the low-amplitude default — accept the tradeoff for the payoff.

## Seat Belt Roll (Shoulder Over Head)

1. Lock a strong seat belt (near-side neck lock works well for this entry).
2. Raise your shoulder, drive it over the opponent's head, head down on the mat.
3. Lift the rear leg, turn your body through in one motion — roll the opponent over.
4. Momentum separates their knees from chest — throw a foot across into hooks/body triangle/cross-body ride.

## If Knees Stay Tucked (Crab Ride)

1. Get your feet in close behind the opponent.
2. Use your foot to lengthen one of their contracted legs.
3. Pass your foot to the inside, fall across to the opposite side.
4. Land in your standard attacking sequences.
5. Alt: hook a foot behind their leg and extend it out directly.

## Seat Belt Roll (Double Underhooks / Body Lock)

1. Get double underhooks or an open-hand body lock, grip high under the chest.
2. Roll lined up directly with the opponent (not out to a side) for max momentum.
3. Land in hooks or a cross-body ride.

## No Seat Belt Available

1. Get hand-fought out of a front seat belt? Release, go behind the arms/armpits.
2. Lock hands palm-to-palm, no thumbs.
3. Drive into a high tripod: head down, feet up, body rising over the opponent.
4. Throw YOUR body first — opponent follows. Never just hook-and-pull.
5. Opponent breaks down to the mat — fight for seat belt/hooks from there.

## Don't

- Don't hook and pull with both bodies still locked together — create separation, go first.
