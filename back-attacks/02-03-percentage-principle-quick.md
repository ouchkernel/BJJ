# Percentage Principle — Quick Reference

## The point

The rear naked strangle is the single highest-percentage submission in nogi/MMA — build your back attack strategy around it, not around variety for its own sake.

## Why

1. No other submission comes close in finish rate, finality, or certainty.
2. It has finished more people, under more circumstances, than any other hold for generations.
3. Full lockup (control arm + strangle arm + head position) makes defense extremely difficult.

## Action steps

1. Get the back → attack the rear naked strangle first, with real commitment.
2. Don't flirt with it for a few seconds and bail to something else.
3. Only switch to another finish once you've been genuinely stonewalled for a considerable stretch — that means cast-iron RNS defense, not just early resistance.
4. Go back to battering the RNS as your default; treat other finishes as the exception, not the routine.

## Don't

Don't sample the rear naked strangle briefly and hop to another submission at the first sign of resistance — go high percentage and stay there.
