# Rear Triangle — Diversifying Attacks — Quick Reference

## The switch

Arms stalled on the straitjacket? Switch to legs. Rear Triangle (Ushiro Sankaku) is a small, easy transition from the same position — same conditions that enable the straitjacket enable this.

## Setup steps

1. Abandon the arm-strangle grip, bring your strangle hand to the opposite side of the opponent's neck.
2. Lift his wrist, match suicide-zone to suicide-zone (veins cross).
3. Hug 2-on-1 — his free arm is dead weight, trapped under your leg.
4. Connect his knuckles to your chest; extend your leg and sit into a strong position.
5. Leg over his shoulder, tilt across, kink both legs so feet point the same direction — this alone blocks turning in/out.
6. Lock initial triangle → hand-assist (grab own heel) → tight triangle.
7. Shoelace hooked behind his back.

## The one rule: extension

- Opponent's elbow **overhead** = throat exposed, strangle at max power. Good.
- Opponent's elbow toward **his belly button** = he can push your elbow, kill the strangle. Bad — don't chase this with crunching/pulling, it just gives him room to escape.

## Diversify when the strangle stalls (no reset needed)

| Finish | Key detail |
|---|---|
| Juji Gatame (basic) | Foot flat on mat, elbow over your hip |
| Juji Gatame (reinforced) | Hand to your shoulder + wraparound grip, hands on own buttocks, arch |
| Kimura | Elbow to your abdomen — head/arm already trapped by triangle |
| Americana | For a bent arm — hug elbow, drive forward |
| Wrist lock | Control elbow first, knuckle-line + opposite wrist grip, slap wrist down/turned, elbow-over-elbow, hand over hand |

## Don't

- Don't let his elbow travel toward his belly button.
- Don't chase a stalled strangle with crunching forward or grabbing/pulling at legs — it generates escape-enabling movement.
