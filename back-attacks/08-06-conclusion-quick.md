# Conclusion — Quick Reference

## Course Summary

1. Many athletes can take and hold the back; few can finish from it reliably — that gap is what this course targets.
2. Core system: the straight jacket, which pins the opponent's defensive arms to set up the rear naked strangle.
3. Series also introduced a new mechanical approach to the rear naked strangle itself.
4. Auxiliary systems spread attacks across the whole body instead of relying on one strangle to one target (the neck).

## Why Entries Matter

1. None of the finishing systems work without first getting to a winning back position.
2. This volume's entries (near side, rolling, four point/asymmetrical rear mount) are the delivery mechanism for everything taught earlier in the series.

## Takeaway

Master the entries in this volume — they're what put the straight jacket, the rear naked strangle, and the auxiliary systems into play in live rolling and competition.
