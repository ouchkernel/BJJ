# Cross Body Ride — Leg Attacks — Quick Reference

## When to use it

Opponent rides high on your back, strangles and triangles aren't landing. Switch: put **both** arms underneath (give up the strangle) and go after the joints instead.

## Build the position

1. One foot over his leg, other leg out in crab-hooks position.
2. Lift, elevate, turn him; knee behind his buttocks, bump forward.
3. Figure-four the legs, shoelace to shoelace = **Outside Scorpion** (10th Planet lockdown, applied from outside).
4. Heels pinned to the mat, tension in both feet — don't let him pommel a leg free. Elevate briefly only if he sits up, then reset heels to mat.
5. Wrist-deep grip on his forearm, glued to your chest = upper-body control.

## Diagonal control cheat sheet

| He tries to... | Blocked by... |
|---|---|
| Turn into you | Legs (Outside Scorpion) |
| Turn away | Arm/shoulder grip |
| Sit up | Re-elevate him back down |
| Slide away | Legs + arm together |

## Three leg attacks off this position

1. **Calf slicer (far leg):** shoelace to shoelace, 5-finger grip on the **toe knuckle line** (never toes/shoelaces/shin/ankle), figure-four or foot-on-own-Achilles, turn knee out so shin blade digs into the calf.
2. **Knee compression (far leg):** same entry, pump knees in, snatch the leg with arm going **around** the hip (never in front), figure-four or foot-to-foot, shin blade into the **knee**, hips drive forward.
3. **Near-leg hip lock ("wishbone"):** if he defends the foot grip with his other leg, sweep up to the knee, hug the whole leg, head by his knee, push outward — attacks the hip joint (flexibility-proof). If he extends the leg free, go underneath and figure-four it instead.

## Don't

- Don't reach a hand inside his legs to grip a foot — he can trap that wrist and armbar you off your own body.
- Don't grip the toes, shoelaces, shin, or ankle for the calf/knee attacks — grip the toe knuckle line.
- Don't just stretch the leg on the hip lock — rotate and pull the knee out in an arc ("wishbone"), or it's just a muscle stretch he can flex through.
