# Rolling Entries 02 — Quick Reference

## Prerequisite: Reverse Underhook

1. Reverse underhook = same-side arm under opponent's same-side arm (not a normal underhook).
2. Purpose: exposes hips/lower back, not upper-body control.
3. Nothing else in this entry works without it first.

## Top Leg / Bottom Leg Setup

1. Face the opponent's legs from the reverse underhook.
2. Use your hand only to smack one leg into a top position (don't fight leg strength with hand strength).
3. Immediately hook the top leg at the Achilles tendon with your foot.

## Rolling to the Back

1. Come up to your knees, lock an outside scorpion on the ankle — both legs controlled.
2. Elbow inside the knee, roll the opponent through.
3. Land in cross-body ride or hook a foot in directly to a favorite back position.
4. Optional: catch the opposite foot too (two-on-one) before rolling for extra control.

## Vs. the Mount Elbow Escape

1. Deliberately offer your foot as bait (lift it, let them take it).
2. Withdraw your knee, re-center over their hips.
3. Punch through to a reverse underhook.
4. Lift the trapped foot out, catch the second foot.
5. Elbow in, roll — capture hip and head control.

## From Half Guard Top

1. Tripod, walk across the body; hand on hip, elbow inside the trapped knee.
2. Drive the knee free into a quasi-mounted position (two knees down, foot still held).
3. Back-step, punch to reverse underhook.
4. Unhook the feet (or just hook the opposite foot) and roll through.

## Don't

- Don't grab and yank a leg with your hand against resistance — smack it over, then control with your leg.
