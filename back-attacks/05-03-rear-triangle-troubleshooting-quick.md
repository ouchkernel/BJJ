# Rear Triangle — Troubleshooting — Quick Reference

## Problem → Fix

1. **Opponent has strong arms.** Don't muscle it — 2-on-1 grip, use your **legs** to move his body down and pin knuckles to chest. Result: a "stocks" position (foot in armpit, thigh wedges neck, hook controls hips).
2. **Opponent locks hands / buries near elbow.** Reach for the **far elbow** instead, hug it up, gather both elbows together. Alternative: pull that brings the far elbow up high, weakening the near one — draws both up together.
3. **Contact with hands lost entirely (buried at stomach).** Elbow to elbow → hand on your own knee → finger-walk the hand up your leg, leaning to the side, to bring his wrist to your elbow.
4. **Opponent strips/interferes with your feet.** Switch your body across, kick legs over as he comes up chasing them → rear triangle becomes a front triangle (Omote Sankaku). Cast leg over far shoulder, figure-four, finish.

## Always

- Target: opponent's elbow **above his forehead** — that's the win condition for every troubleshooting line above.
- Knee inside his bicep, not the reverse. Pull to fix it if it's backwards.
- From seated: elbow-to-elbow lift for momentum, not crunching forward.

## Don't

- Don't chase a buried elbow with crunching forward or grabbing/pulling at a leg — generates movement that lets him turn and slip out.
- Don't panic when he strips your feet and rises — that's not an escape, it's a walk into the front triangle.
