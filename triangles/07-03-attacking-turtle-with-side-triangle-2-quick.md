# Attacking Turtle With Side Triangle 2 — Quick Reference

## Setup

From a front headlock (often arising while attacking turtle) — the seated version of Yoko Sankaku.

## Do this, in order

1. Knee forward, thigh/hip flexor on their trapezius.
2. Post on their head — keeps it down (up = they double-leg you).
3. Foot through, hook, tight waist, second hand to the floor.
4. Pull their arm back, slide down **staying over them** (not flopped to the side).
5. Tight waist → seize them, foot behind the tricep (eliminates the shoulder).
6. Figure-four, hand on the waist, squeeze.

## Take away their double leg first

Leg behind their reaching arm before you commit to sitting through. Two knees flat on the floor = free double leg for them.

## The one mistake that ruins this

**Flopping to the side** instead of sitting through with control, head over their waist. Costs you top position for nothing.

## Slippage check

Foot **over your own knee** before locking the figure-four, not under it.
