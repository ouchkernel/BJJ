# Triangle Vs Single Leg 3 — Deep/Elbow-Deep Grip — Quick Reference

## When

Opponent's single-leg grip is elbow-deep (above your knee), not the usual wrist-deep. Tight waist stops working here.

## Do this instead

1. Drop the tight waist/whizzer entirely.
2. Seize their tricep/elbow with your hand.
3. Foot goes **over their head** OR **to the back of their neck** (pick one).
4. Turn the corner as they posture into it.
5. Lock figure-four → strangle.

## Bonus option: hantai sankaku (wrong-side triangle)

Same elbow-deep setup — if their arm ends up off-center (not centered on your body) as they posture, turn into a hantai sankaku instead of the standard front triangle. Both finish well; pick whichever their arm position gives you.
