# Omoplata To Front Triangle — Quick Reference

## The problem with a standard omoplata

Lying flat/aligned to finish removes weight from opponent's head → they escape by posturing out.

## Do this instead

1. Enter the clamp, foot over the top, hook the far shoulder.
2. Stay **perpendicular** — don't lie flat/aligned. Come up to an elbow.
3. Toes on the mat, hip-heist, knee forces their head down.
4. Post up on both hands, drive hips forward.
5. This creates a kimura-like threat on the trapped arm → opponent's choice: broken arm or roll forward. Most roll.
6. As they roll, hook your foot inside theirs — one-in-one-out achieved.
7. Hug the head, knee down, turn body across so only head + one arm are trapped.
8. Head to mat, legs up, lock figure-four.
9. Finish top or bottom — punch arm through to come up under if you want the strangle from below.
10. If they stand/move, hook inside their leg as usual.

## Key mechanical fix

**Perpendicular, not flat.** That's the entire difference from a standard, hard-to-finish omoplata.
