# Front Triangle & Other Submissions 4: Omoplata — Quick Reference

## Why omoplata sets up triangles

Hard to finish (esp. no-gi) → common defense is a forward roll → that roll is your trigger.

## Entry

1. Clamp: knee front of shoulder, knee behind opposite shoulder.
2. Foot through and across his face/head.
3. Around the corner → omoplata.

## Head-position race

Keep his head **down** with your straightened leg. Head rises = he can put you on your back. Head stays down = forces the forward roll.

## When he rolls

1. Extract arm, head well forward.
2. Leg comes around behind you as he rolls up.
3. Drive across him, hug the tricep.
4. Hand on floor, back-step across his body → one-in-one-out.
5. Feet around the corner, catch him → **Mote Sankaku (front triangle)**.
6. Head down → feet go light → figure-four, finish top or bottom.

## Pendulum mechanic

Weight on inside leg, swing/attack with outside leg. Keep everything out to the side — this is what forces the roll in the first place.
