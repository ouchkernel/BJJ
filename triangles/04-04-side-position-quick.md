# Front Triangle Side Position — Quick Reference

## The problem

Top-side triangle needs one-in-one-out. Near arm is defensively framed against your hips — hardest to trap.

## Trap the near arm (hip-drag method)

1. Switch cross-face grip → over-grip.
2. Walk down toward opponent's hips.
3. Buttocks/lower back against his trapped elbow.
4. Walk backward using your lower back (not arms) — drives his elbow **above his shoulder line**.
5. Return to knees without losing it: post hand, hip-heist, knee brushes into place.

Result: elbow trapped between your elbow, his head, your hip.

## Finish the entry

1. Rise off chest-to-chest.
2. Catch his wrist, push inward, hand behind his head.
3. Rise in level, **face forward** (not across his chest — critical).
4. Chin over your own knee, body forward past his head.
5. Whip leg around, hook under his neck.
6. Legs forward, head to mat, lock figure-four.

## Key cue

Head over his chest = leg feels impossible to lift.
Face forward instead = leg comes free easily.
