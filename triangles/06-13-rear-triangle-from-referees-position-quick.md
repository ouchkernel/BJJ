# Rear Triangle From Referees Position — Quick Reference

## Turtle vs. referee's position

| | Turtle | Referee's position |
|---|---|---|
| Elbows | On the mat | **Off** the mat |
| Purpose | Hide/protect arms & neck | Raise head/chest to stand up |

## The trigger

Attacking an armlock from turtle → opponent shifts to extended arms, elbows off the mat (referee's position) → that's your cue to switch to Ushiro Sankaku.

## Do this

1. Insert your foot to get **one-in-one-out** (whether their arm blocked your roll or is threatening to stand).
2. Use thigh/knee to turn them over (thigh brings head down, or knee drives into belly button).
3. Switch legs → trap triangle → hand-assist → figure-four → strangle.

## Skill being trained

Reading the elbow position mid-attack — that read is what tells you to switch, not a fixed sequence.
