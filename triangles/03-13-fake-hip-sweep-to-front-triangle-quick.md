# Fake Hip Sweep To Front Triangle — Quick Reference

## Do this, in order

1. Closed guard, hands to the inside, pull their hands to the mat.
2. Hips out to the side, elbow up, then hand up.
3. **Strength comes from hip position, not your arm** — leg brings your hips above theirs, chest nearly faces the mat.
4. Drive forward — they're forced to post a hand to avoid falling (fake sweep, not a real one).
5. Hand posts → feet off the mat, balance on their seat → your weight shifts onto their head.
6. Move head from close to far, pull them into the triangle.
7. Resistance (stack/posture) → keep knee and shin close as you turn the corner.
8. Figure-four → strangle.

## The trick

You're not actually sweeping them — you're forcing the defensive hand-post that a real sweep threat would cause, then using that moment to load your weight onto their head.
