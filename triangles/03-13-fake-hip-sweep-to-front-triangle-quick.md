# Fake Hip Sweep To Front Triangle — Quick Reference

## Do this

1. Closed guard: hands to opponent's biceps, inside.
2. Pull their hands to the mat.
3. Shift hips out → elbow → hand → foot planted.
4. Drive forward hard enough that they're forced to base out a hand, like a real sweep is coming.
5. The instant they base out: feet off the mat, balance on their base. Your weight is now on their head.
6. Legs go weightless → move head close-to-far, pull them into the triangle.
7. They resist (stack/posture) → scoop hand inside their leg, knee and shin close together.
8. Lock figure-four, strangle.

## The trick

You're not actually sweeping them. You're threatening a sweep hard enough that they base out — and that base-out is what transfers their weight and makes your legs free to swing over.

## Finish detail

Keep knee and shin tight together as they try to move — denies them room to pull free.
