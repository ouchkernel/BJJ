# Reverse Triangle Mechanics — Quick Reference

## What it is

1. Opponent supine, you top or bottom.
2. Step over → **one-in-one-out** (one arm of theirs outside your legs, one inside). Same rule for every triangle.
3. Strangle leg crosses **above** their shoulder line — eliminates the shoulder.
4. Back-heel → strangle comes on.

## The one detail that makes or breaks it

- **Your knee must be below their ear**, sitting on the carotid artery.
- Shin goes across/above the shoulder line, perpendicular to their spine.
- Knee above the ear = worthless, they bench-press you off no matter how hard you squeeze.
- Knee below the ear = shoulder fully eliminated, strangle comes on immediately.

Works the same mirrored from bottom position.

## Reputation

- Lowest-scoring of the 5 triangles in competition statistically.
- But: done correctly, arguably the **tightest** of all 5. Done poorly, the **weakest**.
- Precision > resistance. This one lives or dies on your knee placement, not how hard the opponent fights.
