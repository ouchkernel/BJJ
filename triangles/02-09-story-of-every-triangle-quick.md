# Story Of Every Triangle Is The Same — Quick Reference

## The 2-step story (applies to every triangle type)

1. **Get one-in-one-out** → lock a trap triangle (ankles crossed, one arm in your legs, one arm out, head caught).
2. **Manage trap → figure-four** using the mechanics you already know (shoulder-line, eliminate shoulder, knee below ear, tricep not lat, posture control, scoop arm, unified legs, shoulders up).

## Applied sequence

1. One arm in, one arm out.
2. Lock trap triangle.
3. Opponent pulls away → **knee pull** (knees to chest) closes the distance.
4. Get your wrist (not just hand) around the head once it's close enough.
5. Unlock trap triangle safely, scoop hand in, form angle.
6. Feet unified, same direction → lock figure-four.

## Why this matters

Front triangle, back triangle, reverse triangle — all of them are this same 2-chapter story. Learn the pattern once, apply it everywhere.
