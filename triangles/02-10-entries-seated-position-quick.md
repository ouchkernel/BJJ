# Entries To Front Triangle (Seated Position) — Quick Reference

## Start seated, not supine

Seated bottom position gives way more mobility than the traditional flat-on-back closed guard entry. All entries in this course start here.

## Entry sequence

1. Secure a two-on-one grip on one arm (push wrist to centerline → pronated grip → slide to elbow grip, thumb over humerus).
2. Pull their wrist across your body to your own hip, elbows tight — hides the grip's weak point.
3. Put a foot on their hip, same side as the trapped arm → this leg is **unweighted** (your weight's on your butt) → very mobile.
4. When they try to catch that foot, circle around and land it on their bicep instead.
5. Throw legs into the trap triangle, both feet forward (unified).
6. Opponent posts to escape → knee-pull brings their head to you.
7. Scoop grip in, turn corner, wedges in, lock figure-four.

## Why seated

- More mobile for you.
- Easier to move the opponent into "one-in-one-out."
- Sets up everything else you already know how to finish.
