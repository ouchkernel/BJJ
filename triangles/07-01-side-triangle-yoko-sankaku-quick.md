# Side Triangle — Yoko Sankaku — Quick Reference

## Why it matters

Judo's #1 triangle (more back exposure from throws, doubles as a pin). Underused in BJJ — shouldn't be.

## Do this, in order

1. Foot into opponent's armpit → one-in-one-out.
2. Strangle leg / support leg split. Support leg under the opponent's **head** (never the scapula).
3. Hollow of knee all the way around the **tricep** — calf-only contact caps you at a trap triangle.
4. Heel to the rear deltoid.
5. Eliminate the shoulder: thigh under their head, knee down, hook the tricep not the shoulder.
6. Lock figure-four with a back-heel pull — don't extend your legs to finish.

## The fix for almost every failed attempt: belly down shift

- [ ] Support leg under the **scapula** instead of the head? → unlock, bottom foot to mat, both knees pointing down (belly down), then shift back to a hip.
- Expect to need this **almost every time** — a clean first-attempt lock is rare.

## Foot check

Shoelaces belong in the hollow of your own knee. On the calf = no strangle.

## Reputation

"Side triangle is weak" = bad mechanics (leg under scapula), not a flaw in the move. Done right, it's as tight as any triangle.
