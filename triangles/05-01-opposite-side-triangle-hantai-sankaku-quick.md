# Opposite Side Triangle — Quick Reference

## What it is

A triangle locked in the "wrong" direction. Looks broken, isn't — the fix for a weak-looking Hantai Sankaku is always **alignment**, never more squeeze.

## Do this, in order

1. Start in a trap triangle.
2. Opponent defends by putting a hand behind your hip (brings shoulder in) → counter by reaching your hand deep between your own legs and pulling to a **90° angle**. Arm gets you to 90° and no further — don't fight for more with it.
3. Swing your **opposite-side leg like a pendulum** — this is what actually completes the turn to north-south, not the arm.
4. Knee goes under the opponent's ribcage/armpit as your foot reaches the floor.
5. Knee behind their head as a wedge.
6. Grab your own shin, pull into figure-four directly above their head.

## 2 checks before you squeeze

- [ ] Are you fully **north-south** with the opponent? (whole body facing the same direction)
- [ ] Is your shin/foot pointing **straight up to the ceiling**? (not off to the side)

If either check fails, you get a kimura opportunity, not a strangle.

## Weird but true

Leg roles flip vs. a normal triangle — the **thigh** hits the carotid, and what's normally the support leg becomes the strangle leg here.
