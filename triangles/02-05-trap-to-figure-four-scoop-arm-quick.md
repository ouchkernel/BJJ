# Trap → Figure Four: The Scoop Arm — Quick Reference

## Four limbs, four jobs

- Strangle leg = does the choking
- Support leg = locks the strangle leg
- Posture arm = controls the head
- **Scoop arm = creates the angle** (this lesson)

## Target orientation

Perpendicular to the opponent. Square-on finishes work on beginners and get countered against anyone good.

## Scoop arm — 3 options, in order

1. **Inside near leg** (favorite) — elbow-deep scoop, ear turns toward their knee, feet point forward.
2. **Outside far leg** (if they pull the near leg away) — same mechanics, other side.
3. **Under the arm** (if both legs are defended) — weaker but still works.

## Key logic

Opponent can only defend one leg at a time. Near leg denied → take far leg. Both denied → arm scoop. There's always a next option.
