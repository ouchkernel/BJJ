# Mindset

## Overview

Fourth of the four critical themes. Most success factors in triangles are physical, but this chapter isolates the mental piece: you cannot act on an opportunity you don't perceive.

---

## The core idea

- "If you can't see an opportunity, you'll never take the physical action required to actually make it happen." Vision comes before action, every time.
- Applied to triangles specifically: you have to train your mind to actively see the possibility of creating the one-in-one-out scenario, in a wide variety of positions and scrambles.
- Every triangle — regardless of type — is ultimately telling the same story: an opponent's arm caught inside your legs, and an opponent's arm caught outside your legs. Once that pattern is internalized as a mental image, students start recognizing far more triangle opportunities live than they did before.
- Success with the triangle is described as coming down largely to your ability to see the one-in-one-out opportunity arise in a wide variety of situations — the physical execution follows once you see it.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Train recognition, not just execution — actively look for one-in-one-out during live rolling, don't wait for it to appear.
- Every triangle is mechanically the same underlying story (one arm in, one arm out) — internalizing that single mental image is what increases attempt frequency in live situations.
- This theme is the bridge from the previous three (multiplicity, two-stage approach, mechanics) into the drilling portion of the course that follows.
