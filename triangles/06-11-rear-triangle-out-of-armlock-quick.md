# Rear Triangle Out Of Armlock — Quick Reference

## The situation

Jujigatame in progress, opponent escapes by pushing your **cross-face leg** away and over their own head. Armlock is now dead — but this isn't a lost position.

## Sequence 1

1. Curl your foot around their head as they push it off — knee ends at the crown of their head.
2. Let them keep pushing (it's what they want to do anyway).
3. Other foot goes just over their shoulder, rotate your body around.
4. Legs come in → trap triangle → catch wrist → hand-assist north → Ushiro Sankaku.

## Sequence 2

1. Stay crowded over their head (distance = their escape route).
2. When they push their head over the top — that's your cue.
3. Foot out, grip it, turn an angle, walk up.
4. Knuckles to chest, pressure, strangle complete.

## Rule

Their escape motion IS the setup. Don't fight it — ride it into the triangle.
