# Kuzushi Entries Into Front Triangle — Quick Reference

## The move

1. Foot on opponent's knee, **toes out**, curved hook (not toes up — slips off).
2. Slight pull + push together.
3. Opponent's balance reflex forces a hand to the mat — universal, unavoidable.
4. Grip you're using doesn't matter — any upper-body tie works once the hand is down.
5. Cover arm, cover hip, turn corner, knee to crotch, figure-four.

## Rule

**Push, never kick.** Kicking just angers your partner and does less. Pushing destabilizes.

## When to reach for this

- Opponent standing over you → attack legs instead (Ashi Garami).
- Opponent on two knees → this kuzushi entry (or other upper-body attacks) makes sense.

## Why it works

Every human is hardwired to recover balance by posting a hand down. You don't need a specific grip to trigger it — just an off-balancing push/pull combo.
