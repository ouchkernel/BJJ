# Front Triangle Side Position 2 — Quick Reference

## Two ways to finish the near-arm trap

| | Foot penetration | Knee penetration |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Fast | Slower |
| Space given up | More (window of exposure) | Less (tighter) |
| Grip | Hold wrist, push to sternum | Shoot hand to bicep |
| Head direction | Up, over weighted knee | Down and across |
| Best for | Fast, committed athletes | Everyone else |

## Foot penetration steps

1. Trap arm to hip, push hand to sternum.
2. Rise/level-change, head over your **weighted** knee (not between knees).
3. Other leg goes light — step over top, lock around neck.
4. Commit fully once you start — hesitating = opponent frees the elbow.

## Knee penetration steps

1. Same starting trap.
2. Shoot hand to opponent's bicep (not the wrist).
3. Head down and across, fist to mat, clear opponent's fist.
4. Knee drives up and penetrates through the shoulder.
5. Windshield-wipe legs → trap triangle.
6. Control head, turn corner, figure-four.

## Balance cue

Head over the WEIGHTED knee, not between your knees. That's what makes the other leg light.
