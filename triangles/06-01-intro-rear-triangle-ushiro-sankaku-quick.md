# Intro To Rear Triangle — Quick Reference

## Why this triangle is the easy one

- Locking triangles on big people with short legs = alignment + eliminating the shoulder.
- Front triangle: YOU have to do the work to eliminate the shoulder (angle + inner thigh to tricep).
- Rear triangle: the position eliminates the shoulder **for you**, almost automatically.

## The one check that matters

- [ ] Hips **above** opponent's shoulder line → easy lock, only head+arm trapped, strong strangle.
- [ ] Hips **below** shoulder line → you'll fight to even lock a full triangle. Fix this first if the lock feels loose.

## Who should start here

Struggling with short legs vs. broad shoulders on other triangles? Build confidence with Ushiro Sankaku first — it's the most forgiving of the 5. That confidence transfers to the rest.
