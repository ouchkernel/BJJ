# Rear Triangle From Back 3 — Quick Reference

## Starting point

Already-established back control (body triangle, hooks in, rear mount) — not turtle.

## The one thing that matters

Head **directly over** opponent's head, straight line. Too far to the side = you won't hit the triangle. Correct alignment auto-aligns your legs.

## Sequence 1 — From back control

1. Lift one hand, push the other down (isolate primary + secondary defensive hands).
2. Strangle hand to wrong side of their head, wrist-to-wrist.
3. Knee pushes them down, head goes directly over theirs.
4. Trap triangle locks itself → hand-assist → figure-four.

## Sequence 2 — From rear mount

1. Fall to the side, foot over their forearm.
2. Knees clear their head out of the way, forearm goes in behind them.
3. Knuckles to chest, turn, head directly over theirs.
4. Unified legs → trap → figure-four → strangle.

## Fix if stuck

Can't lock the triangle → check your head position first, not your legs.
