# Unified Legs — Quick Reference

## The mistake

Good turn to angle, but the two lower legs end up pointing in **different directions**. Result: no resistance, opponent postures out freely.

## The fix

Both lower legs point in the **same direction** — like two arrows aligned, not just "close."

## Why it matters

- Same direction = less effort to close the figure-four, harder for opponent to escape.
- Different directions = triangle formation has no real integrity, opponent postures up like it's nothing.

## Check every rep

Before locking figure-four: are both lower legs pointing the same way? If not, fix that before anything else.
