# Partner Inversion & Rotational Drill — Quick Reference

## Do this

1. Partner kneels behind you, grab wrists.
2. Invert — weight on shoulders, knees to chest.
3. Hook a **heel** (not sole) into partner's armpit — one foot brings the other across.
4. Partner stays strong/still — you're practicing rotating around the corner, not yanking them.
5. Catch the shoulder, release second hand, rotate through, lock figure-four.

## The one cue that matters

Catch with the **hollow of your knee** on the partner's trapezius, not your calf muscle.
- Calf catch = weak connection = scramble.
- Knee-hollow catch = clean lock, easy rotation (you're balanced on your shoulder points, low mat friction).

## Sequence to remember

Hook heel → rotate around corner → knee-hollow catches trapezius → second knee-hollow follows → figure-four.
