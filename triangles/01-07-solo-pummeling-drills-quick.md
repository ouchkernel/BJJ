# Solo Pummeling Drills — Quick Reference

## What pommeling is

Circular foot movement, either foot, either direction — lets you thread a foot into tight spaces the way your hands already can.

## Progression — do in order

1. **Supine** (on your back) — no leg weight, relaxed abdomen. Good daily warm-up.
2. **Seated, hands on floor** — knees come up higher toward shoulders, more core engaged.
3. **Seated, hands off floor** — core alone supports the legs now.
4. **Elbow-propped, vertical** — closest to live sparring positions.

## Why bother

This is the dexterity that lets you pommel a foot into the one-in-one-out position even against a resisting opponent. Do it daily, not once.
