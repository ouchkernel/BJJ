# Side Triangle ↔ Armbar Links — Quick Reference

## From a bad near-side armbar attempt

1. Foot through opponent's armpit (one-in-one-out).
2. Tilt to the side, extend bottom leg through.
3. Belly-down shift: foot to mat, come up, kick leg all the way through.
4. Come back into position — shoulder eliminated, strangle is immense.

## From a spinning armbar (turn the mistake into a weapon)

Normal spinning armbar mistake: knee inside opponent's armpit → traps your foot, kills your cross-face leg, opponent escapes the armbar.

**Do it on purpose instead, to go to the triangle:**
1. Underhook as normal.
2. Deliberately put knee inside the armpit.
3. Sit through.
4. Lock kimura.
5. Tilt to hips, kick bottom leg through.
6. Belly-down shift, punch leg through.
7. Lock — tight strangle.

## The idea

A bad armbar leg position ≠ a bad triangle leg position. What kills the juji gatame (knee inside the armpit, no cross-face leg) is exactly the setup you want for yoko senkaku.

## Checklist

- [ ] Recognized "no cross-face leg" as your cue this isn't going to be an armbar
- [ ] Did the belly-down shift before trying to lock
- [ ] Kicked the leg all the way through before coming back to lock
