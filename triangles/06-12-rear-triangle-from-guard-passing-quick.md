# Rear Triangle From Guard Passing — Quick Reference

## The idea

Seated guard = back exposed on the far side. Kimura grip removes their turn-away option → forces them to turn into you → triangle.

## Sequence 1

1. Foot inside, knee over far hip, armpit above their head.
2. Secure wrist or post hand, then secure arm with frame arm.
3. Roll over into a **kimura grip**.
4. They can only turn into you (kimura blocks turning away).
5. Knee inside their hip, lift the elbow, foot over their shoulder, return them to the mat.
6. Unified legs → trap triangle → hand-assist → strangle.

## Sequence 2 (opposite direction)

1. Foot inside, knee to opposite hip, kimura, roll across.
2. Form a T-shape, lift elbow + knee to their lat as they turn in.
3. Foot hooks over → one-in-one-out (foot in armpit).
4. Trap triangle → hand-assist → figure-four → strangle.

## Key detail

Armpit above their head early = easier kimura entry.
