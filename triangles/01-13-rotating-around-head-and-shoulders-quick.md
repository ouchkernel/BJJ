# Rotating Around Head & Shoulders — Quick Reference

## The one idea

Front / rear / opposite-side triangle = the same lock (legs around head + shoulder), different angle. Not three separate techniques.

## Do this

- If a triangle angle isn't finishing, **rotate around the opponent's head/shoulder** into a different category-A triangle — don't release and reset.
- Practice starting from a basic front triangle (Amote) and rotating through rear/opposite-side without letting go.

## Why

This is the physical, drillable version of the multiplicity principle — "use all 5 as a team" becomes a rotation skill within category A.
