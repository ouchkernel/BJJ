# Multiplicity Of Triangles — Quick Reference

## The point

Most people only know "triangle" = front triangle. Front triangle IS the best one — but it's one of five tools, not the only one.

## Why it matters

1. Front triangle finishes more than the rest combined — keep it primary.
2. But when it fails, you have 4 more options.
3. Inside a category (A: front/rear/opposite, or B: side/reverse), you can switch triangles **without releasing** — no reset needed.
4. Train yourself to see all 5 as live options from any given position, not just the front triangle.

## Don't

Don't treat the other 4 triangles as backups you'll "get to eventually." They're teammates you switch to mid-attack.
