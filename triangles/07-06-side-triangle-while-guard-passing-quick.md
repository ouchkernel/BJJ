# Side Triangle While Guard Passing — Quick Reference

## The dilemma

Seated guard = back exposure on the far side. Lock a kimura → opponent can't turn away (kimura blocks it) → their only option is to turn **in**.

## Do this, in order

1. Step foot inside, lock across the body into a **T-kimura**.
2. They turn in (forced) → lift their elbow to slow them, knee underneath, leg over their head.
3. Kick through as they fight to come up → lands in Yoko Sankaku.
4. **Belly-down shift** first — don't force the lock from the cramped position.
5. Punch leg through, come back, lock the figure-four.

## Rule

Same engine as the rear-triangle-from-guard-passing entry: kimura removes their turn-away option, forcing them into the triangle.
