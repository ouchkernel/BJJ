# Side Triangle Switching To Arm Locks — Quick Reference

## The 4-step system (final recap)

1. Working grip.
2. One-in-one-out → trap triangle.
3. Trap → figure-four (strangle).
4. Strangle fails → joint lock. Yoko Sankaku has an unusually deep menu here.

## Kimura — far arm

1. Drag far arm in, take the wrist.
2. Push wrist out/away — one-handed kimura.
3. Wrist toward your knee, push across, legs pull back.
4. Stronger: one hand on wrist, one on elbow — pull elbow, push wrist.

## Kimura — near arm (if far arm isn't working)

1. Lock near-arm kimura, pop to base, hand behind their back.
2. Sit into the power line, legs separate.
3. Everything up to their shoulder line, pull for the finish.

## Wrist lock

1. Back-heel, push the wrist — or two-on-one (push one hand, pull the other) for more.
2. Jam their tricep on your bicep, close hand-over-hand.
3. Twisting out becomes very hard — nasty finish.

## Can't get any of them?

Go back to the strangle: ear to their hip, kick through, relock. Everything here threatens everything else — cycle through it.
