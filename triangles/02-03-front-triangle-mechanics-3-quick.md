# Knee, Inner Thigh & Foot Direction — Quick Reference

## Three things to check every time

1. **Knee → below the ear**, not on top of it. Below the ear = lock lands behind the head (survives posture/standing). Above the ear = lock lands behind the shoulder (easy to posture out of).
2. **Inner thigh → tricep, not lat muscle.** Lat muscle contact = opponent can posture. Tricep contact = arm stays locked, shoulder stays out.
3. **Both feet point forward** (not down toward the opponent's hips). This is what keeps the inner thigh pinned on the tricep.

## Do this

1. Turn the corner, land your knee crook below the opponent's ear (on the carotid, not on the ear itself).
2. Brush your inner thigh along their tricep as you go.
3. Throw both lower legs so they point the same forward direction.
4. Lock figure-four. Should feel unusually tight immediately — if it doesn't, check knee/thigh placement first.

## Watchword

"Knee below the ear, lock behind the head." Say it every rep until it's automatic.
