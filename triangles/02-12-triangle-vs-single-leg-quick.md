# Triangle Vs Single Leg — Quick Reference

## When

Opponent shoots and gets a single leg, head inside, both knees down, working to put you on your back — common off a takedown, an underhook-into-single-leg escape, or a half guard scramble.

## Step-over method

1. Tight waist on the opponent.
2. Free leg off the mat (don't leave both legs down — invites the double leg).
3. Stuff their head down.
4. Knee blocks their elbow; base on your **hand** (not elbow/shoulder — you need height).
5. Pull their trapped elbow up to your hip.
6. Step your body across in one motion — catch head with hand, switch hand to floor, knee comes around the corner.
7. Head to the floor → lock figure-four → strangle or arm lock.

## Don't

Base on an elbow or shoulder. You lose the height advantage (head/hips above theirs) that the whole entry depends on.
