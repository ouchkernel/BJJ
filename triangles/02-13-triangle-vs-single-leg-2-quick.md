# Triangle Vs Single Leg 2 — Quarter Nelson — Quick Reference

## Setup

Same as before: opponent deep on a single leg, head inside, both knees down. Take your free leg up, don't leave both legs on the mat.

## Two quarter nelson variants (either works)

1. **Head post** — stuff their head down, post on it, break shoulder then hip to the floor.
2. **Chin strap** (slightly stronger) — grab the jaw, grab your own forearm (not wrist), lever straight down into the mat.

## Then into the triangle

1. Hip is down → shift grip to back of head → down to one hand.
2. Block their hip with that hand, walk around their body.
3. Catch wrist + head together on the far side.
4. Throw across your body, head to the mat.
5. Elbow locked to your own hip (trapped arm can't pull free) → step over → figure-four.
