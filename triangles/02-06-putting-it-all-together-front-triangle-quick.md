# Putting It All Together (Front Triangle) — Quick Reference

## The combined sequence

1. Trap triangle established.
2. Punch through, control the head (posture).
3. Scoop the leg, lower back off the ground, both feet forward.
4. Wedge goes in behind the head (still open — hold the head here).
5. Drive forward/up, turn shoulders in, shoulder disappears.
6. Lock figure-four — wedges close around head + one arm only.

## What this position auto-defeats

| Opponent tries | What happens |
|---|---|
| Stack you | Drives them deeper into the strangle instead |
| Posture / stand up | Lock stays behind the head, doesn't break |
| Turn to angle | Scoop arm inside their leg makes it near-impossible |
| Elbow to the floor | Arm caught under yours prevents it |

## Myth to drop

You do **not** need to pull the elbow fully across the body to finish. Essential: minimal trapped volume (head + one arm), shoulder excluded. Elbow position: optional.

## Reminder

Short legs ≠ excuse. Correct position (perpendicular angle + shoulder-line + shoulder elimination) often makes short-legged triangles *tighter* than long-legged ones.
