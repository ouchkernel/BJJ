# Partner Pummeling Drills — Quick Reference

## The no-hands drill

1. Closed guard, partner's hands on your biceps (not your hips).
2. Flare elbows to make room for your knees.
3. Open guard, turn out to one side (foot on floor or partner's hip helps).
4. Pommel one foot inside/over the shoulder, no hands, start locking triangle.
5. Thread the foot through, lock figure-four. Partner's hands stay on your biceps the whole time.
6. Repeat side to side, 20–50 reps.

## The one cue that matters

Knee points **down at the mat**, not up at the ceiling. Wrong direction = foot can't thread through, no matter how good your hip offset is.

## Why

Trains knees-to-chest + hollow body against real resistance — the single most important entry skill.
