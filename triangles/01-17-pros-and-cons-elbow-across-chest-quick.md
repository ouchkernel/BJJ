# Elbow Across Chest — Quick Reference

## The move

From front triangle, as you lock up: take the opponent's trapped elbow and move it across your own chest, then strangle from there.

## Pros

- Stronger body structure
- Head and elbow end up closer together (good for mechanics)

## Cons

- A lot of the most effective triangle **counters** happen exactly because the elbow got brought across the chest
- Most of the time, there's actually an advantage to leaving the arm where it is

## Bottom line

Situational, not a default. Most of the time: don't move it across. Know the specific circumstances where it helps before you use it.
