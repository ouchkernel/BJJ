# Magic Of 2-On-1 Gripping — Quick Reference

## Why it works

Two of your arms locked onto one of theirs = built-in strength advantage, regardless of size. You only need one arm + the head for a triangle — the other arm doesn't matter.

## Pick the grip by what they give you

| Opponent does this | Use this grip |
|---|---|
| Hands low, normal hand-fight | Pronated elbow two-on-one (default) |
| You want speed | Supinated two-on-one — secure elbow, hug no-thumb, block their push with your chest |
| They collar-tie you | Counter: lock their elbow one hand, chop over the bicep with the other — their own grip attempt gets trapped |
| Hands high / dominating your shoulders | Two-on-one wrist grip |

## Every variation funnels into the same follow-up

1. Foot on the hip.
2. Create an unweighted leg.
3. Pull opponent in, lock the trap triangle.
4. Turn the corner, knee points at centerline/crotch.
