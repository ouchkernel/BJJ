# Rear Triangle From Mounted Position — Quick Reference

## The paradox

Mount = face-to-face, but you can still get a *rear* triangle. The entry rotates around the opponent's arm.

## Do this, in order

1. Inside position on opponent's hands.
2. Walk one hand through, secure at the head, other hand under their arm working elbow-to-elbow.
3. **Keep your own elbow off the mat** — if it touches down and they clamp it, they bridge and roll you.
4. Walk your arm out (direction your fingers point), cycling **walk → straighten → walk → straighten**.
5. Goal: get opponent's elbow **above their own shoulder line**. That's the whole battle.
6. Once above the line: hips forward, hug, head outside their elbow, chin to bicep, trap the arm across the body.
7. Hug over the head, one elbow out, knee to your own elbow → capture their wrist.
8. Extract foot → two unweighted feet → one foot into armpit = **one in, one out**.
9. Elbow tight to chest, wrist-to-wrist overhead, unified legs → trap triangle → hand-assist → figure-four → strangle.

## The one thing to watch

Your elbow. Off the mat, always, until the fight for their elbow-above-the-line is won.
