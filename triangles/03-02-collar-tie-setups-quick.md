# Collar Tie Setups — Quick Reference

## Why it works

Collar tie = direct head control. All posture defense starts at the head, so this shuts it down early.

## Do this, in order

1. Hand-fight → catch wrist → secure the **elbow** (underhand grip, not overhand).
2. Second hand → collar tie.
3. Foot on the **same side as your elbow control** — wrong side = Ashi Garami counter risk.
4. Foot to hip, then inside the bicep.
5. Around the corner, kick through, trap triangle.
6. Control head, grip leg, turn so both feet point forward.
7. Figure-four on top of the head/neck.

## Grip check

Underhand on the elbow, not overhand — matters for this specific entry.
