# Triangle Vs Single Leg 5 — Slouch Entry — Quick Reference

## Entry

1. Opponent deep on single leg, head inside.
2. Hook in and **slouch down** (instead of falling to rear corner).
3. Grab your own foot, pull it high into their armpit.
4. Lock figure-four — adjust until it's right.
5. Return to waist grip, apply strangle.

## Key detail

Heel **high in the armpit**, not low on the tricep — low = weak strangle.

## Risk management

- [ ] Don't let your foot dangle loose mid-adjustment — gap lets them finish a double leg.
- [ ] Base on your hand if possible, elbow at minimum, during the entry.
- [ ] Fallback if the strangle alone doesn't finish: keep tight waist, grab their foot, turn them onto their back — same yoko sankaku, finish from top instead.
