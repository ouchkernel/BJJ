# Trap → Figure-4 Transition — Quick Reference

## The two definitions

- **Trap triangle** = ankles crossed, control only. No strangle yet.
- **Figure-four triangle** = shoelaces locked behind your own knee-hollow. This is what actually strangles.

## Why this matters

Described as **the single biggest determinant of whether your triangles succeed or fail** — not a footnote. Causes frustration across all 5 triangle types, not just front triangle. Worth dedicated drilling time.

## The reality of it

- Trap triangle = control only, not a finish.
- The instant it lands, the opponent feels the danger and starts resisting the transition to figure-four.
- Train this transition **against resistance** — it's never automatic.

## Demo setup to practice against

Partner on two knees → enter any generic trap triangle → work the transition to figure-four while they actively defend.

## Universal pattern

Same crossed-ankles → transition → figure-four sequence shows up in **every** triangle variant (front, side/Yoko Sankaku, etc.) — not position-specific.
