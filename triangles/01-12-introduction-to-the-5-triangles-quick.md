# Introduction To The 5 Triangles — Quick Reference

## The 5, by name

| # | Name (Japanese) | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Front — **Amote Senkaku** | The one everyone knows. Most finishes of any triangle. |
| 2 | Rear — **Ushiro Senkaku** | From behind the opponent; swap-in when arm strangles stall. |
| 3 | Opposite-side — **Hantai Senkaku** | "Wrong-way" lock; backs up a front triangle stopped by posture. |
| 4 | Side — **Yoko Senkaku** | Common in judo, underused in jiu-jitsu. |
| 5 | Reverse — **Gyaku Senkaku** | Least productive statistically, still a needed part of the system. |

## Categories (which ones you can chain without releasing)

- **A:** Front ↔ Rear ↔ Opposite-side
- **B:** Side ↔ Reverse

Senkaku = "triangle/three sides" in Japanese — you'll hear it in every variation name.
