# Pulling Down The Head — Quick Reference

## When to use it

Fallback, used specifically when:
- You can't get an angle on the opponent, AND
- Their shoulder is still trapped inside your triangle (shoulder-elimination hasn't happened)

## The one detail that decides everything

- [ ] Shoelaces (top of foot) tucked into the **pocket of your own knee**
- [ ] NOT resting on top of your own calf muscle

Looks tiny. Isn't. This is described as the actual difference between tapping someone and not. Make it a reflex — check it the instant you get hold of the opponent, not after the finish stalls.
