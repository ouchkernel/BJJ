# Solo Body Folding Drills — Quick Reference

## The point

Build the hollowed, folded-forward body shape almost every triangle needs. Never arched.

## Do these, in order

1. **Toe touch + walk** — touch toes over one shoulder, walk to the other side looking where you're going, knees stay close to shoulders.
2. **Straight-leg fold + lock** — hollow the body, legs straight over to touch floor, come up and lock a triangle shape both sides.
3. **Crucifix switch** — arms out crucifix, legs up and touch, slide across, switch direction, look each way.

## When

Pre-class warm-up, same as the pummeling drills.
