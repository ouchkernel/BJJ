# Entries Into Rear Triangle Back Position — Quick Reference

All 3 entries start with a **tight waist** on a turtled opponent. All 3 end: unified legs → trap triangle → hand-assist → figure-four → strangle.

## Entry 1 — Snatch the hand

1. Tight waist, knee props opponent's knee, break down to a hip.
2. Snatch near hand (all 4 knuckles), second hand posts on wrist.
3. Knee behind tricep locks the arm.
4. Second leg up, sit back, hand across head, feed wrist to chest.
5. Trap triangle → hand-assist → figure-four → strangle.

## Entry 2 — Off-balance to a hip

1. Tight waist, wedge knee, pull opponent off-balance sideways.
2. Take knee out — they fall to a hip, you stay over top.
3. Head over their head, snatch hand, second hand pins wrist.
4. Knee behind elbow, foot kicks in, second leg up, sit down.
5. Wrist-to-wrist, draw arm back, turn out → trap triangle → figure-four.

## Entry 3 — Full break-down

1. Tight waist, knees in, break opponent flat to the mat.
2. Secure both hands.
3. Knee behind tricep, leg hooks the arm.
4. Sit back, arm over head, wrist-to-wrist.
5. Trap triangle → figure-four → strangle.

## The one rule across all three

Secure the far arm/wrist **before** you sit back. Everything else is just how you get there.
