# Top Position Passing Guard 2 — Quick Reference

## Grip

Collar tie + one wrist. This arm is a **decoy** — you're triangling the OTHER arm.

## Do this

1. Secure collar tie + wrist (top or inside grip, either works).
2. Push the gripped wrist down.
3. Jump **front-on** (not diagonal) — step outside foot, go straight over the top like jumping guard.
4. Cross ankles = trap triangle.
5. Lift trapped hand out, punch through, control head.
6. Opponent postures up → turn corner, wedge around head/shoulders, lock figure-four.

## Mental cue

Don't think "lock a triangle." Think: jumping guard on a seated opponent, except the guard locks around his **head + one arm**, not his waist.

## Free timing cue

Opponents instinctively yank a gripped elbow back to their own hip. Let them do it, then jump — it sets the entry up for you.

## vs. Passing Guard entry #1

| | Entry 1 (over-tie) | Entry 2 (this one) |
|---|---|---|
| Grip | Over-tie | Collar tie + wrist |
| Jump angle | Diagonal | Front-on |
| Foot position | Outside | Outside |

Both work well — different look, same underlying jump-into-trap-triangle idea.
