# Underhooks To Triangle — Quick Reference

## Correct underhook mechanics (do this or it gets stripped)

- [ ] Hand cups the **front deltoid** (not a superficial rear-deltoid grip).
- [ ] Elbow **high**, forearm parallel to the mat.

## Entry 1 — Near-side, flattened on your back

1. Forward shift up to your **elbow** (not shoulder — you need the mobility).
2. Foot on their thigh or floor outside → frees your hip.
3. Hip moves → pull into trap triangle, punch underhook hand through for head control.
4. Wedge in, figure-four.

**Their stacking counter:** get angle *before* they stack — makes stacking useless. If they stack anyway while naive, push them straight over.

## Entry 2 — Far-side (deeper, kills the whizzer)

1. Clear their elbow, reach across to the **far side** of their neck — controls head + arm together.
2. Come up to your knees — this grip shape neutralizes their whizzer almost entirely.
3. Knee to hip, foot to knee, leg over their lower back → jump straight into trap triangle.
4. Arm around for head control, quick turn, figure-four.
