# Eliminating The Shoulder — Quick Reference

## The rule

Physically remove the opponent's shoulder from inside your locked legs. Only the head + one arm should be inside the triangle — never the shoulder.

## How

1. Form angle so your knee goes directly in front of the opponent's deltoid.
2. Check: from an outside view, the shoulder should be invisible.
3. If you can see the shoulder, the triangle is bad — reset the angle before locking.

## Why it matters

- Triangle gap is narrow by design (barely wider than your forearm) — a shoulder doesn't fit without needing much longer legs.
- Removing the shoulder lets short legs lock a tight figure-four just as well as long ones.

## Two rules so far (stack them)

1. Strangle leg above the shoulder line.
2. Shoulder eliminated from inside the triangle.
