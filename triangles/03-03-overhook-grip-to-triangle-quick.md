# Over Hook Grip To Triangle — Quick Reference

## Learn triangles seated first

Easier to generate movement (yours and theirs) from seated than from flat on your back — the usual beginner teaching position.

## The overhook tradeoff

Holds opponent close (kills their posture defense) but makes it harder to get your foot over. Fix: use their own sweep-recovery instinct.

## Do this, in order

1. Draw forward into a collar tie, lock the overhook (thumb near your own pec).
2. Secure their wrist, elbow to your hip, figure-four grip on your own wrist.
3. Tilt and **Sumi Gaeshi** (hook sweep) them to their back.
4. Almost nobody stays down — they'll fight back up. Let them.
5. Step over, keep the same grip as they climb back to their knees.
6. They climb straight into a triangle setup.
7. Cover the head, scoop grip, turn corner, both feet forward.
8. Figure-four, knee to groin, strangle.

## Key insight

The grip never changes through the whole sweep-recovery cycle — only your legs move as they climb back up.
