# Entering Reverse Triangle (Open Guard) — Quick Reference

## Starting position

- Open guard, one foot across opponent's stomach.
- Both hands gripping their triceps.
- One foot already in inside position.

## Do this, in order

1. Extend outside leg to create space.
2. Pommel that foot around their head to the other side.
3. Extend other leg to get underneath them.
4. Pass a hand under their ribcage, turn around the corner to face them.
5. **Checkpoint: knee underneath their ear.**
6. Bring everything down to the shoulder joint, lock figure-four.
7. Apply **reverse tight waist grip** with your free hand.
8. Finish.

## Why the reverse tight waist matters

Without it, opponent bench-presses or pulls away. With it, they can't move — that's what makes the strangle "ungodly tight."

## Second detail that matters

Bring your quadriceps/knee all the way down to their **tricep** — this is what fully eliminates their shoulder from the lock. Don't stop short.

## Sanity check

Legs should point the **same direction** as your opponent — makes it much harder for them to yank free while you're turning underneath them.
