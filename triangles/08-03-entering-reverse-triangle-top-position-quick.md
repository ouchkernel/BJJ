# Entering Reverse Triangle 2 (Top Position) — Quick Reference

## Build the side crucifix

1. Top side control, sit through, pull their elbow up (partial sit-through).
2. They retract their arm and keep it in → hip forward, push their wrist down/forward.
3. Step over their arm.
4. **Scissor your legs — do NOT pinch.**

## Why scissor, not pinch

- Pinch = no real control, they limp the arm out and keep their head flat on the floor. Escape.
- Scissor (one knee forward, one back) = lifts their shoulder, forces their head to rise. No escape.

## Enter the triangle

1. Bent-wrist hand over their head.
2. Come forward/up, lift.
3. Leg over their head as a wedge underneath.
4. Post other hand on floor.
5. Drive legs through, start locking.
6. Widen base (hands out) for stability.

## The trick that matters

**Reach across and catch their far leg, feed it to your arm.**
- Secures your base.
- Stops them bridging (a free leg on the floor = bridge threat).
- Shuts down shrimping/turning too.

## Finish

Look toward their legs → both your legs rise → strangle locks on. "Enormously tight" from top.
