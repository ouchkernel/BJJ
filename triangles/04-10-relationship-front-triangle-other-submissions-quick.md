# Front Triangle & Other Submissions (Armlock) — Quick Reference

## Core idea

Threaten a different submission → opponent's own defense hands you the triangle.

## Basic method

1. Standard Juji Gatame entry from closed guard (wrist, elbow, foot on hip, come to the side).
2. He yanks the arm free → let it go, leg around the other side, turn across → triangle.

Well known — experienced opponents already defend it.

## Advanced: two-stage method

1. Postural control first.
2. Draw elbow across centerline, throw legs into a **top lock** (feet over his shoulders, not around his lower back). Higher lock = harder to posture away.
3. Now attack the armlock a second time — he's forced into a defensive arm configuration.
4. He pulls his elbow to defend → that pull **hands you a trap triangle** on the opposite side.
5. Figure-four, finish.

## Key upgrade over the basic method

Top lock (feet over shoulders) denies posture — this is what forces the real two-stage sequence and makes the triangle "free" when he defends.
