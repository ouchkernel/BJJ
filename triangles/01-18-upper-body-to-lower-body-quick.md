# Upper Body To Lower Body — Quick Reference

## The one idea

Triangle = strangle attempt AND upper-body pressure generator. If the strangle stalls, the pressure you already created opens the legs.

## Build this reflex

Triangle attempt failing → go directly to the opponent's lower body. Don't just reset the position.

## Setup shown

Opponent on two knees → convert triangle pressure into a leg entry (leads into leg-attack/X-guard-style material in later volumes).
