# Rear Triangle From Side Positions — Quick Reference

## Setup

Across-side pin, opponent's arms in standard defense.

## Do this, in order

1. Hand posts at opponent's hip (blocks hip movement).
2. Second arm over the opponent, move to north-south.
3. Work wrist under their elbow — thumb back, hand through, angle change if needed. There's always *some* gap.
4. Past the forearm → hand under neck → figure-four grip on their arm (wrist to your own wrist).
5. Knee under their shoulder, sit down.
6. Foot clears their legs, hook one foot in, extract the other → **two unweighted legs**.
7. Ankle into armpit (even if they grip your foot — it's legs vs. hands, you win).
8. Lean forward, wrist-to-wrist on the other side.
9. Knuckles to chest, unified legs → trap triangle → hand-assist → figure-four → strangle.

## If they push hard on your knee

Doesn't matter — strangle still lands. If truly overpowering: reach through with the second hand, bring both arms overhead, strangle from there.

## Mindset

Getting the wrist under the elbow is a puzzle, not a strength fight.
