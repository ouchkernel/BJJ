# Rear Triangle → Opposite Triangle — Quick Reference

## The idea

Stalled rear triangle can convert to the **opposite triangle (Hantai Sankaku)** too, not just the front triangle. Which one you go to depends on which way the opponent moves.

## Sequence 1 — Stalled strangle

1. Turn out, move in — opposite direction from the front-triangle conversion.
2. Get the head, scoop the leg, turn the corner, both feet forward.
3. One-in-one-out, shoulder eliminated, wedge the head/shoulders (blocks posture-up).
4. Figure-four, strangle.

## Sequence 2 — Opponent grabs your foot

1. Foot over the top, clear the legs — opponent forced up (staying = arm lock danger).
2. Catch the hand, scoop in, opposite direction from front triangle.
3. Initial 90° angle, but the **real** angle comes from a pendulum swing of your leg.
4. Wedges tight, hand-assist to figure-four, quick finish.

## Rule

Category A triangles back each other up — read which way the opponent moves, then go front triangle or opposite triangle accordingly.
