# Your Body And The Triangle Strangle — Quick Reference

## Why triangles feel hard

Hands get lifelong fine-motor training (typing, instruments). Feet mostly just walk/run. Triangle = a stranglehold done with the less-coordinated half of your body.

## Self-check: the 4 minimum requirements

- [ ] **Foot dexterity** — feet can move to a precise spot with hand-like coordination
- [ ] **Knee flare** — sit one foot in front of other, both knees to floor (no hands) + elbows to mat, same time
- [ ] **Knees to chest** — knees comfortably up toward your own armpits
- [ ] **Shoulder-stand fold** — up onto points of shoulders, lower back off ground, move side to side

Most people pass all 4 right now, no warm-up needed.

## The real inhibitor

Not leg length — **waist size**. Can't change leg length. Can change waist size. If your stomach blocks the elbows-to-mat check, that's the fix.
