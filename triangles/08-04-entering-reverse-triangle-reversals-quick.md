# Entering Reverse Triangle 3 (Reversals) — Quick Reference

## The axiom

Once the triangle is **set**, top/bottom position doesn't matter. True for every triangle in the system. (Losing top position *before* it's set is still dangerous — this only applies after lock-in.)

## Why this triangle specifically can get you rolled

Your body lines up **with** the opponent's here (unlike other triangles where you're perpendicular). Lined-up = easiest orientation to get rolled. Expect it to happen sometimes.

## If you get reversed mid-entry

1. Punch hand through → **tight waist grip**.
2. Check knee-to-ear — it's probably flipped to **above** the ear now (bad).
3. Add a shoulder grip too (now: shoulder + waist).
4. Don't panic about unlocking the triangle briefly — your grips already stop them posturing.
5. **Shift your hips, drop the knee from above the ear to below it.** This is the whole fix.
6. Leg around the tricep to re-eliminate the shoulder.
7. Re-lock figure-four.
8. Strangle — just as tight from bottom as it was from top.

## One-line summary

Getting reversed here isn't a disaster — it's a known part of the position. Fix = shift hips, drop knee below ear, relock.
