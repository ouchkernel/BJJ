# Turning Off The Shoulder — Quick Reference

## Do this

1. Crossed feet in front of kneeling partner (trap triangle, not locked yet).
2. Calf muscle over partner's shoulder.
3. Hips go offline; lower legs turn to point the same direction.
4. Figure-four locks.

## Why hands-free first

Builds the ability to draw an opponent into a triangle with body mechanics alone. Hands/arms get added to this same movement later — but nail the no-hands version first.

## This chapter feeds directly into

The "trap triangle → figure-four" transition drilled later in the course.
