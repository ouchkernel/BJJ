# Trap → Figure Four: Posture Control — Quick Reference

## The problem

9/10 lost triangles are lost during the trap→figure-four transition, not before or after. Opponent's first move: posture up, push head away, pop feet apart.

## Don't

Grab two hands on the head too early. Traps you directly in front of opponent → they can stack you, you can't turn to angle.

## Do — pick one

1. **One hand on back of head, one hand scoops inside the leg.** Head control stops posturing; scoop hand sets up the turn.
2. **Hold your own shin.** Forms a locked wedge around both sides of the head. Also stops posturing, also allows the turn.

## Result

Either grip → opponent can't posture away → you get the angle → transition to figure-four completes.
