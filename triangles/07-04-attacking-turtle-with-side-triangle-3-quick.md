# Attacking Turtle With Side Triangle 3 — Quick Reference

## The idea

Seated Yoko Sankaku not finishing? Convert it to a pin, then relock a tighter triangle from there.

## Do this, in order

1. Seated Yoko Sankaku not landing (opponent resisting, elbow pulled back)?
2. Tight waist grip, grab their ankle.
3. Pull your head around toward their hamstring, adjust body position — pulls them on top of you, sends them across your body.
4. Now they're pinned underneath you.
5. Lock kimura on the near arm.
6. Belly-down shift, knee in → relock the triangle. Now it's ungodly tight.
7. Squeeze as they try to move.

## Payoff

Seated + supine versions together let you cycle the opponent: strangle → pin → strangle again, tighter each time.
