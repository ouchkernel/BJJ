# Overview — Quick Reference

## The 4-step system

1. Create one-in-one-out (one arm trapped inside your legs, one kept outside).
2. Shoot into a trap triangle (ankles crossed, head + arm controlled — not strangling yet).
3. Manage the transition to figure-four (this is where the actual strangle happens).
4. No strangle? Fall back to a joint lock (juji gatame/kimura) or, if opponent stands, transition to Ashi Garami leg attacks.

## The 5 triangles

- **Category A** (interchangeable with each other): Front (Amote) → Rear (Ushiro) → Opposite-side (Hantai)
- **Category B** (interchangeable with each other): Side (Yoko) → Reverse (Gyaku)
- Can't cross categories mid-attempt — leg configuration won't allow it.
- Front triangle = king, most finishes by far. Don't neglect the other 4 though.

## The 3 problems every triangle has to solve

1. **Circumference** (fitting head+shoulder+arm inside your legs) → eliminate the shoulder from the lock, keep only neck+arm.
2. **Posture** (opponent posturing out of trap triangle) → knee pull / head pull / shin pull methods.
3. **Stacking** (opponent stacking or lifting you) → scoop grip inside the leg, work to an angle off to the side.

## Why strangle > joint lock (as default)

Opponent can fight through joint damage. Nobody fights through going unconscious.
