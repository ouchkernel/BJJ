# Triangle Vs Single Leg 4 — Side Triangle — Quick Reference

## Entry

1. Control head, free leg off the mat.
2. Come up, create space.
3. Hook foot into their armpit.
4. Tight waist, post hand on floor.
5. Fall to the **rear corner** — not straight across their body.

## Control & finish

1. Kimura lock on the near-side arm = your anchor/security.
2. Thigh under their head, come up, go belly-down, extend leg toward far arm.
3. Return to Kimura whenever unsure.
4. Available finishes: strangle, arm lock, wrist lock (both arms).

## Key detail

Heel goes **deep in the armpit**. Pop up first, then return to the strangle for a fast, tight finish.
