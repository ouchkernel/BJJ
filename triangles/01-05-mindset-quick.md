# Mindset — Quick Reference

## The one idea

You can't take an action you don't first see the opportunity for. Vision before execution.

## Do this

1. Actively look for the **one-in-one-out** pattern (one of opponent's arms trapped inside your legs, one kept outside) while rolling — don't wait for it to appear, hunt for it.
2. Remember: every triangle, all 5 types, is the same underlying story — one arm in, one arm out. One mental image covers all of them.
3. More recognition = more attempts. The physical skill only gets used if you see the chance first.
