# Overtie Series — Quick Reference

## What it is

Reach over and around the opponent's collar tie, grip your own collar tie outside theirs, same side. You get outside position + head control; they get inside position.

## Do this, in order

1. Opponent collar-ties you. Play wrists/tricep first.
2. Sneak the overtie on — doesn't feel threatening to them yet.
3. Second hand controls their **elbow**.
4. Foot on the **same side as the trapped arm** — never the opposite side (invites a leg-lock counter).
5. They try to yank the arm — can't, head and elbow both controlled.
6. Foot in (unweighted), fall around the corner.
7. Hand inside their legs, invert/turn.
8. Figure-four directly over their head → strangle.

## Reality check

Expect real hand-fighting first (wrist ties, arm drags, re-gripping). The overtie is one tool in that scramble, not a clean first-attempt grab.
