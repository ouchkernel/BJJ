# Tight Waist Vs. Whizzer — Quick Reference

## Core mechanism (same as every triangle entry)

Weightless hips → easy lateral hip movement → legs thrown into triangle.

## Do this

1. From bottom, frame a hand across, knee shield toward underhook.
2. Underhook target: **far trapezius** — NOT the usual far hip / near shoulder (those collapse under a whizzer).
3. Come up to elbow, feet at his knee, hand up next to him.
4. Foot over his lower back → weightless hips.
5. Lock feet (underhook arm can stay a bit awkward at first).
6. Lift underhook arm free, go back to controlling his head.
7. He pulls away → hook inside legs, knee wedge behind head, ankle+knee tight.
8. Harder he pulls, tighter the finish.

## Half guard variant

1. Bottom half guard, frame across.
2. Come up, step over his far hip.
3. Arm through, punch over top, hold head.
4. Turn corner, strangle.

## Why far trapezius

Far hip / near shoulder underhooks get flattened by the opponent's whizzer. Far trapezius doesn't.
