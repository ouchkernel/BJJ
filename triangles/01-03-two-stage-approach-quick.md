# The 2-Stage Approach — Quick Reference

## The 2 phases

1. **Trap triangle** — ankles crossed, head+arm controlled. Control only, no strangle. This is where you almost always start.
2. **Figure-four triangle** — legs fully locked. This is where the strangle actually happens.

## The one thing that matters most

Managing the transition from trap → figure-four **under resistance** is the biggest factor in whether your triangle finishes. The opponent feels the trap triangle land and immediately starts fighting the transition — that fight is the real skill test, not either phase alone.

## Mental model

Don't drill "the triangle" as one move. Drill entry (trap) and drill the transition (trap→figure-four) as two separate skills.
