# Mechanics Of Rear Triangle — Quick Reference

## Do this, in order

1. Hollow of knee on the **carotid artery** (strangle leg).
2. Hips **north of opponent's shoulder line**.
3. Bring in support leg → lock a **trap triangle** (feet crossed, not full figure-four yet).
4. Hand-assist: pull heel, drag knee up toward the **armpit** (not low on the body — kills your power).
5. Wedge foot behind opponent's **lat muscle**.
6. Keep opponent's arm **extended**, elbow over their own head — locked/bent arm lets them push your knee.
7. Support knee → strangle knee, press inward, squeeze to finish.

## Two roles

- Strangle leg = carotid contact.
- Support leg = completes the lock.

## Non-negotiable rule

Legs stay **unified** — both lower legs pointing the same direction, always. Applies to every triangle in the system.

## Fix if it's loose

Knee too low on the body → bring it up toward the armpit. Arm bent/locked → straighten it, elbow over their own head.
