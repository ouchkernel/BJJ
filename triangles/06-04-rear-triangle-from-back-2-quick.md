# Rear Triangle From Back 2 — Quick Reference

## The idea

Back crucifix is a bridge, not a dead end. Threaten the trapped arm → opponent bridges to protect it → that bridge delivers the rear triangle entry.

## Sequence 1 — Sweep to crucifix

1. Tight waist, secure hand, post, knee around corner, sweep opponent's leg.
2. Sit into back crucifix.
3. Pressure the trapped arm → they bridge.
4. Sweep knee behind them, tilt to the mat.
5. Move back, foot into armpit.
6. Unified legs → trap triangle → hand-assist → figure-four → finish.

## Sequence 2 — Strangle/armlock dilemma

1. Secure hand, lock, around the corner to crucifix.
2. Opponent bridges to avoid armlock pain either way.
3. Knee around corner into attacking position.
4. If they slide down to block the strangle → come up to elbow, shift, sit up.
5. Foot in armpit, wrist-to-wrist, unified legs → trap → figure-four → strangle.

## Remember

Don't force a choice between strangle and armlock from the crucifix — either threat gets the same bridge, same entry.
