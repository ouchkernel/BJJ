# Reactive Entries To Triangle — Quick Reference

## Universal skeleton (all 4 entries below)

1. Pommel a leg in → one-in-one-out.
2. Lock trap triangle.
3. Opponent pulls/postures away → pull IN with your knees (don't fight it, use it).
4. Turn the corner, control the head.
5. Lock figure-four, strangle.

## Entry 1 — Double scoop, hands locked

1. Hips up, knees straight up (body in a line).
2. Thumb-post both wrists.
3. Shimmy legs out straight.
4. Knee inside wrist, point knee out — pommels opponent in.
5. Climb to armpit, cut behind neck, trap triangle.

## Entry 2 — Both hips controlled, hands unlocked

1. Body up, double thumb-post.
2. Shimmy, knee inside elbow.
3. Hips climb, leg over top, anchor to opponent's back, lock feet.
4. Pull in as he pulls away, swim to the corner, finish.

## Entry 3 — Opponent underneath both legs

1. Form an angle — never stay square (stacking risk).
2. Hips off mat, pommel leg through, secure arm = one-in-one-out.
3. Trap triangle, same finish sequence.

## Entry 4 — Over-under pass

1. Hold far wrist, tilt opponent down using knee as lever.
2. Second leg to floor, control elbow, position tilts, leg frees.
3. (Alt) Tilt directly extracts the foot — cross ankles, same finish.

## Don't

Stay lined up square to an opponent underneath your legs — that's how you get stacked.
