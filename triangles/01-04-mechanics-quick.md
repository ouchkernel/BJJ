# Mechanics — Quick Reference

## The claim

Almost every common triangle failure = a mechanics problem, not a body-type problem.

## Do this

- If your triangle keeps "running away" right at the finish → look for a mechanical error, not a flexibility excuse.
- Don't buy the "long legs only" myth — short-legged athletes can build a real triangle with correct mechanics.
- Body type still matters (some), but it's not the primary driver most people assume it is.

## Why this chapter exists

Justifies why the rest of the course drills mechanical detail (grip depth, angle, exact foot/knee placement) so heavily — it's the actual lever you control.
