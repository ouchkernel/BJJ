# Rear Triangle From Side Positions 2 — Quick Reference

## When to use this

Basic "dig under the elbow" entry (see side-positions chapter 1) isn't working — opponent's arms are locked in too tight.

## Do this instead: wrist first, not elbow first

1. Underhook/supinated grip, dig under the **wrist**, punch hand in, secure it immediately.
2. Now they can't yank the arm away — it's stuck.
3. Go over the top, lock hand over their forearm.

## Add pressure

1. Shoulder to their opposite hip (sprawl action) — knees off mat, legs apart, shoulder turns down.
2. Foot-to-foot, turn corner, drive toward far shoulder.
3. Grab their wrist, grab your own wrist, sit into the lock.
4. Foot through, second leg out → two unweighted legs.
5. Foot into armpit → trap triangle → hand-assist → finish.

## The rule behind every triangle

**One arm outside your legs, one arm inside.** No exceptions. No "one in one out" = no triangle. Foot in the armpit = confirmation you have it.
