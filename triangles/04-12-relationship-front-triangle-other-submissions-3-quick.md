# Front Triangle & Other Submissions 3: Ude Gatame — Quick Reference

## Why use Ude Gatame here

Not the tightest hold — but it scares opponents into overreacting. The overreaction is what you're actually after.

## Setup

1. Underhook (e.g. via collar tie pulled through).
2. Clamp variant: knee front of shoulder, knee behind, extended underhook arm.
3. Attack Ude Gatame — hand on own elbow, hips away, head toward him.

## When he escapes (head up, elbow rotates in)

1. Losing the hold → extend leg across his shoulders, foot inside far shoulder.
2. His own escape (head coming toward you) sets up **foot penetration**.
3. Foot comes back, catch over the top → trap triangle.
4. Cover head, scoop legs, feet forward, knee wedge behind head, figure-four.

## Alternate setup: under-the-arm clamp

1. Underhook, pull opponent down into an under-the-arm clamp (not over).
2. Attack Ude Gatame. Feel it fail → knee+ankle together on far shoulder.
3. Extract, foot penetrates over his neck → trap triangle.
4. Control posture, scoop leg, figure-four.

## Key idea

It's a **head-position battle**. His head coming toward you to escape is exactly what opens the foot penetration into the triangle.
