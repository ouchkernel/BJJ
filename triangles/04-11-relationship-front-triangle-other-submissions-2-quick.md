# Front Triangle & Other Submissions 2: Juji Gatame From Pin — Quick Reference

## Do this

1. Opponent flat, arm locked defensively — hand on your own thigh for stability.
2. **Misalign your legs** — one foot back to his ear, one up near the armpit. Never symmetrical (he'll explode you backward).
3. Elbow down, knee inverted (points at mat).
4. Inside-knee position against his bicep (from the misaligned legs).
5. Foot to knee, push a space, thread the other foot through.
6. He must re-lock hands or eat the armlock.
7. Both feet over his shoulder, pull tight.
8. Catch his leg, pull to bring his head up off the floor.
9. Swap cross-face leg for a knee wedge behind his neck.
10. Head forward, figure-four, strangle.

## Key cue

**Asymmetrical/misaligned legs** = your stability base for the whole sequence. Symmetrical legs = you get knocked backward if he explodes.
