# Entries Into Rear Triangle Back Position

## Overview

First set of live entries into Ushiro Sankaku, all starting from a **tight waist** on an opponent in turtle position. Three variations drilled, each ending the same way: unified legs → trap triangle → hand-assist → figure-four → strangle.

## Entry 1 — Tight Waist, Break Down, Snatch The Hand

1. Arm around the opponent's waist, lock a good **tight waist**.
2. Hand to the floor, knee props against the opponent's knee — breaks them down to a hip.
3. Snatch the opponent's near hand, gripping all four knuckles, lock in close.
4. Second hand posts on the opponent's wrist.
5. Knee goes in behind the opponent's tricep — locks up their arm.
6. Second leg up, begin sitting back toward the opponent.
7. Hand goes across the opponent's head, lift their hand, feed the wrist in, lock close to your own chest.
8. Sit back — unified legs (lower legs pointing the same direction) — lock a trap triangle.
9. Hand-assist to bring everything north, lock the figure-four.
10. Arm over the wrist, strangle.

## Entry 2 — Off-Balance To A Hip, Wrist-To-Wrist

1. Knee up, tight waist in place — same starting point.
2. Hand to the floor, knee wedges against the opponent's knee, move to the side and pull them toward losing their balance.
3. At the balance-loss point, take the knee out — opponent falls to a hip, you stay over them.
4. Head over their head, snatch their hand; second hand goes over the top and pins the wrist as a base of support.
5. Pop up, place knee behind their elbow, go around the corner, foot kicks in.
6. Second leg up, sit down to the floor.
7. Bring your forearm over, lift their hand, go **wrist-to-wrist** (veins of your wrist on the veins of theirs).
8. Two hands draw their arm back, turn your body out to the side — unified legs, trap triangle.
9. Adjust to figure-four — tight strangle.

## Entry 3 — Full Break Down, Both Hands Secured

1. Turtle position, tight waist, knees in close.
2. Break the opponent down to the mat, secure one hand then the other.
3. Knee penetrates behind their tricep, leg goes around the corner and hooks their arm.
4. Sit back, arm over the head, wrist-to-wrist.
5. Sit down — unified legs — trap triangle.
6. Hand-adjust to figure-four, lock up, strangle.

## Key Takeaways

- All three entries start from a **tight waist** on a turtled opponent and end the same way: unified legs → trap triangle → hand-assist → figure-four.
- The variation between entries is really about *how* you secure the far arm/wrist and break the opponent down — direct hand-snatch, off-balance-to-a-hip, or full break-down-to-the-mat.
- Securing the opponent's arm/wrist *before* sitting back is the common thread across every version.
