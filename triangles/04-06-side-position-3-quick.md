# Front Triangle Side Position 3 — Quick Reference

## Knee penetration (extended)

1. Trap near arm.
2. Punch hand through to dominate the bicep from inside.
3. Drive forward, knee to your own hand.
4. Elbow as a prop → turn corner, extract foot.
5. Knee-first, foot-second penetration.
6. Trap triangle → figure-four.

Good for slower athletes — less escape space than foot penetration.

## The #1 failure mode

Opponent turns INTO you → extracts elbow to safety inside your hip → turns back to face you → escapes.

## The fix

1. See him turning away → take inside thumb position on his wrist.
2. As he finishes the turn, push your forearm into his armpit (double up if needed).
3. That forearm blocks him from turning BACK to face you — even though he already freed the elbow.
4. Walk down hips, recapture elbow.
5. Foot next to his elbow, sit through, pass foot inside armpit → one-in-one-out again.

## Finish

Not the front triangle this time — lock **Ushiro Sankaku (rear triangle)**. Strangle comes on fast as he pulls away.

## Key insight

You can't stop the elbow extraction. You CAN stop him turning back to face you afterward. That's the whole fix.
