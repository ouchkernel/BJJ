# Rear Triangle: Strangle → Joint Locks — Quick Reference

## The 4-step system (every triangle, not just this one)

1. Working grip.
2. One-in-one-out → trap triangle.
3. Trap triangle → figure-four (the actual strangle).
4. Strangle stalls → joint locks (planned fallback, not improvisation).

## Joint-lock chain (starting point: stalled Ushiro Sankaku, arm extended)

1. **Armbar (basic)** — elbow over your hip, hand to your armpit. Grip half hand/half wrist. Pinky knuckle to chest.
2. **Armbar (stronger)** — V-grip wrist, arm behind your lat, grab own buttocks, come to an elbow.
3. Opponent bends arm to escape → **American lock** — head over their head, hold elbow, hand-over-hand, lat-assisted.
4. Opponent re-locks hands → **Kimura** — post over their head, hand outside your thigh, elbow to chest. Extra tight (their head can't move).
5. Opponent keeps arms in tight → **Wrist lock** — chop wrist into theirs, knuckles driven over the top, elbow-over-elbow. No figure-four needed for this one.

## Bottom line

1 entry → 6 finishes (strangle + 5 joint locks). Each opponent defense opens the next option in the chain.
