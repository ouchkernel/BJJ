# Attacking Turtle With Side Triangle — Quick Reference

## The dilemma

Kimura threat vs. turtled opponent → they can let the roll happen (kimura wins) or base out wide to stop it (side triangle wins). Either way you finish.

## Do this, in order

1. Secure hand, two-on-one grip.
2. Knee over their head, in and down — levers the arm out.
3. Thread hand through, set the kimura.
4. Opponent bases wide to stop the roll → switch.
5. Foot over the armpit, low squat, fall **straight back** (not sideways) over their head.
6. One-in-one-out achieved → belly down shift.
7. One leg extends, one retracts → kimura on the near arm or continue to the strangle.

## Remember

Straight back, not to the side — that's the detail that actually lands the triangle position.
