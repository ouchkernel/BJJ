# Rear Triangle ↔ Front Triangle — Quick Reference

## The system

5 triangles total. Category A = 3 (rear triangle included). Category B = 2. Triangles in the same category back each other up — crossing categories is rare and usually an opponent mistake, not something you engineer.

## When the rear triangle stalls

Opponent pushing your knee, or grabbed your foot before you locked up? Don't force it.

1. Move your head away from the opponent.
2. Take your free foot over the top, pass it over the trapped one to untangle it.
3. Opponent must turn **into** you now (turning away = arm lock danger).
4. Their turn-in delivers a front triangle setup — get the head, scoop the leg, lock trap triangle → figure-four.

## Mindset

A stalled triangle isn't a dead end — it's a cue to switch categories using the opponent's own escape movement.
