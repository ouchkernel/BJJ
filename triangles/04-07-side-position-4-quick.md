# Front Triangle Side Position 4 — Quick Reference

## The shin pin setup

1. Sit through as opponent pushes into your hips.
2. Head over his FAR shoulder.
3. Inside-wrist grip (thumb inside, wrist bent 90°), push down.
4. Body weight forward, knee forward to inside position.
5. Shin pins his bicep to the floor.

## Control both arms

- One arm: shin-pinned.
- Other arm: push out, underhook it.
- = one-in-one-out.

## Entry

1. Drive across his body, knee into armpit.
2. Fall across — knee behind his shoulder blocks him.
3. Pull foot back, pommel over the shoulder.

## The dilemma (this is the point of the whole move)

- Stays put → Udigatami (straight arm lock). "Suicide."
- Comes up to escape → straight into the triangle.

He has no good option.

## Finish

Clear the arm (either method), get angle + posture, lock figure-four.
