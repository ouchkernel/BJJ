# Front Triangle Mounted Position — Quick Reference

## Ratchet-walk elbow control

1. Underneath opponent's head/arm, base with arms.
2. Forearm/wrist under his elbow.
3. Walk it outward.
4. Hit resistance → straighten your arm → resume walking.
5. Never go backward. Repeat until elbow reaches crown of head.
6. Lock both arms around head + shoulder.

## Entry (method A — hand-over-hand)

1. Draw arm across centerline.
2. Chin around OUTSIDE his arm.
3. Knee to elbow, sit out to the side, hug head in.
4. His arms come in defensively → push his wrist down.
5. Step over, legs curl under = one-in-one-out.
6. Forehead to mat, figure-four.

## Entry (method B — high leg, same as side position)

1. Same arm control.
2. Catch wrist, push under his own neck.
3. Head over your own knee.
4. High-leg action, foot over his shoulder = one-in-one-out.
5. Head to floor, figure-four.

## Note

Once locked from mount, staying mounted or sliding to bottom makes no difference — same strangle either way.

## Universal reminder

Working grip → one-in-one-out → eliminate the shoulder → trap triangle → figure-four.
