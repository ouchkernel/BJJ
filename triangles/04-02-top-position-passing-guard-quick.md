# Top Position Passing Guard — Quick Reference

## Trigger

Standing over a seated opponent, hand-fighting → see a posted/extended arm.

## Do this

1. Go over the top of the extended arm, take an **over-tie**.
2. Step to the **outside** foot position (not your usual inside preference).
3. In one motion, take your leg directly over the opponent's arm.
4. Lock up, land on your **elbow** (not flat).
5. Opponent stays put → pass through to top. Opponent rolls up → you still have the triangle.

## Body angle

Fall across at a **diagonal**, not straight forward. Left over-tie + left foot outside step → right leg becomes the strangle leg, fall to your right side.

## Works especially well against

Opponents who like to play inside foot position — this entry flips the usual matchup.

## Don't

Crash straight forward onto the opponent — you lose the elbow-landed mobility that makes this entry safe.
